    Tickets

      How to run a raffle

      Add everyone who entered, choose how many prizes there are, and draw.

      1. Add the entries. One name per line. Each gets a ticket number in list order, so No. 001 is the first name.
      2. Choose the number of winners. Up to 10. Nobody can win twice in one draw.
      3. Draw. Winners are revealed one at a time, in prize order.
      4. Keep a record. "Copy winners" copies the results, ticket numbers included, to paste into a post or an email.

      Is it fair?

      Every ticket has the same chance. Winners are picked by your browser's secure random number generator, and the order of the list doesn't change anyone's odds. For a public draw, share the link first, so everyone can see the list of entries before you draw.

      Questions

      +Can I draw more than one winner?

      Yes, up to 10 at once, revealed in order: 1st, 2nd, 3rd and so on. Nobody can win twice in the same draw.

      +Is this good for giveaways?

      Yes. Paste in your entries, draw, then use "Copy winners" to post the result. Check your platform's own giveaway rules too.

      +What do the ticket numbers mean?

      They follow the list order: the first name is No. 001. They make it easy to announce winners without anyone needing to check spelling.

      +Do you keep the list of entries?

      No. It's saved in your own browser only. A share link carries the names inside the link itself.
