Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between warrior, shaman, rogue, chieftain, and berserker orc names?

Warrior names emphasize strength and combat prowess (Gromtar, Thrallok, Zugul). Shaman names reflect connection to the elements and spirits (Drogar, Volgrin, Lokgor). Rogue names suggest stealth and cunning (Rugth, Ulgash, Throk). Chieftain names command respect and authority (Ghash, Thrak, Boruk). Berserker names reflect fury and rage (Drogoth, Rhazgar, Brogna).

How do I choose between male and female orc names?

Male orc names typically end in harsh consonants (-k, -g, -th, -sh) that emphasize strength. Female orc names often incorporate similar sounds but may add softer endings (-a, -ra, -ka) while maintaining power. Both genders have names for all classes, so choose based on your character's gender.

Can I use these names for other Warcraft games?

Yes! While these names are specifically from World of Warcraft, the naming conventions are consistent across Warcraft games. These names will work well for orc characters in Warcraft III, Hearthstone, or any Warcraft setting.

What makes a World of Warcraft orc name authentic?
