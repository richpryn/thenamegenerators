How the Spell Name Generator Works

It works from 375 names and name parts, which combine into over 420,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • School of magic: Any school, Abjuration, Conjuration, Divination, Enchantment, Evocation, Illusion, Necromancy, Transmutation.
  • Name format:
    • Name only, e.g. Lantern Augury
    • With an effect, e.g. Mother Hollin's Cruel Hare: makes a rope grow twice as long

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

A good spell name tells you what it does and hints at who made it: Thornbind, Hollowmark Hex, Ember's Last Whisper. This spell name generator makes names for homebrew spells in D&D, stories and games, sorted by the eight schools of magic, with a short effect to go with each.
