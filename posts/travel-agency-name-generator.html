Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a travel agency name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as two similar names nearby confuse customers.
+What should an independent travel agent call their business?
Many home-based agents use their own name ("Travel with Sarah", "Sarah Rose Travel"), because clients book with a person they trust. If you specialise, say so: cruises, honeymoons, ski or family holidays. "Holidays" sounds British and "Vacations" American, so pick the one your clients use, or plain "Travel" for both. Keep well clear of big tour operators' and airlines' names.
