How pop punk names work

Pop punk took punk's speed and added big choruses. Green Day's Dookie (1994) and Blink-182 made it huge, and the Warped Tour carried it across America every summer from 1995 to 2019.

The names follow a few habits:

  • Numbers. Sum 41 named themselves after the day they formed, 41 days into the summer holidays. Here: Ollie 52, Detention 23.
  • Places: Albany Summer, Leeds Motel.
  • "The ... Years": The Gap Years, The Basement Years.
  • Two easy words: Easy Signals, Second Hearts.

Choose Stage name for members with nicknames, like Alex 'Ziggy' Yates, and Album title for records like Weekend Curfew or Last Summer Grudges.

Using a pop punk name

  • Make it fun to chant. Pop punk crowds sing back everything, including the band's name.
  • Numbers age. A year in your name dates you; a random number doesn't.
  • Search before you commit. Look the name up on Spotify, Bandcamp and YouTube, and check the social handles are free. Two acts with one name confuse fans and streaming services alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good pop punk band names?
Fun, nostalgic and easy to chant: The Bus Pass Years, Detention 23, Tulsa Heights or Easy Signals. A number or a hometown is a classic pop punk touch.
+Why do pop punk bands have numbers in their names?
It's a tradition from bands like Blink-182 and Sum 41, who named themselves after the day they formed. A number makes a plain name more memorable.
+Are these real pop punk bands?
No. They're made up in the style of the 2000s bands and the revival. Search Spotify and Bandcamp first, since the scene has a lot of bands.
