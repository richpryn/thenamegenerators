What makes a plumbing name work

The word plumber comes from the Latin plumbum, lead, because pipes were once made of it. Most firms still name themselves in one of a few plain ways:

  • Say the job. Customers search for "plumber near me" or "boiler repair", so a name with Plumbing, Heating or Drainage in it is easier to find and to trust.
  • Puns work on vans. Pipe Down or Kitchen Sink Drama gets a smile at the traffic lights. Keep them clean, and add a straight slogan so people know you're qualified.
  • Family and town names sound settled and local, which matters for a trade people let into their homes.
  • Gas work needs registration. In the UK, anyone working on gas appliances such as boilers must be on the Gas Safe Register, so only put gas or boiler work in your name if you're registered.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A plumbing name ends up on a van, a website, a Google Business Profile and a stack of invoices, so it's worth a few minutes' checking before any of that is printed:

  • Press Check on any name above. The Business Name Checker looks up the domain, links to the company register (Companies House in the UK) and opens the trade mark search and social handles.
  • Search your area. Look up the name on a map and in local search results. Two firms with the same name a few miles apart confuse customers and reviews.
  • If you do gas work, show your Gas Safe registration next to the name on your van and website, not instead of it.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, from shortlisting to registering, read our guide to Business Naming Best Practices.

Getting the name onto the van

  • Read it at a distance. A short name and a phone number are what people remember from a passing van.
  • Add a slogan if the name is a joke. Choose Name with a slogan for lines like Warm homes, clear drains.
  • Use the same name everywhere: van, invoices, Google Business Profile and social media.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good plumbing business names?
Names that say the job and are easy to read on a van: Plumb Perfect, Leak Geeks, Nash & Sons Plumbing Services or Heatwise Boiler Services. Add your town for local search.
+How do I check a plumbing business name is free?
Press Check next to any name to see if it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. Then make sure: in the UK, search Companies House and the UK IPO trade mark register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. A quick map search for the name in your area helps too.
+What's a good slogan for a plumbing business?
Promise speed, honesty and a tidy job: Fixed fast, fixed right or Day or night, we put it right. Keep jokes clean, because the slogan goes on your van. Choose Name with a slogan in the Format menu to see names paired with one.
+Can I put gas or boiler work in my business name?
Only if you're on the Gas Safe Register. In the UK anyone working on gas appliances such as boilers must be registered, so show your registration alongside the name.
