How garden businesses name themselves

Landscaping covers everything from planting borders to laying patios, so the name should hint at the kind of work you want more of.

  • Nature names (oak, fern, rowan, ivy, moss) suit planting, soft landscaping and wildlife gardens.
  • Design names borrow garden words: a parterre is a formal garden laid out in patterned beds, and a sundial or clipped box suggests a traditional, high-end garden.
  • Hard landscaping firms often put the work in the name: Paving, Patios, Driveways, Fencing.
  • Garden history has a famous example: the 18th-century landscape designer Lancelot Brown was nicknamed "Capability" because he told clients their grounds had "capability" for improvement.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Your van, your website and the boards you put up outside jobs all carry the name, and each costs money the moment it's printed, so check the name first. Press Check next to any name to open the Business Name Checker, which looks it up in one go:

  • The company register: Companies House in the UK, or your state's business register in the US. A limited company's name can't be the same as one already registered.
  • Trade marks: the UK IPO and USPTO registers. Registering a company doesn't stop someone else owning the name as a trade mark.
  • A domain and social handles, so customers can find you under the same name everywhere.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, read our guide, Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for a garden business

  • Photograph your work and post it under the same name everywhere: gardens sell themselves on Instagram.
  • Think seasonally. A name tied only to mowing or planting can limit you in winter; a broader name leaves room for fencing, clearance and design.
  • Keep it easy to spell, so people can find you after seeing the van once.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good landscaping business names?
Names that sound green and skilled: a plant pairing like Rowan & Wildflower Landscapes, a garden feature like Sundial Garden Design, a pun like Tree's Company Landscaping, or your town with what you do, like Hampton Paving & Landscapes.
+How do I check a landscaping business name is free?
Press Check next to any name to see if it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. Then make sure: in the UK, search Companies House and the UK IPO trade mark register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. A quick map search for the name in your area helps too.
+What's the difference between a landscaping and a lawn care business name?
Landscapers design and build gardens (planting, patios, borders), so their names lean on gardens, nature and design. Lawn care firms mow and treat turf, so their names are about lawns, mowing and stripes. If mowing is most of your work, try the Lawn Care Business Name Generator.
+What's a good name for a garden design business?
Something elegant that suggests a planned, traditional garden, like Sundial Garden Design or Clipped Box Landscapes. Choose Luxury garden design for more names in that style.
