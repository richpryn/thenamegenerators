A good moon name tells you what the moon is to the people below it: a mining claim like Kerrigan's Diggings, a lamp in a fantasy sky like The Owl Moon, or a goddess like The Hunting Mother. How real moons got their names

  • Our Moon is just "the Moon" in English, with a capital letter. The Romans called it Luna and the Greeks Selene, after the moon goddess.
  • Jupiter's four largest moons were discovered by Galileo in 1610 and named Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto, after figures loved by Zeus, the Greek Jupiter.
  • Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos, "fear" and "dread", named after the sons of Ares, the Greek god of war.
  • Uranus breaks the pattern: its moons are named after characters from Shakespeare and Alexander Pope, like Titania, Oberon, Miranda and Ariel.

The generator follows each habit. Sci-fi moons are named after claims, prospectors and surveys: Calloway's Claim, Jorvic IV-b. Fantasy moons get everyday names, and sometimes come in pairs: Tam and Wren. Mythic moons are goddesses and their belongings, and Poetic moons are lamps, mirrors and saucers. Moons in your world

  • Decide what the moon means. A farming world names its moon after harvests; a mining colony after money.
  • Two moons make a story. Twins, rivals or a big and a small one each give the calendar and the legends something to work with.
  • Let the name change. Settlers might call a moon one thing, and the survey office another.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good moon names?
Names that say what the moon is to the people below: Kerrigan's Diggings for a mining moon, The Owl Moon for a fantasy sky, The Hunting Mother for a moon goddess, or The Pale Saucer for something gentle.
+How are real moons named?
Mostly after figures from myth, like Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto at Jupiter, or Phobos and Deimos at Mars. Uranus is the exception: its moons are named after characters from Shakespeare and Alexander Pope.
+Can a planet have two moons with linked names?
Yes, and it often works well: twins, a pair of lovers or rivals. The Fantasy style sometimes gives paired names like Tam and Wren.
