A good cookie business name is fun, short and makes people hungry, whether you sell loaded cookies, boxes by post or hand-iced biscuits for parties. This cookie business name generator has names in seven styles, from puns and silly names to one-word brands and decorated-cookie studios, plus names built from your town or your own name.

  • Cookies Are My Cardio: funny and very Instagram.

  • Cut & Iced: for decorated biscuits, saying exactly what you do.
  • The Rosy Biscuit Co.: cute, and British in using biscuit.

How cookie business names work

  • Loaded cookie shops go big and playful: Gooey Gang Cookies, Stuffed Cookie Society.
  • Decorated cookie makers name the craft: royal icing (the smooth icing that sets hard), piping and sprinkles. Piping Bag Co., Royal Icing Cookies.
  • One-word brands such as Gooey or Stack Cookies look good on packaging, but single words are more likely to be taken, so check them carefully.
  • Cookie or biscuit? Use the word your customers search for: soft and chunky bakes are usually cookies in the UK, and decorated party ones are often iced biscuits.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A name from a generator is a starting point, not permission to use it. Before you order the sign, the boxes or the van livery:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; you can't register a limited company with the same name as an existing one. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK trade mark register (or the USPTO in the US) for the goods and services you'll sell.
  • Domain and handles. Check the .co.uk or .com and the Instagram and TikTok handles you'll need.
  • Your area. Search a map app for businesses with the same or a similar name nearby.

Press Check on any name, or use the Business Name Checker, to run most of these at once. For the full process, read our guide, Business Naming Best Practices. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

Selling food you've baked at home counts as a food business in the UK, so register with your local council at least 28 days before you start.

Making the name work for postal boxes

  • Fit it on the tape. Branded tape and stickers are the cheapest packaging, so a short name wins.
  • Think about TikTok. Cookie businesses sell on video, and a name people can say out loud is easier to share.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good cookie business names?
Short, fun names that make people hungry: puns like Dough Good, funny ones like Cookies Are My Cardio, or simple brands like Pistachio Cookie Lab. Decorated-cookie makers often name the craft, like Cut & Iced.
+How do I check a cookie business name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Before you commit, search the official registers too: in the UK, Companies House and the UK trade mark register; in the US, your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Look on a map app for businesses with the same name nearby.
+Should my business say cookies or biscuits?
In the US a cookie is any sweet biscuit, while in the UK cookie usually means the big, soft, chunky kind and biscuit the crisp kind you dunk in tea. Most British shops selling soft or loaded bakes still call them cookies, and decorated party ones are often iced biscuits. Use the word your customers would type into a search.
+What's a good name for a decorated cookie business?
Name the craft: icing, piping, sprinkles or a couture feel. Cut & Iced, Piping Bag Co. and The Sprinkle Cookie Company all tell customers you make decorated biscuits.
