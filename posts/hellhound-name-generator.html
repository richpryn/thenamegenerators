Hellhounds guard the gates of the underworld in myths from Greece to Britain, where black dogs with burning eyes haunt lonely roads. This hellhound name generator makes infernal names like Ashmaw, names from Greek myth, silly pet names, and, for three-headed dogs, a name for each head.

Cerberus and his family

The best-known hellhound is Cerberus, the dog who guarded the gates of the Greek underworld so the dead couldn't leave.

  • Three heads: later writers settled on three. The poet Hesiod, one of the first to describe him, gave him fifty.
  • Heracles dragged him up to the world of the living as the last of his twelve labours.
  • His brother Orthos, a two-headed dog, guarded the cattle of the giant Geryon.

The generator's "Three heads" format gives one name for each head, like Biscuit, Toffee and Pickles, which is handy for a three-headed dog costume or three dogs who go everywhere together.

Britain's black dogs

Britain has its own hellhounds: black dogs with burning eyes, seen on lonely roads and in churchyards.

  • Black Shuck of East Anglia is said to have burst into the churches at Bungay and Blythburgh in a storm in 1577. Scorch marks on Blythburgh's door are still called his claw marks.
  • The Barghest haunts Yorkshire, and Wales has the Gwyllgi, the "dog of darkness".
  • The Hound of the Baskervilles (1902), Arthur Conan Doyle's Sherlock Holmes story, drew on Dartmoor's black dog legends.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good names for a three-headed dog?
Choose the Three heads format for a name for each head, like Biscuit, Toffee and Pickles, or the Greek style for underworld names.
+How many heads did Cerberus have?
Usually three, though the early Greek poet Hesiod gave him fifty.
+What is Black Shuck?
A ghostly black dog of East Anglian folklore, said to have attacked two Suffolk churches during a storm in 1577.
