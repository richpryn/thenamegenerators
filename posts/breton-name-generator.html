How the Breton Name Generator Works

It works from 676 names and name parts, which combine into over 5,200,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Gender: Any, Male, Female.
  • Name format:
    • Any, e.g. Ermont Audemard
    • First name and surname, e.g. Bertin Bonfils
    • Noble, e.g. Lord Tugdual Delattre
    • Knight, e.g. Sir Romuald of Alcaire
    • First name only, e.g. Felicien
    • Surname only, e.g. Fauchard
  • Starts with: depends on the name format:
    • Any, First name and surname, Noble, Knight and First name only: Any letter, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, L, M, N, O, P, R, S, T, V, Y
    • Surname only: Any letter, A, B, C, D, F, G, L, M, P, R, S, T, V

Where the names come from: Some names are real and some are invented. First names are real French names, old and new, with some made-up ones in the same style. Surnames are French-sounding family names, some real and some new, with famous people's surnames left out. Every name is checked against the characters on the Elder Scrolls wiki, so none belongs to a Breton from Skyrim, Elder Scrolls Online or the older games.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Bretons have the most French-sounding names in Tamriel: in Skyrim you meet Sybille Stentor, the court wizard in Solitude, and Amaund Motierre, the man who hires the Dark Brotherhood to kill the Emperor. This Breton name generator gives you new names in that style, a first name and surname, a noble's Lord or Lady, or a knight's Sir or Dame, for Skyrim, Elder Scrolls Online or your own story. It's a fan-made, unofficial tool, not connected to Bethesda Softworks or ZeniMax, and it never gives you the name of a character from the games.

How Breton names work

Most Bretons in the later games have a first name and a surname, both French in flavour: Sybille Stentor and Amaund Motierre in Skyrim are typical. Plenty go by one name too, like Belethor, who runs the general goods shop in Whiterun, and Delphine of the Blades.

  • First names are often real French names or near them: old ones like Mahaut and Archambault sit next to modern ones.
  • Surnames sound like French family names, often built from a place or a thing: Boisclair, Noirmont, Mauvoisin.
  • Titles matter in High Rock. Elder Scrolls Online is full of Breton knights called Sir and Dame, and nobles called Lord and Lady.

It wasn't always this way. In Arena and Daggerfall, Breton surnames were English-sounding, built from parts like -ford and -smith. The French style came in with the later games, and it's the one this generator follows.

Eight good Breton names

  • Viviane Dumaine: a first name and surname that would sit happily in any Breton court.
  • Gatien Desrosiers: a plain, solid name for a merchant or a battlemage.
  • Lady Emerance Bellanger: a noblewoman with an old-fashioned first name.
  • Sir Evariste Noirmont: noir mont is French for 'black mountain', a good name for a grim knight.
  • Dame Noelle of Wayrest: a knight known by her city rather than her family.
  • Lord Archambault Mauvoisin: Mauvoisin is an old French surname meaning 'bad neighbour', which says a lot about the family.
  • Mahaut: the medieval French form of Matilda, for a Breton who needs only one name.
  • Boisclair: a surname meaning 'bright wood', for a family from the forests of High Rock.

Nobles, knights and the kingdoms of High Rock

High Rock, in the north-west of Tamriel, isn't one country but a patchwork of small kingdoms and cities: Daggerfall, Wayrest, Camlorn, Evermore, Shornhelm and more. In Elder Scrolls Online they're held together by High King Emeric of the Daggerfall Covenant. That's why so many Bretons carry a title:

  • Noble adds Lord or Lady. Pick it for a courtier, a baron's heir or a schemer.
  • Knight adds Sir or Dame, and sometimes names the knight by a city of High Rock instead of a family, as in Sir Aubin of Wayrest.
  • First name only suits a commoner, or a character like Delphine who would rather not give a surname.

Bretons are part human and part elf, and known for their magic. Give a Breton mage a noble title and you have a court wizard in the mould of Sybille Stentor.

Frequently Asked Questions

+Do Bretons have last names in Skyrim?
Most do, like Sybille Stentor and Amaund Motierre, but some go by one name, like Belethor and Delphine. Choose First name and surname for a full name or First name only for a single one.
+Are Breton names French?
They're French in style, and many first names are real French names. The surnames here are French-sounding family names, and none belongs to a character from the games.
+What are good female Breton names?
Set the gender to Female for names like Mahaut, Emerance and Viviane. Choose Noble or Knight for a Lady or a Dame.
+How do I make a Breton knight name?
Choose Knight. You get Sir or Dame with a surname, or a knight named by a city of High Rock, like Dame Noelle of Wayrest.
+Can I just get a Breton surname?
Yes: choose Surname only for a family name like Boisclair or Mauvoisin to go with a first name you already have.
+Can I use these names in Elder Scrolls Online?
Yes. ESO names must be unique on your server, and a first name with a surname is much easier to find free than a single name.
+Is this an official Elder Scrolls tool?
No. It's a fan-made, unofficial generator, not connected to Bethesda Softworks or ZeniMax. The names are new, and free to use for your characters.
