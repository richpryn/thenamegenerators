How the Chinese Dragon Name Generator Works

It works from 247 names and name parts, which combine into over 1,900 possible results.

The choices:

  • Name format:
    • With its meaning, e.g. Jinhuo (“gold fire”)
    • With a title, e.g. Qiuhui, Lord of the Thunder Clouds
    • Name only, e.g. Qiuhai

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

The Chinese dragon, or long, is a wise, lucky creature of water and weather, not a fire-breathing monster: it brings rain, rules rivers and seas, and guards pearls. This Chinese dragon name generator makes names from real Chinese words, like Leiyun for thunder cloud, with their meanings and a title.
