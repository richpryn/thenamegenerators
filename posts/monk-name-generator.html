How the Monk Name Generator Works

It works from 439 names and name parts, which combine into over 690,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Gender:
    • Any, e.g. Bree the Empty Hand
    • Male, e.g. Caedmon the Staggering
    • Female, e.g. Cecily the Daybreak
  • Tradition: Any tradition, Way of the Open Hand, Way of Shadow, Way of the Four Elements, Way of the Drunken Master, Way of the Kensei, Way of the Sun Soul, Way of the Long Death, Way of Mercy, Way of the Astral Self.
  • Name format: Name and epithet, Name only.
  • Starts with: Any letter, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O, P, R, S, T, U, V, W, Z.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Monks are defined by discipline, and their names are usually simple, calm and easy to say between breaths. This monk name generator gives D&D 5e monks a short, steady first name and an epithet for their Monastic Tradition, from the Way of the Open Hand to the Way of the Drunken Master.

The monk in D&D

A D&D monk fights with fists and focus, channelling inner energy into fast, precise strikes.

  • Traditions: monks follow a Monastic Tradition, from the Way of the Open Hand, Shadow and the Four Elements in the Player's Handbook to the Drunken Master, Kensei, Sun Soul, Long Death, Mercy and Astral Self in later books.
  • The word: monk comes from the Greek monachos, "solitary".

Naming a monk

Monk names here are calm, plain English names: Vera, Linus, Ada, Wren.

  • The epithet follows the tradition: an Open Hand monk "the Still Hand", a Shadow monk "the Shadow-Step", a Drunken Master "the Lucky Stumble".
  • No borrowed names: the generator avoids taking names and terms from real Buddhist and other religious traditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's a good monk name?
A calm, plain name, like Vera or Linus, and an epithet for the tradition, like "the Still Hand".
+What are the monk subclasses?
Way of the Open Hand, Shadow and the Four Elements in the Player's Handbook, plus Drunken Master, Kensei, Sun Soul, Long Death, Mercy and Astral Self.
+What does monk mean?
It comes from the Greek monachos, meaning solitary.
