How gym names work

  • Strong names borrow from industry and stone: Quarry Strength Club, Slate Fitness Club, The Anvil Yard.
  • Classic names use a surname or a building: Emerson's Strength Club, The Old Brewery Gym.
  • Modern names are one word: Charge Fitness, Stride.
  • Funny names win on social media: Planks a Lot, Swole Mates Gym.

Choose Personal training or Studio for the right trade words: PT, coaching, training studio, bootcamp or classes. A barbell club suggests lifting; a studio suggests classes; a health club suggests a pool and changing rooms.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Press Check on any name, or paste one into the Business Name Checker, to see whether the domain and social handles are free and to jump to the company register and trade mark searches. Then:

  • Search the company register. In the UK that's Companies House; in the US, your state's business register.
  • Search trade marks with the UK Intellectual Property Office or the USPTO, especially if you'll sell products or open more than one site.
  • Look locally. Search Google Maps for gyms near you: a similar name a few streets away will confuse customers even if it's legally fine.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For more on choosing and checking a name, read our guide: Business Naming Best Practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good gym names?
Short, strong names that look good on a T-shirt: Foundry Barbell, Benchmark Gym, Torque Gym, or something friendlier like Kettle On Fitness. Choose what you run for the right words.
+What should I call my personal training business?
Many personal trainers use their own name, because clients buy the coach: "Dan Hale PT", "Train with Dan" or "Coach Dan". If you plan to hire other trainers or open a studio later, choose a brand name instead ("Kettle On Fitness", "Northgate Strength"), so the business can grow without you on every session. Either way, check the Instagram handle and domain are free before you print anything.
+How do I check a gym or fitness business name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, also search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as another business nearby with a similar name can confuse clients.
+What are funny gym names?
Puns people share, like Planks a Lot, Swole Mates Gym, Squats & Giggles or Will Train for Snacks. Choose Funny for more.
