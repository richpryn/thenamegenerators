How streetwear names work

Streetwear grew out of surf, skate and hip-hop, and the names still sound like it. Supreme opened as a skate shop in New York in 1994, and Palace started as a London skate crew before it was a brand. The labels people chase tend to share a few habits:

  • Crew names: Club, Society, Collective, Union. The brand sounds like something you belong to.
  • Drop language: limited releases are part of the appeal, so words like Waitlist, One Run and Archive work.
  • Utility words: Supply, Goods, Dept. and Uniform Co. make a small label sound established.
  • Numbers: a year, a postcode or a drop number, as in Sarah 31, for a name that looks good on a tag.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Before you pay for a logo, labels or a stall banner, press Check on the name you like, or paste it into the Business Name Checker. It looks for:

  • Company names: in the UK, Companies House won't register a limited company name that's the same as one already on the register. In the US, check your state's business register.
  • Trade marks: search the UK Intellectual Property Office and, if you sell abroad, the USPTO. A registered company name doesn't give you trade mark rights.
  • Web and social: the .com or .co.uk, plus the Instagram, TikTok and Etsy handles streetwear customers will look for.

Clothing sits in trade mark class 25, one of the most crowded classes there is, so a trade mark search matters more here than in most trades. Keep well away from big labels' names and logos.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full checklist, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Making it work on a garment

  • Test it as a print. Mock it up as a chest print, a back print and a woven label before you order.
  • Keep the handle short. Drops sell on Instagram and TikTok, so a long name becomes a clumsy handle.
  • Leave room to grow. A name tied to one design or one joke can feel stuck after a few drops.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good streetwear brand names?
Short names that sound like a crew, a drop or a skate trick: No Restock Clothing, Noseslide Club, Night Owls Collective or Atelier Pewter for a luxury feel. Pick a style to get names like these.
+How do I check a streetwear brand name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it is free as a company name, a web address and a social media handle. In the UK, search the Companies House register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Do this before you pay for a logo, labels or signage.
+What makes a good streetwear brand name?
Short, easy to say and good on a chest print or a neck label. Streetwear names often sound like a crew (Night Owls Collective), a catalogue number (Issue 04) or a skate term (Grip Tape Goods). Stay well clear of the big labels and their logos: Supreme, Palace and Stüssy guard their names closely, and clothing is a crowded trade mark class.
+Should a streetwear brand have numbers in its name?
It can work well: a year, a drop number or a postcode looks good on a tag and is harder for someone else to have already. Check the handle is free, as short number names are popular.
