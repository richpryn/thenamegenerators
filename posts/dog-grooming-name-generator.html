Good dog grooming names are friendly, a little playful and say what you do: Groom With a View, Bubbles & Pups or The Posh Pooch. A grooming name goes on the shop front, the van and every booking reminder, so it should sound friendly and say what you do. This Dog Grooming Name Generator has names in eight styles, including mobile grooming and names built from your own name or town.

Eight good dog grooming names

All eight came from the generator:

  • Groom With a View: a pun on the film title that owners will repeat.
  • Ruff Around the Edges: funny, and honest about how dogs arrive.
  • Bubbles & Pups: cute and clear, with the bath in the name.
  • Lather Up Pups: catchy, with a word that sounds like a good wash.
  • The Posh Pooch: a classic for a salon with a smart shop front.
  • Coat & Co.: short and modern, and it works on a van.
  • Pups on Wheels: says you come to the customer.
  • Grooming by Sarah: your own name, for a one-person home studio.

What owners look for in a grooming name

Owners want a groomer who is gentle, tidy and trustworthy with their dog, so the best names balance fun with care.

  • Bath and coat words make the job clear: suds, lather, bubbles, fluff, coat, trim.
  • Dog words make it friendly: pups, paws, hound, pooch, wag.
  • Puns stick in the memory, but keep one per name: From Hairy to Handsome works because it's a story, not just a joke.
  • Spa, Boutique and Lounge say you charge a little more: Kensington Dog Spa, Glossy Spaniel Grooming Boutique.

If you're mobile, say so in the name. on Wheels, Mobile or on the Move tells people what the van is for before they read the phone number.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Every name here has a Check button, which opens the Business Name Checker: it looks up domains, social handles, the company register and trade mark searches in one place. Before you order signs or cards:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; a limited company's name can't be the same as one already registered. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK Intellectual Property Office or the USPTO for marks in your line of work. Registering a company doesn't give you trade mark rights.
  • Domain and handles. Try the .co.uk or .com and the Instagram and Facebook names at the same time, and change the name slightly rather than settle for a messy handle.
  • Local search. Look up the name with your town. Two similar businesses a few miles apart confuse customers and reviews.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good dog grooming business names?
Friendly names that say what you do, like Bubbles & Pups, Groom With a View or The Posh Pooch. Bath words (suds, lather, fluff) and dog words (paws, pups, hound) make the job clear at a glance.
+What is a good name for a mobile dog grooming business?
Say that you come to the customer: words like on Wheels, on the Go, Mobile or Van tell people at a glance, and the name reads well on the side of the van. Pick the Mobile grooming style for ideas such as Pups on Wheels or Lather on the Move.
+Are funny dog grooming names a good idea?
Yes, if the joke is kind. Names like Ruff Around the Edges or From Grubby to Sparkling make owners smile and remember you; avoid anything that makes the dogs sound neglected.
+How do I check a dog grooming name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Before you commit, search the official registers too: in the UK, Companies House; in the US, your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search.
