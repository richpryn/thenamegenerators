How the Dog Grooming Name Generator Works

It works from 390 names and name parts, which combine into over 5,900 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, Catchy and punny, Cute and friendly, Funny, Classy and luxury, Unique and modern, Mobile grooming, From your town, From your name.
  • Your town or area: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Hampton").
  • Your first name: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Sarah").

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A grooming name goes on the shop front, the van and every booking reminder, so it should sound friendly and say what you do. This Dog Grooming Name Generator has names in eight styles, including mobile grooming and names built from your own name or town.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a dog grooming name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Before you commit, search the official registers too: in the UK, Companies House; in the US, your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search.
+What is a good name for a mobile dog grooming business?
Say that you come to the customer: words like on Wheels, on the Go, Mobile or Van tell people at a glance, and the name reads well on the side of the van. Pick the Mobile grooming style for ideas such as Suds on Wheels or Happy Paws Mobile Grooming.
