How the Cleric Name Generator Works

It works from 471 names and name parts, which combine into over 810,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Domain: Any domain, Life Domain, Light Domain, War Domain, Knowledge Domain, Nature Domain, Tempest Domain, Trickery Domain, Grave Domain, Forge Domain, Twilight Domain, Order Domain, Peace Domain.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A cleric's name often sounds like it belongs in a temple, and their epithet tells you which power they serve. This cleric name generator gives D&D 5e clerics a first name with a saintly ring and an epithet for their Divine Domain, from the healing Life Domain to the storm-calling Tempest Domain.

The cleric in D&D

A D&D cleric's power comes from a god, and each cleric serves one Divine Domain.

  • Domains range from Life and Light to War, Tempest, Trickery, Knowledge, Nature, Grave, Forge, Twilight, Order and Peace. Each gets its own epithets here.
  • Channel Divinity lets a cleric call on their god directly, for example to turn the undead.
  • The word: cleric shares its root with clergy and clerk, from the Greek klēros, meaning a lot or share, as in the share given to God.

Naming a cleric

Cleric names here sound like saints and scholars: Cecilia, Cyprian, Winifred.

  • The epithet tells you which power they serve: a Forge cleric might be "Keeper of the Forge", a War cleric "Hammer of the Gods".
  • Name the god too: the Angel Name Generator has names for the messengers of the gods.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's a good cleric name?
A saint-like name, like Cecilia or Cyprian, with an epithet for the domain, like "Keeper of the Forge".
+What's the difference between a cleric and a paladin?
A cleric is a priest who casts divine spells; a paladin is a holy warrior bound by an oath, who fights first and casts second.
+What are the cleric domains?
They include Life, Light, War, Knowledge, Nature, Tempest, Trickery, Grave, Forge, Twilight, Order and Peace.
