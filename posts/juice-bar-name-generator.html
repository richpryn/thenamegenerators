What makes a juice bar name work

  • Fresh words: pulp, press, peel, rind, root and leaf all say "made in front of you".
  • Juicery is a modern word for a juice shop, and suits cold-pressed juice in bottles as well as a counter.
  • Juice or smoothie? Name what most of your menu is, or choose a name without either word if you might add bowls and coffee later.
  • Keep health claims out of the name. Words that promise to cure or detox can fall foul of food advertising rules.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A name from a generator is a starting point, not permission to use it. Before you order the cups, bottle labels or sign:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; you can't register a limited company with the same name as an existing one. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK trade mark register (or the USPTO in the US) for the goods and services you'll sell.
  • Domain and handles. Check the .co.uk or .com and the Instagram and TikTok handles you'll need.
  • Your area. Search a map app and the delivery apps for businesses with the same or a similar name nearby.

Press Check on any name, or use the Business Name Checker, to run most of these at once. For the full process, read our guide, Business Naming Best Practices. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

In the UK, any food business must register with the local council at least 28 days before it starts trading. Registration is free, and the council will inspect you and give you a food hygiene rating.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good juice bar names?
Fresh, bright names: Beet Goes On Juicery, Glow Getter Juice Co., Celery & Mint Juice Bar or Pulp & Pith. Choose a style for punny, cute, fresh, tropical or modern names.
+How do I check a juice bar name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Before you commit, search the official registers too: in the UK, Companies House and the UK trade mark register; in the US, your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Look on a map app for businesses with the same name nearby.
+Should my name say juice bar or smoothie bar?
Say what most of your menu is. If you might add smoothies, acai bowls or coffee later, a name without the drink in it (Peel Good, Wild Leaf) or with a wide word like Juice Co. or Juicery gives you room. Avoid health claims in the name, such as cures or detox promises, which advertising rules in the UK and US restrict.
+What's a juicery?
A modern word for a juice shop, often one that sells cold-pressed juice in bottles. It's a good word to use if you sell more than drinks over the counter.
