How the Hamster Name Generator Works

It works from 1,335 names and name parts, which combine into over 13,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Boy or girl:
    • Any, e.g. Steamroller
    • Boy, e.g. Prince Whiskerbun
    • Girl, e.g. Princess Cow
  • Style or colour: Any, Cute, Funny, Food, Big names, Human names, Golden or brown, White, Grey, Black and white.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A hamster's name gets said a lot for something that fits in your hand, so it might as well make you smile. This hamster name generator gives boy and girl names in five styles, from cute and funny to food, human names and big names for small pets, plus names for golden, white, grey and black and white hamsters. The names suit gerbils and pet mice just as well.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's a good name for a boy hamster?
Something short that's fun to say, like Squeakums, Snufflebun or Cheeks, or a grand name that's funny on a tiny pet, like Lord Algernon or Titan. Choose Boy and a style above for more ideas in the same mood.
+Do hamsters know their name?
Hamsters can learn that a sound means something good is coming, so a short name said gently before a treat often gets a twitch of the ears. They won't come when called like a dog, but a name with one or two syllables helps.
