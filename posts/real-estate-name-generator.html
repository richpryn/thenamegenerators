Estate agent or realtor? The words matter

The same job has different names on each side of the Atlantic, and the right word helps people searching for you.

  • In the UK you're an estate agent if you sell and a letting agent if you rent. Names usually end in Estate Agents, Property, Homes or Lettings.
  • In the US firms use Realty, Real Estate, Properties or Group. REALTOR® is a registered trade mark of the National Association of Realtors, for its members, so don't use it in a business name unless you belong.
  • Teams inside a brokerage often take the lead agent's surname: The Ingram Team. US states and brokerages have their own rules on team names, so check with your broker first.

Choose US or UK in the generator and the names use the matching words. Works anywhere sticks to Homes and Properties, which read naturally in both.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Every name here has a Check button, which opens the Business Name Checker: it looks up domains, social handles, the company register and trade mark searches in one place. Before you order signs or cards:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; a limited company's name can't be the same as one already registered. In the US, search your state's business register. UK estate and letting agents must also belong to a government-approved redress scheme before they trade.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK Intellectual Property Office or the USPTO for marks in your line of work. Registering a company doesn't give you trade mark rights.
  • Domain and handles. Try the .co.uk or .com and the Instagram and Facebook names at the same time, and change the name slightly rather than settle for a messy handle.
  • Local search. Look up the name with your town. Two similar businesses a few miles apart confuse customers and reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good real estate business names?
Names that sound trustworthy and local: a surname firm like Bramley & Co., a warm idea like Welcome Mat Property Co., or a team name like The Walsh Home Team. Choose US or UK wording to match what clients search for.
+Can I call my business "Realtors"?
Only with care. REALTOR® is a trade mark of the US National Association of Realtors, for its members, so these names use "Realty" and "Real Estate" instead. US states and brokerages also have rules for team names (some ban words like "Realty" or "Group" for teams), so check with your broker and state licensing board. In the UK, an estate agency can use any name that isn't already taken.
+What's a good name for a real estate team?
The lead agent's surname plus Team or Home Team (The Ingram Team) is the usual shape, because clients often follow an agent rather than a brand. Choose the Team or group style for more.
+How do I check a real estate business name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as two similar names nearby confuse customers.
