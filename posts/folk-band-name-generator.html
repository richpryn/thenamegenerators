How the Folk Band Name Generator Works

It works from 628 names and name parts, which combine into over 13,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • What are you naming?:
    • Band, e.g. The Ramsden Family
    • Singer, e.g. Joan Urquhart
    • Album, e.g. Ballads of the Fens
  • Singer's gender: depends on the what are you naming?:
    • Band and Album: Any
    • Singer: Any, Male, Female

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Folk bands borrow their names from old songs, birds, villages and the families who sang them. This folk band name generator makes band names, singer names and album titles in the style of English trad, folk-rock, Appalachian ballads and nu-folk.
