Good cyberpunk city names sound crowded, corporate and a little worn out: a sprawl named like a metro area, districts named after the industry that left, and towers named after the company that owns them. This cyberpunk city name generator names every layer of a future megacity: the sprawl itself, its districts and neighbourhoods, gleaming corporate arcologies, slums and undercities, and the neon nightlife strips in between. How cyberpunk names its cities

The genre's cities grew out of William Gibson's Neuromancer (1984), set in the Sprawl, the Boston–Atlanta Metropolitan Axis: one city running down a whole coastline. Since then, cyberpunk cities have tended to be named like real metropolitan areas, with the old names still showing through. The tabletop game Cyberpunk gave us Night City, later the setting of Cyberpunk 2077.

  • Megacity names use the language of planning departments: Metro, Greater, Special Zone, Sprawl. Greater Halira, Zania Special Zone.
  • Districts keep the name of what used to be there: a cannery, a gantry, a chimney. Chimney Heights, the Gantry Blocks.
  • Arcologies are vast self-contained buildings, a word the architect Paolo Soleri made from architecture and ecology. Here they're named after the corporations that own them: the Prentiss Arcology, Goldleaf Ziggurat.
  • Slums and undercities are named by the people who live there: Bucket Bottoms, Rag End, Mouldtown.
  • Neon strips sound like a night out: Kaleidoscope Strip, Ultraviolet Boulevard.

Choose With a description for a line about the place, like the Gantry Blocks, whose residents grow vegetables on every balcony.

Building your sprawl

  • Name the city first, then go down. One megacity name, a handful of districts, two or three arcologies and one undercity is plenty for a campaign.
  • Let the corporations show. If a tower is named after a company, decide what the company makes and how far its name reaches into the streets.
  • Give the poor parts better names. Official names are bland; the nicknames locals use are where the character is.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good cyberpunk city names?
Names that sound like a real metro area grown too big, like Greater Halira or the Sevara–Ostira Sprawl, with districts named after old industries (Cannery Square) and towers named after corporations (the Prentiss Arcology).
+What is an arcology?
A huge self-contained building where people live, work and shop without leaving. The word, from architecture and ecology, came from the architect Paolo Soleri, and cyberpunk turned it into the corporate tower.
+Can I use these names in my RPG?
Yes. They're made up and free to use. Choose With a description for a ready-made hook for each district.
