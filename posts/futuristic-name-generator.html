What future names really sound like

In good science fiction, future names aren't random letters: they're today's names, worn smooth and mixed by centuries of travel.

  • Mixing: when people move, names travel with them, so a future crew list mixes surnames from every continent.
  • Double-barrelled surnames join two families, as many families already do: Elara Okafor-Lindqvist.
  • Sleek first names like Kael, Nova and Elowen are short, easy to say over a radio and hard to place in any one century.

Naming your crew

  • Choose First name for a single name, Full name for a first name and surname, or Double-barrelled for a joined surname.
  • Mix it up: a crew where every name comes from one place will feel like today, not the future.
  • Famous names like Shepard, Ripley, Deckard and Atreides are left out.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's a good futuristic name?
A short, sleek first name like Kael or Elara with a surname from anywhere in the world, or a double-barrelled one, like Elara Okafor-Lindqvist.
+How do you make a name sound futuristic?
Mix names from different cultures, keep first names short, and join surnames. Random letters tend to sound silly rather than futuristic.
+What's a good name for a spaceship captain?
A full name with a strong surname, like Tarek Vasquez-Oduya. Choose Full name or Double-barrelled.
