How florists name themselves

  • Your own name builds trust for weddings and funerals, where people want to know who they're dealing with.
  • A flower in the name tells people what you do: Peony, Posy, Larkspur, Clover.
  • French suggests premium floristry: fleur is flower, pivoine peony, muguet lily of the valley and lilas lilac.
  • The Victorians gave flowers meanings, known as floriography or the language of flowers, so a name can carry a message: forget-me-nots for remembrance, for example.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Before you pay for a logo, labels or a stall banner, press Check on the name you like, or paste it into the Business Name Checker. It looks for:

  • Company names: in the UK, Companies House won't register a limited company name that's the same as one already on the register. In the US, check your state's business register.
  • Trade marks: search the UK Intellectual Property Office and, if you sell abroad, the USPTO. A registered company name doesn't give you trade mark rights.
  • Web and social: the .com or .co.uk, plus the Instagram, TikTok and Etsy handles florist customers will look for.

Many flower orders come through relay networks and online directories, so also check your name isn't close to one of the big flower brands customers might confuse you with.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full checklist, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for a florist

  • Say it on the phone. If you have to spell it every time, simplify it.
  • Think about weddings. A pun can feel too light for wedding and funeral flowers; a name or a French word suits them better.
  • Make it work on a van and a ribbon, where it'll be seen most.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good florist names?
Names that say flowers and are easy to say on the phone: Poppies by Sarah, Clover & Stem, Stem Cellar for a pun, or Atelier Muguet for a French, premium feel.
+How do I check a florist name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it is free as a company name, a web address and a social media handle. In the UK, search the Companies House register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Do this before you pay for a logo, labels or signage.
+What is a good name for a flower shop?
One that says flowers at a glance and is easy to say over the phone, because many orders still come by phone. Your own name (Flowers by Sarah) builds trust locally, a pun such as Peony for Your Thoughts is memorable, and a French word such as Pivoine (peony) feels premium. Avoid names close to Interflora, Bloom & Wild or Teleflora.
+What are some French words for a flower shop name?
Fleur (flower), pivoine (peony), muguet (lily of the valley), lilas (lilac) and atelier (workshop) are popular. Keep the accents in your logo but check the name without them for web addresses.
