How the Half-Orc Name Generator Works

It works from 894 names and name parts, which combine into over 540,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Raised: Between two worlds, Among humans, Among orcs.
  • Gender: Any, Male, Female.
  • Name Format:

    • With a surname or deed-name, e.g. Zyltha Grimsunderer

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

A half-orc's name says more about who raised them than about their blood. One brought up in a human village might be plain Bram or Hester; one raised in an orc camp, Ugak or Mogsha; and one who's never quite belonged to either might have a name that's a bit of both. This half-orc name generator lets you choose the upbringing, then adds a surname or a deed-name to match.

Names follow upbringing

The D&D Player's Handbook has always said this: half-orcs usually have names from the culture they were raised in. A half-orc raised by humans takes a human name; one raised by orcs, an orc name. Some choose the other kind on purpose, to fit in or to make a point.

  • Among humans: short, plain, working names, the kind given in farming villages and frontier towns. The surname is a trade or a place.
  • Among orcs: hard orc names, with a deed-name that has to be earned.
  • Between two worlds: names with orc sounds and human shapes, for a half-orc who has drifted between both.

A name with a history

  • Two names at once. A half-orc might have an orc name from their mother's people and a human name for town. Which one they give says who they think they're talking to.
  • A name they chose. Plenty of half-orcs in fiction drop the name they were given. That's a good backstory question: who gave you your name, and why don't you use it?
  • A deed-name in a human town. Skullsplitter means one thing in an orc camp and another on a wanted poster.

Half-orcs in the 2024 rules

The 2024 Player's Handbook dropped half-orcs as a separate choice: an orc character with human family is now just an orc, or a human, with the background you give them. The names work exactly the same way, and this generator fits either edition.

For full orcs, try the Orc Name Generator, and for a half-orc who took up a sword and a code, the Knight Name Generator.

Frequently Asked Questions

+Should a half-orc have an orc name or a human name?
Whichever the people who raised them would give. Choose "Among humans" or "Among orcs" for that, or "Between two worlds" for a name that mixes both sounds.
+What's a deed-name?
An orc-style surname that describes something the character did, like Bonebreaker or Elfbiter. In orc culture it's earned, and it can be lost.
+Can I use these names in D&D?
Yes, they're free to use and you don't need to credit the site. They suit half-orcs from the 2014 rules and orc characters with human family from the 2024 rules.
