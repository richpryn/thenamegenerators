Minecraft server name ideas

A good Minecraft server name is short, easy to type and says what kind of server it is.

  • Short and easy to type: players will search for it, say it on Discord and put it in video titles.
  • Say what it is: a tag like SMP, Factions or Skyblock tells people what to expect before they join.
  • Check it's free: search server lists, Discord and YouTube before you commit, and check the web address if you'll want a website.

SMP, Realm, factions and skyblock names

Different kinds of Minecraft server suit different names.

  • SMP stands for survival multiplayer: a shared survival world, usually for friends or a community.
  • Realms are Mojang's own paid servers, easiest for a small group of friends. The SMP and survival styles suit them.
  • Factions: teams claim land and raid each other.
  • Skyblock: everyone starts on a tiny island in the sky.
  • Prison: players mine their way up through ranks.
  • Minigames: Bedwars, Skywars and the like, so names that sound like a network.
  • Anarchy: no rules, so names that sound like it.
  • Creative and roleplay: builders and storytellers.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What does SMP mean in Minecraft?
Survival multiplayer: a survival world shared by a group of players, often friends or a creator's community.
+What's a good name for an SMP?
A short, memorable word plus SMP, like Storm SMP or JadeGlen SMP. Choose the SMP style.
+What's a good name for a Minecraft Realm?
Realms are usually for friends, so pick something everyone will recognise. The SMP and survival styles work well.
+What are good factions server names?
Factions servers suit names that sound like a battleground or an empire, ending in Factions: choose Factions for names like RunePort Factions.
