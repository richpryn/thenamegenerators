  • Off the Mat Yoga & Pilates: a phrase yoga teachers already use.
  • Spring Loaded Pilates: a pun on the reformer's springs.
  • The Hayloft Yoga Studio: a classic name from the room itself.
  • Studio Reform: modern and minimal, for reformer Pilates.
  • Fig & Clay Yoga Co.: an aesthetic name in warm colours.
  • Drishti Yoga House: Sanskrit for a focused gaze.
  • Karuna Pilates Studio: Sanskrit for compassion.

How yoga and Pilates names work

  • Sanskrit words carry meaning, and a shala is a yoga school or house: Chandra Yoga Shala. Pick words you can explain, and use them respectfully.
  • Pilates is named after Joseph Pilates, who developed the method and the reformer machine. Reformer names often play on springs, cores and control: To the Core Reformer Studio.
  • Aesthetic names use calm colours and natural materials: Studio Birch, Terracotta Yoga.
  • Classic names use a street or a building: Church Street Yoga House.

Choose Yoga or Pilates to get the right trade words, or leave it on Any for a studio that teaches both.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Press Check on any name, or paste one into the Business Name Checker, to see whether the domain and social handles are free and to jump to the company register and trade mark searches. Then:

  • Search the company register. In the UK that's Companies House; in the US, your state's business register.
  • Search trade marks with the UK Intellectual Property Office or the USPTO, especially if you'll sell products or open more than one site.
  • Look locally. Search Google Maps for yoga studios near you: a similar name a few streets away will confuse customers even if it's legally fine.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For more on choosing and checking a name, read our guide: Business Naming Best Practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good yoga studio names?
Calm, easy names that say what you teach: Slow Flow Yoga, The Hayloft Yoga Studio, Fig & Clay Yoga Co., or a Sanskrit name like Drishti Yoga House.
+What are good Sanskrit names for a yoga studio?
Choose a short word whose meaning you can explain to students: prana (breath, life force), shanti (peace), sukha (ease, happiness), sthira (steady), ananda (bliss), sangha (community) or santosha (contentment). Use it respectfully and check the spelling with a trusted source. Some words, such as surya (sun), chandra (moon) and agni (fire), are also names of Hindu deities, so think carefully before using them. Pair it with a plain word ("Sukha Yoga Shala", "Prana Pilates") so people know what you offer.
+How do I check a yoga studio name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, also search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as another business nearby with a similar name can confuse clients.
+What's a good name for a Pilates studio?
Something that hints at the method or the reformer, like Spring Loaded Pilates, Studio Reform or To the Core Reformer Studio. Choose Pilates for names like these.
