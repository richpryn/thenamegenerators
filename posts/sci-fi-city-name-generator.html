Good sci-fi city names tell you what kind of future you're in: a bright utopia, a dome on a cold planet, an alien city or the ruins of somewhere that used to be grand. How future cities get their names

Science fiction has a few famous cities that are the size of planets: Isaac Asimov's Trantor, the capital of his Foundation galaxy, and Coruscant in Star Wars. Most fictional cities are smaller, and many are named the way real colonies were: after somewhere left behind, or after a hope. New Amsterdam became New York when the English took it in 1664, and colony names in fiction often begin with New for the same reason.

  • Utopian cities are named after ideals: Accord, Concord, Harmony, Bright.
  • Alien cities use sounds and shapes that don't belong to any human language: Qhyl'eth Aessal, Ythoom.
  • Domed colonies are named by tired engineers and hopeful founders: Lifeline Habitat, Valdés's Hope.
  • Ruined cities keep a grand old word with something sad in front of it: Sunken Prosperity, Broken Victory.
  • Solarpunk cities are green and gentle: Juniper Rise, Lichen Commons.

Naming cities for your setting

  • Decide who named it. A colony named by a corporation sounds different from one named by its settlers.
  • Let names age. A city called New something for three hundred years is a nice detail; so is a nickname that replaced the official name.
  • Match the planet. A city on a frozen world should sound cold, and the planet it's on deserves a name too.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good sci-fi city names?
Names that show what kind of future it is: Ophena Spires for a utopia, Frostline Dome for a colony, the Remains of Optimism for a ruin, or Ythoom for an alien city.
+How do you name a space colony?
After a founder, a hope or the thing that keeps it alive, the way real colonies were named: Nakamura's Hope, Lifeline Habitat, Frostline Dome. Choose Domed colony for names like these.
+What is solarpunk?
A hopeful kind of science fiction about green, sustainable cities, where people live alongside plants and clean energy. Choose Solarpunk for names like Juniper Rise and Bracken Greenway.
