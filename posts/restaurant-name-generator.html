How the Restaurant Name Generator Works

It works from 785 names and name parts, which combine into over 3,400 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, Catchy and fun, Classic, Modern, Fancy and fine dining, Italian, Spanish and Mexican, From your town, From your name.
  • Your first name: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Sarah").
  • Your town or area: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Hampton").

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A restaurant name sets expectations before anyone reads the menu, and it has to work on a sign, a booking site and a map search. This restaurant name generator gives you names in eight styles, from catchy to fine dining, Italian and Spanish, including ones built from your town or your own name.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a restaurant name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Before you commit, search the official registers too: in the UK, Companies House and the UK trade mark register; in the US, your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Look on a map app for businesses with the same name nearby.
+How do I come up with a unique restaurant name?
Start with what makes the place yours: your name, the street, the building, the cooking or the feeling you want. Say it out loud, picture it on the sign and type it into a map search to see whether a restaurant nearby already uses it. Avoid names so narrow that you can't change the menu later.
