Blood elves, the sin'dorei, are proud, elegant and a little vain, and their names show it: Kael'thas Sunstrider, Lor'themar Theron, Lady Liadrin, Halduron Brightwing. This blood elf name generator gives you sin'dorei first names, some with the apostrophe of Kael'thas and Lor'themar, and surnames full of sun, flame and gold.

How blood elf names work

  • First names are graceful and often long. Some are split by an apostrophe: Kael'thas, Lor'themar. Others aren't: Liadrin, Rommath, Halduron.
  • Surnames are often English compounds about light, the sun or flight: Sunstrider, Windrunner, Brightwing. Some, like Theron, are single elven words.

The surnames in this generator are new compounds in that style, leaning on the sun, flame and gold that suit Silvermoon.

From high elves to blood elves

The blood elves were once the high elves, the quel'dorei, of Quel'Thalas. After the Scourge destroyed much of their kingdom and corrupted the Sunwell, the survivors took a new name, sin'dorei, "children of the blood", in memory of their dead. Their city, Silvermoon, and the restored Sunwell are at the heart of their story.

That history is useful for naming: a blood elf old enough to remember Quel'Thalas before its fall might keep an older, gentler name, while one born after might choose something fiercer.

Getting a free name in WoW

WoW names must be one word and unique on your realm, and can't include an apostrophe, so a name like Kael'dorin needs to become Kaeldorin in game. Use the full name with surname for roleplay.

For other Warcraft races, see the Night Elf, Troll and Orc name generators.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What does sin'dorei mean?
"Children of the blood". The high elves took the name after the fall of Quel'Thalas, in memory of those who died.
+Can I use an apostrophe in a WoW name?
No. WoW character names are a single word of letters only, so drop the apostrophe: Kael'dorin becomes Kaeldorin.
+Are blood elves the same as high elves?
They were the same people. Most high elves became blood elves after Quel'Thalas fell; a few kept the old name, quel'dorei.
