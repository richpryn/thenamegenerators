How the Paranormal Podcast Name Generator Works

It works from 545 names and name parts, which combine into over 71,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Show style: Any, Eerie, Investigative, Cosy-spooky, Folklore.
  • Name format:
    • Show name, e.g. The Hollinsford Logbook
    • With a tagline, e.g. The Galloway Case Notes: true hauntings examined

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A good ghost show name tells listeners what kind of night they're in for: a candlelit chill, a sceptical case file, a cosy cup of tea with a poltergeist, or old tales from the hedgerow. This paranormal podcast name generator makes original names for ghost, true-haunting and folklore podcasts and YouTube channels, with a tagline if you want one.
