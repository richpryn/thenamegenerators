Good sewing business names either say exactly what you do (Better Fit Alterations & Repairs) or borrow a word from the sewing box: bobbin, thimble, hoop, stitch. Eight good sewing business names

All eight came from the generator:

  • A Stitch in Time: the proverb about mending early, for an alterations or repair service.
  • Button & Bobbin: two sewing-box words that sound friendly together.
  • The Whipstitch: a real stitch, for a studio that knows its craft.
  • Seed Stitch Embroidery Studio: named after an embroidery stitch, so it's clear what you make.
  • Sandford Tailors: a classic name-and-trade pairing for bespoke tailoring.
  • Better Fit Alterations & Repairs: says the job and the result.
  • The Sewing Shed: homely, for classes or a garden workroom.
  • Material Difference: a pun that also says quality matters.

Tailor, dressmaker or alterations?

People search for the job, so the word you choose matters:

  • Tailor traditionally means made-to-measure suits and jackets, and bespoke means cut from a pattern made for one customer.
  • Dressmaker or seamstress suits dresses, bridalwear and general sewing.
  • Alterations and repairs is what most customers type when a hem needs taking up.
  • Embroidery names often use a stitch: French knot, satin stitch, seed stitch, blanket stitch.

Choose a style to match: Classic dressmaker or tailor, Alterations and repairs or Embroidery.

Check it's free before you print the labels

Before you pay for a sign, a logo or printed bags, check the name is free:

  • Company register: if you form a limited company in the UK, Companies House won't accept a name that's the same as one already registered. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks: search the UK IPO or USPTO register for your kind of goods, so you don't clash with an existing brand.
  • Web and social: check the domain and the handles you'll use, since many customers will find you on Instagram first.

Press Check on any name, or use the Business Name Checker, to run those searches in one place. The Business Naming Best Practices guide covers the rest. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

Tips for a sewing business

  • Teaching classes? Words like room, studio or school help people find you.
  • Sewn-in labels are small, so a short name or initials works best on garments.
  • Avoid sewing machine and pattern brands.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good names for a sewing business?
Names that use a sewing word or say the job: Button & Bobbin, The Whipstitch, A Stitch in Time or Better Fit Alterations & Repairs. Puns such as Sew It Seams suit a handmade or Instagram business.
+How do I check a sewing business name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it is free as a company name, a web address and a social media handle. In the UK, search the Companies House register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Do this before you pay for a logo, labels or signage.
+What should I name my sewing business?
Say what you do, so people searching for alterations or a dressmaker find you: Perfect Fit Alterations or Langford Bespoke Tailoring work on a shop front, while a pun such as Sew It Seams or Stitched by Sarah suits a handmade or Instagram business. Avoid names close to sewing machine and pattern brands such as Singer, Janome or Simplicity.
+What's a good name for an alterations business?
Say alterations or repairs so people searching find you, and add a promise: Better Fit Alterations & Repairs, Fast Fix Bridal Alterations or The Mend Shop.
