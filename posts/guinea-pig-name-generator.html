How the Guinea Pig Name Generator Works

It works from 2,887 names and name parts, which combine into over 31,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Boy or girl:
    • Any, e.g. Charcoal & Soot
    • Boy, e.g. Digestive & Gingerbread
    • Girl, e.g. Malt Loaf & Brownie
  • Style or colour: Any, Cute, Funny, Food, Human names, White, Black, Black and white, Brown, Skinny pig.
  • One or a pair: Either, Single name, Matched pair.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Guinea pigs are happiest in pairs, so most new owners need two names that sound good together. This guinea pig name generator gives single names or matched pairs, like Parsley & Dill or Crumble & Rumble, for boys, girls or a mix. Pick a style, from cute, funny, food and human names to names for white, black, black and white, brown and skinny pigs.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good names for a pair of guinea pigs?
Pick two names that match by theme, like two herbs (Basil & Tarragon) or two puddings (Custard & Trifle), or by sound, like Wobble & Gobble. Choose Matched pair above, and Boy or Girl if you have two boars or two sows.
+Do guinea pigs know their names?
Many do. Guinea pigs soon learn that a voice and a sound mean food, and will wheek when they hear their name or the fridge door. Short names with a clear sound, said the same way each time, are easiest for them to pick out.
