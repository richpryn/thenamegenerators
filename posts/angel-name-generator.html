Almost every angel's name ends the same way, and for a reason: -el is the Hebrew word for God, so Gabriel is "God is my strength" and Raphael "God heals". This angel name generator gives you the angels named in scripture and tradition, with their meanings, or new names built the same way, masculine or feminine. Add a domain, a choir or an epithet to give your angel a purpose.

Biblical angels and their names

  • The word angel comes from the Greek angelos, a messenger, which translated the Hebrew mal'akh, meaning the same.
  • The Bible names very few. Michael and Gabriel appear by name; Raphael is in the Book of Tobit, which is in the Catholic and Orthodox Bibles.
  • The Book of Enoch, an older Jewish text, names seven archangels: Uriel, Raphael, Raguel, Michael, Sariel, Gabriel and Remiel.
  • Later tradition added many more, from Raziel, keeper of God's secrets, to Azrael, the angel of death in Islamic tradition.

Archangels and the nine choirs

Around the year 500, a writer known as Pseudo-Dionysius arranged the angels into nine ranks, or choirs, which medieval writers took up and Dante built into his Paradiso:

  • Seraphim, Cherubim and Thrones, closest to God.
  • Dominions, Virtues and Powers, who govern the order of the world.
  • Principalities, Archangels and Angels, closest to people.

Choose "With a choir" to place your angel in one of them.

Fallen angels

Fallen angels are the angels said to have rebelled against God and been cast out of heaven.

  • Lucifer means "light-bearer". The name comes from the Latin translation of a line in Isaiah about the fallen "morning star", which later tradition read as the fall of Satan.
  • The Book of Enoch tells of the Watchers, angels who came down to earth, led by Semyaza and Azazel.
  • John Milton's Paradise Lost (1667) gave the fallen angels their best-known names in English literature: Beelzebub, Mammon, Belial and Moloch among them.

The masculine and feminine styles make names that suit a fallen angel too, especially with a dark domain. For the other side entirely, try the Demon Name Generator.

Making a new angel name

  • Keep the ending. -iel, -ael and -uel make a name sound angelic at once. The new names here all use them.
  • Give them one job. Traditional angels have a single task: healing, secrets, death. An Angel of Lost Things or of Thresholds is more memorable than an angel of everything.
  • Fiction borrows freely. Castiel in Supernatural comes from Cassiel; Aziraphale in Good Omens is invented, but sounds right because of that ending.
  • Female angels are rare in tradition, where the named angels are male, but common in fiction. The feminine names here keep the angelic sound with softer endings: -aela, -ielle, -essa.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What does -el mean in angel names?
"God", in Hebrew. Most angel names are short sentences about God: Michael is "who is like God?", Raphael "God heals", Uriel "God is my light".
+Who are the seven archangels?
The Book of Enoch lists Uriel, Raphael, Raguel, Michael, Sariel, Gabriel and Remiel. Other traditions give different lists of seven, often including Jophiel, Zadkiel or Chamuel.
+Can I use these names for a character?
Yes. The new names are free to use and you don't need to credit the site, and the traditional names belong to everyone. For the other side, try the Demon Name Generator.
+What is a good fallen angel name?
An angelic name with the classic -el ending, given a dark domain, like an angel of the Last Hour. Fallen angels in tradition keep angelic names: Lucifer means light-bearer.
+What are the biblical angel names?
The Bible names only Michael and Gabriel. Raphael appears in the Book of Tobit, in the Catholic and Orthodox Bibles, and older texts like the Book of Enoch name more archangels, such as Uriel.
+Can I get names that start with a certain letter?
Yes. Use the Starts with option to pick a letter. It ignores "The" and titles like Sir or Captain, so it matches the name itself.
