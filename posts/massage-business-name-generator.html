How massage business names work

  • Say the kind of massage. Clients search for sports, deep tissue, remedial or pregnancy massage, so put the one you specialise in into the name: Comeback Sports Massage.
  • Clinical words build trust. Therapy, therapist, clinic and practice tell people this is a professional treatment: Corbett Massage Clinic.
  • Holistic names suit reflexology, reiki and relaxation massage: Fieldstone Massage & Reiki.
  • Keep puns gentle. Anything that could be read as a different kind of business will put off the clients you want.

Rules on titles differ by country. In the US, many states license massage therapists and control who can advertise massage or use the title "massage therapist", and some ask for a licence number in adverts. Check your state board before you choose a name.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Press Check on any name, or paste one into the Business Name Checker, to see whether the domain and social handles are free and to jump to the company register and trade mark searches. Then:

  • Search the company register. In the UK that's Companies House; in the US, your state's business register.
  • Search trade marks with the UK Intellectual Property Office or the USPTO, especially if you'll sell products or open more than one site.
  • Look locally. Search Google Maps for massage therapists near you: a similar name a few streets away will confuse customers even if it's legally fine.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For more on choosing and checking a name, read our guide: Business Naming Best Practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's a good name for a massage business?
A good massage business name sounds calm and professional, says what you offer (sports, remedial, deep tissue, pregnancy or holistic massage) and can't be misread. Clinical words such as "therapy", "clinic" and "remedial" build trust; avoid jokes, "luxury" words and anything that could suggest a different kind of business. In many US states only a licensed massage therapist may advertise massage, so check your state board's rules before you choose a name.
+What's a good name for a sports massage business?
Something that speaks to athletes and recovery, like Comeback Sports Massage, Grounded Sports Massage or Full Range Performance Massage. Choose Sports and remedial for names like these.
+How do I check a massage business name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, also search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as another business nearby with a similar name can confuse clients.
+Do I need a licence to call myself a massage therapist?
It depends where you are. Many US states license massage therapists and control the title, while in the UK it isn't a protected title but professional bodies offer membership. Check your local rules before you choose a name.
