  • Sip & Sketch: a class that tells you what happens.
  • Farrow Editions: sounds like a print publisher.
  • The Old Dairy Gallery: a gallery named after its building.

Prints, originals, classes or a gallery

  • Originals sell on the artist, so your own name often works best.
  • Print shops suit a brand name. Editions hints at limited runs, which are numbered, as in 12/50, and are often printed as giclée prints.
  • Classes want a welcoming name: Club, Workshop, Art Room.
  • Galleries are usually named after their founders or their building.

Pigment names make good studio names: ochre and umber are earth pigments, and cadmium gives bright yellows and reds.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Before you pay for a logo, labels or a stall banner, press Check on the name you like, or paste it into the Business Name Checker. It looks for:

  • Company names: in the UK, Companies House won't register a limited company name that's the same as one already on the register. In the US, check your state's business register.
  • Trade marks: search the UK Intellectual Property Office and, if you sell abroad, the USPTO. A registered company name doesn't give you trade mark rights.
  • Web and social: the .com or .co.uk, plus the Instagram, TikTok and Etsy handles art customers will look for.

If you sell prints of your own work you own the copyright, but never sell art based on other people's characters, logos or photographs without permission.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full checklist, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for an art business

  • Keep your name for originals. Many artists use their own name for paintings and a brand name for prints.
  • Match it to your style: soft names for soft work, bold names for bold work.
  • Check Etsy's limit of 20 characters with no spaces if you'll sell there.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good art business names?
Names that suit what you sell: your own name for originals, Sage & Sparrow for a print shop, Sip & Sketch for classes, or The Old Dairy Gallery for a gallery.
+How do I check an art business name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it is free as a company name, a web address and a social media handle. In the UK, search the Companies House register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Do this before you pay for a logo, labels or signage.
+Should I use my own name for my art business?
Many artists do: Sarah Hart Art or Art by Sarah sells you as well as the work, and buyers of originals often want to know the artist. A separate brand name (such as Pale Fern Prints) suits a print shop, a studio with classes or a business you may grow or sell one day. Many artists keep their own name for originals and a brand for prints.
+What does "Editions" mean in an art business name?
It suggests limited-edition prints: a set number of prints, each numbered (12/50, for example). It's a good word for a print shop or a small publisher.
