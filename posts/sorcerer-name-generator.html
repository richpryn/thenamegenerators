How the Sorcerer Name Generator Works

It works from 410 names and name parts, which combine into over 470,000 possible results.

The choices:

A sorcerer's magic is in their blood, and their name often hints at where it came from: a dragon, a storm, the heavens or something stranger. This sorcerer name generator gives D&D 5e sorcerers a first name with a touch of the arcane and an epithet for their Sorcerous Origin, from the Draconic Bloodline to Wild Magic.

The sorcerer in D&D

A D&D sorcerer is born with magic rather than learning it, and can bend spells with Metamagic.

  • Origins: the magic comes from somewhere: a Draconic Bloodline or Wild Magic in the Player's Handbook, or a Divine Soul, Shadow, Storm, Aberrant Mind or Clockwork Soul in later books.
  • Sorcerer or wizard? A wizard studies magic from books; a sorcerer has it in their blood.

Naming a sorcerer

A sorcerer's name often hints at where the power came from.

  • The name (Drusilla, Draven, Vesper) has something strange about it.
  • The epithet follows the origin: a Draconic sorcerer "Child of the Wyrm", a Storm sorcerer "the Storm-Born", an Aberrant Mind "Who Hears Whispers".

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's a good sorcerer name?
A name with something strange about it, like Drusilla or Vesper, and an epithet for the origin, like "Child of the Wyrm".
+What's the difference between a sorcerer and a wizard?
A wizard learns magic through study; a sorcerer is born with it.
+What are the sorcerer origins?
Draconic Bloodline and Wild Magic in the Player's Handbook, plus Divine Soul, Shadow, Storm, Aberrant Mind and Clockwork Soul in later books.
