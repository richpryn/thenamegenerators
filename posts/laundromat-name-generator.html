Laundromat, launderette or wash house?

The word tells customers where they are. "Laundromat" and "Coin Laundry" are American; "Launderette" (often spelt "laundrette") is the British word for a self-service laundry; "Wash House" recalls the public wash houses of Victorian towns; "Wash & Fold" means a service where staff do the washing; "Dry Cleaners" means professional cleaning and pressing. Choose your kind of laundry and the generator uses the right words.

Puns on suds, spin and folding work well on a sign, but read them aloud before you commit: laundry jokes can slide into double meanings, and "laundering" means something else entirely.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Every name here has a Check button. Press it before you order business cards, and run through these yourself:

  • The company register: Companies House in the UK, or your state's business register in the US.
  • Trade marks: search the UK IPO or USPTO for the same or a similar name in your field.
  • Domains and handles: a matching .co.uk or .com, and the same handle on the platforms your clients use.
  • Your street: look at nearby laundries on a map, as two similar names a few streets apart confuse customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good laundromat names?
Short names that read on a sign and say what you do: a pun (Lint Condition Laundry Co.), a cosy name (Pegs & Mittens Laundrette) or a local one (Hampton Street Wash House).
+What is a good name for a laundromat?
A good laundry name is short enough to read on a shop sign from across the street and tells people what you do: "Laundromat" or "Coin Laundry" in the US, "Launderette" in the UK, "Wash & Fold" for a service, "Dry Cleaners" for pressing. Puns on socks, suds and folding ("Lint Condition Laundry") are remembered and shared; a family or street name ("Hartley's Dry Cleaners") feels established. Read any pun aloud before you commit: laundry jokes can slide into double meanings, and "laundering" means something else entirely.
+What's the difference between a laundromat and a launderette?
Nothing but the country. Laundromat is the American word for a self-service laundry and launderette (or laundrette) the British one. Wash and fold is a service where staff do the washing, and a dry cleaner cleans and presses clothes professionally.
+How do I check a laundromat name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as two similar names nearby confuse customers.
