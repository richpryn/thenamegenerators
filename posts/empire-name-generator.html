How empires name themselves

The word comes from the Latin imperium, the power to command. Empires tend to name themselves after their founding people or city, then add titles to make the claim bigger. The Byzantine Empire called itself the Roman Empire to the end, and the Holy Roman Empire lasted so long under that name that Voltaire joked it was neither holy, nor Roman, nor an empire.

  • Roman-style names use Latin endings and titles: Imperium Aquilae, The Eagle Throne of Tertessa, the Principate and the Triumvirate.
  • Holy empires are blessed, sacred and radiant, ruled by priests or crowned by them.
  • Eastern empires are named like dynasties, after a colour, a creature or an object: The Crimson Bell Empire.
  • Steppe horde names use khanate and khaganate, the realms of a khan or a great khan. The word horde comes from a Turkic word for a camp.
  • Dark empires are dread, undying or ashen.
  • Space empires borrow political words, hegemony, suzerainty, protectorate, and number themselves like dynasties.

Building an empire

  • Give it a past. Choose With a description for a line of history, like The Oztet Horde, whose sons divided the empire between them.
  • Name the conquered too. An empire is more interesting when its provinces still remember being kingdoms.
  • Number it. A Second or Third Empire suggests rises and falls your players can find the ruins of.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good fantasy empire names?
A people's name with a grand title: The Pellantine Empire, The Radiant Throne of Luminara, The Great Khanate of Uljai or The Dread Empire of Nhexath. Pick a style to match the empire's culture.
+What are good names for a space empire?
Names that sound political and permanent: The Third Nyrari Empire, The Zorenti Suzerainty, The Empire of the Eleven Suns. Choose the Space style.
+What's a khanate?
A realm ruled by a khan, the title of rulers on the Eurasian steppe. A khaganate is ruled by a khagan, a great khan over other khans.
