Good dog walking names are friendly, easy to remember and hint at the walk itself: Tails on Trails, Wag & Wander Dog Walking or a simple Walks with Sarah. Your name goes on the van, the flyers through doors and the lead clipped to every dog, so it should be friendly and easy to remember. What makes a dog walking name work

Owners are trusting you with their dog and often their house keys, so the name should feel friendly first and funny second.

  • Walk words make it clear: walks, strolls, trails, adventures, pack walks.
  • Weather and mud are a good joke in Britain: Mucky Pooches Adventures, Wellies Dog Co.
  • Your own name or town builds trust with neighbours, and helps people searching for a dog walker near them.
  • Pack walks or solo walks: if you offer group walks, say so; some owners look for exactly that.

Some councils limit how many dogs one walker can take in parks and public spaces, so check local rules before you name the business after big pack walks.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Every name here has a Check button, which opens the Business Name Checker: it looks up domains, social handles, the company register and trade mark searches in one place. Before you order signs or cards:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; a limited company's name can't be the same as one already registered. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK Intellectual Property Office or the USPTO for marks in your line of work. Registering a company doesn't give you trade mark rights.
  • Domain and handles. Try the .co.uk or .com and the Instagram and Facebook names at the same time, and change the name slightly rather than settle for a messy handle.
  • Local search. Look up the name with your town. Two similar businesses a few miles apart confuse customers and reviews.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good dog walking business names?
Friendly names that picture the walk, like Tails on Trails, Wag & Wander Dog Walking or Stroll Dog Co. Your own name, as in Walks with Sarah, works well for a local walker.
+What is a good dog walking business name for kids?
Keep it simple and honest: your first name plus what you do, such as Sam's Dog Walking or Walks with Sam, is easy for neighbours to remember and trust. Pick the From your name or Cute and friendly style, and ask a parent to help with flyers and payments.
+What are funny dog walking names?
Puns on walking and dog words, like Leash Expectations, Dogged Determination or Doggy Paddle Walks. Choose the Funny or Catchy style for more.
+How do I check a dog walking name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Before you commit, search the official registers too: in the UK, Companies House; in the US, your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search.
