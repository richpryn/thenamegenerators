How the Minecraft Name From Your Name Generator Works

It works from 370 names and name parts, which combine into over 970 possible results.

The choices:

  • Your name: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Sam").
  • Style:
    • Any, e.g. LilSam
    • With a mob, e.g. SamWither
    • Miner and ores, e.g. Sam_Calcite

    • Funny, e.g. SamTheNoob

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

This Minecraft name from your name generator turns your first name or nickname into Minecraft usernames: SamTheCreeper, DiamondSam, LilSam. It cleans your name to letters, numbers and underscores and builds it into usernames in five styles, all within Minecraft's 16-character limit.

How the Minecraft name maker builds your name

The generator takes your name, removes anything Minecraft won't allow, and adds a Minecraft word before or after it.

  • It cleans your name first. Minecraft usernames only allow letters, numbers and underscores, so spaces and accents are taken out and the result is kept short enough to add a word.
  • Then it adds a Minecraft word before or after it: a mob (PiglinSam, Sam_Wolf), an ore or tool (NetheriteSam), a cool word, a cute word or a joke (NoobSam).
  • Every result fits the 3 to 16 character limit, so you can use it straight away.

Tips for a username based on your name

A username based on your name is easy for friends to remember, but keep it to a first name or nickname.

  • Try a nickname as well as your full name: a short name leaves room for a longer word.
  • Not keen on your real name online? Use a nickname or an initial instead. It's safer, especially for younger players.
  • If it's taken, try the underscore version or add a number.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's my Minecraft name?
Type your name or nickname into the box and pick a style: you'll get a list of usernames built from it, like SamTheCreeper or DiamondSam.
+Should I use my real name in Minecraft?
A first name or nickname is usually fine, but don't add your surname, age or where you live. Younger players are safer with a nickname.
+Why has my name been shortened?
Minecraft usernames can only be 16 characters long, so long names are trimmed to leave room for the Minecraft word.
+How do I make a Minecraft username from my name?
Type your first name or nickname into the box, pick a style (mob, miner, cool, funny or cute) and generate. Every result fits Minecraft's 3 to 16 character rule.
