From menageries to safari parks

  • Menageries came first: collections of animals kept by royalty. The Tower of London kept one for centuries, and its animals went to London Zoo in the 1830s.
  • Zoo is short for zoological garden. London Zoo opened in 1828 as a collection for scientific study, and let the public in from 1847.
  • Safari parks, where you drive through the enclosures, arrived in Britain at Longleat in 1966. Their names often borrow Swahili words: twiga (giraffe), tembo (elephant) and kifaru (rhinoceros).
  • Aquariums and sea centres are named after the coast or a sea creature, and children's farms after something friendly.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good zoo names?
A place and the kind of zoo, like Whitden Zoological Park or Manta Reef Aquarium, or a pun like Hippo Campus Petting Zoo. Choose a style to match your park.
+What should I name my zoo in Planet Zoo?
Name it after the kind of park you're building: a safari park for big open habitats, an aquarium for the coast, or a classic zoo in old gardens. Choose With a description for a star attraction to build.
+What does zoo stand for?
It's short for zoological garden, the name London Zoo used when it opened in 1828.
