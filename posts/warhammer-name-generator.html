In the grim darkness of the far future, a name tells you who someone serves. An Ultramarine sounds like a Roman legionary (Marneus Calgar, Cato Sicarius), a Space Wolf like a saga hero (Logan Grimnar, Ragnar Blackmane), and an Imperial Guard officer comes with a regiment attached. This Warhammer 40k name generator builds names for Space Marines in three Chapter styles, the Imperial Guard and the Aeldari.

Space Marine names by Chapter

Each Space Marine Chapter recruits from its own worlds and keeps its own culture, and names follow:

  • Ultramarines and similar Chapters have Roman-sounding names: Marneus Calgar, Cato Sicarius, Uriel Ventris. A first name and a surname, often with a rank in front.
  • Space Wolves come from Fenris and have Norse names and epithets: Logan Grimnar, Ragnar Blackmane, Bjorn the Fell-Handed.
  • Blood Angels and Dark Angels lean towards angelic and Renaissance Italian names, often used alone: Dante and Mephiston of the Blood Angels, Azrael of the Dark Angels.

Ranks come first: Brother for a battle-brother, then Sergeant, Lieutenant, Captain, or a specialist role like Chaplain, Librarian, Apothecary or Techmarine.

Imperial Guard names

The Imperial Guard, or Astra Militarum, is made of regiments raised from a single world and named after it with a number: the Cadian 8th, the Tanith First and Only from the Gaunt's Ghosts novels. A Guard name is ordinary, a little rough, and nearly always comes with a rank and a regiment.

Famous examples include Lord Castellan Ursarkar E. Creed of Cadia, Commissar Sebastian Yarrick of Armageddon, and Commissar Ciaphas Cain. The generator's regiments come from well-known Guard worlds (Cadian, Catachan, Valhallan, Vostroyan, Mordian, Tallarn) with a random number, so you get a regiment that sounds right without copying a famous one.

Aeldari names

The Aeldari, called Eldar in older editions, live on vast craftworld ships such as Ulthwé, Biel-Tan, Iyanden, Saim-Hann and Alaitoc. Their names are long, flowing and elegant: Eldrad Ulthran, the great Farseer of Ulthwé; Prince Yriel of Iyanden; Illic Nightspear of Alaitoc.

Aeldari follow the Path, devoting themselves to one role at a time, so the With rank and unit format names a role (Farseer, Warlock, Autarch, Exarch, Ranger, Spiritseer) and a craftworld.

Picking a name for your army

  • Name your characters, not every model. A named Captain and Sergeant give an army personality; the rest can be Brother this and Trooper that.
  • Make a successor Chapter. If your Chapter is your own, pick the style of its founding Chapter, then add your own twist.
  • Number your regiment with care. A Guard regiment with a high number suggests a world that has sent many, and lost many.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do Space Marine names work?
A rank, then a name in the style of the Chapter's homeworld: Roman-sounding for the Ultramarines (Marneus Calgar), Norse for the Space Wolves (Ragnar Blackmane), angelic or Italian for the Blood Angels (Dante, Mephiston).
+How are Imperial Guard regiments named?
After the world they were raised on and a number: the Cadian 8th, the Catachan 2nd. The Tanith First and Only from Gaunt's Ghosts is a famous exception.
+What's the difference between Eldar and Aeldari?
The same people. Games Workshop renamed the Eldar as the Aeldari in the eighth edition of Warhammer 40,000.
