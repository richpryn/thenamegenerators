How the Orchestra Name Generator Works

It works from 463 names and name parts, which combine into over 13,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • What are you naming?:
    • Anything, e.g. Skylark Consort
    • Orchestra, e.g. Camerata Silva
    • Ensemble or quartet, e.g. Prairie Ridge String Quartet
    • Concert or album, e.g. Dvořák & Brahms

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Most orchestras are named after their town and their size, from a Sinfonia to a full Philharmonic, while quartets and new-music groups pick a single striking word. This orchestra name generator invents believable places and pairs them with real orchestra types, and it also names ensembles, concert seasons and albums.
