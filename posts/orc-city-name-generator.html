How the Orc Stronghold Name Generator Works

It works from 1,675 names and name parts, which combine into over 38,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, War camp, Mountain hold, Goblin warren, Ruin squat, Horde capital.
  • Name format:
    • Name only, e.g. Wolffire
    • With a description, e.g. Zhargor Rubbleheap, where the ivy and the orcs moved in together

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Good orc stronghold names are short and blunt, or plain descriptions of something ugly: Skargash, Fangfell, Bannerditch. Every orc warband needs somewhere to bang its drums. This orc stronghold name generator gives you orc city, fortress and camp names: war camps, mountain holds, swampy goblin warrens, ruined cities the orcs have moved into and great horde capitals, with a short description if you want one.

How orcs name their strongholds

Orc strongholds in games tend to be named in one of two ways: a hard name in the orcs' own tongue, or a plain description in the common one. Warcraft's orc capital, Orgrimmar, is named after the warchief Orgrim Doomhammer. Skyrim's orc strongholds, like Dushnikh Yal and Largashbur, each have a chief and a code of their own.

The generator has five kinds:

  • War camps are temporary and named for banners and boots: Snarling Boot War Camp.
  • Mountain holds are crags and keeps: The Horns of Kraguth, Crackfell.
  • Goblin warrens are damp and crowded: Grubbog, Mouldmire.
  • Ruin squats are captured towns the orcs have renamed: Scrapgate, Chimneystack.
  • Horde capitals are the big ones: Ghornarr, The Throne of Kragnakh.

Choose With a description for a line that tells you what makes the place memorable.

Using an orc stronghold

  • Ruin squats make good adventures: the old kingdom still claims the place on its maps.
  • A war camp moves, so the players might have to track it.
  • Give the capital one custom: a feast, a wrestling challenge, a drum. It makes the orcs a people, not just enemies.

For the orcs who live there, try the Orc, Skyrim Orc and WoW Orc name generators. For the walls they're attacking, there's the Fortress and Castle name generators, and for the dwarves under the next mountain, the Dwarf City Name Generator.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good orc stronghold names?
Short, hard names like Skargash or Ghornarr, or blunt descriptions like Fangfell, Bannerditch or Split Axe Stockade. Choose a kind of stronghold to match how settled the orcs are.
+What should I call an orc war camp?
After its banners or its warbands, like The Nine Banners or Snarling Boot War Camp. War camps move, so their names are simple and practical.
+Can I use these for goblins too?
Yes. Choose Goblin warren for damp, crowded names like Grubbog and Toadwallow.
