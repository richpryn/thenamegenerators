Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a nonprofit name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Before you commit, search the official registers too: in the UK, Companies House; in the US, your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. In England and Wales, also search the Charity Commission register; in the US, check the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search.
+What makes a good nonprofit name?
Say the cause or the people you help, keep it short enough for a banner and a web address, and make sure it does not sound like a big existing charity, which can confuse donors and break the rules on charity names. A hopeful phrase plus a plain ending (Full Plates Food Project, Bright Futures Trust) works well, and a foundation set up in someone's memory often uses their name.
