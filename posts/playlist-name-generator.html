How the Playlist Name Generator Works

It works from 619 names and name parts, which combine into over 3,300 possible results.

The choices:

  • Mood or use: Any mood, Sad, Happy, Chill, Workout, Study, Road trip, Party, Aesthetic, Sleep, Seasonal, With a name.
  • Your first name: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Sam").

Where the names come from: Every name is invented. Names are built from moods, places, seasons and everyday moments, in the styles people use for their own playlists. Famous song and album titles, and the names of the music services’ own playlists, were left out where we could find them.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A good playlist name tells you when to press play: on the bus home, halfway up a hill or at 2am with the lights off. This playlist name generator makes names for every mood and use, from sad and chill to gym, study, road trip, party, aesthetic, sleep and seasonal playlists, and can put your name on one too.

How playlist names work

Most good playlist names do one of three things. They name a moment (songs for the night bus, Sunday Breakfasts), a use (Leg Day Anthems, Revision Mix), or a feeling (soft and sad, Serotonin Supply). A name with a moment in it is often the strongest, because it tells you when to play it, and that is how most people choose a playlist.

Style matters too. Sad, sleepy and aesthetic playlists are often written all in lowercase (cherry summers, counting sheep), which reads softer and quieter. Gym, party and road trip names usually get capital letters and a bit of a shout: One More Rep, Kitchen Disco Classics, Car Karaoke.

Tips for naming a playlist

  • Avoid famous song titles. A playlist named after one hit makes people expect that song. The generator leaves out the best-known ones.
  • Put a searchable word in public playlists. If you want strangers to find it, include the word they would type: gym, study, road trip, Christmas.
  • Keep private ones personal. If only you will see it, the more specific the better: songs for the last bus home is more yours than Sad Songs.
  • Make a set. Matching names (Sam's Car Songs, Sam's Gym Mix, Sam's Sunday Mix) make a tidy library. Choose With a name and keep the ones you like.
  • Rename it later. Most music apps let you change a playlist name whenever you like, so try one out for a week.

For naming your own music rather than a playlist, try the Song Title Generator or the Album Name Generator.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What is a good name for a playlist?
One that tells you when to play it, like songs for the night bus, Leg Day Anthems or Sunday Breakfasts. Choose a mood or use to match yours.
+What are some aesthetic playlist names?
Soft lowercase words or a "pov" scene: cherry summers, sepia streets or pov: you're walking home in the rain. Choose Aesthetic.
+What should I call a sad playlist?
Name the moment, not just the mood: songs for staring out of bus windows, faded photographs or crying on the bus again. Choose Sad.
+What is a good gym playlist name?
Something with a bit of a shout, like One More Rep, Earned the Biscuit or Deadlift Anthems. Choose Workout.
+What are some good study playlist names?
Library Hours, Music to Finish Your Essay To or Phone in the Other Room. Choose Study for more.
+What should I name a road trip playlist?
Car Karaoke, Are We Nearly There Yet or Windows Down, Volume Up. Choose Road trip.
+Can I make a playlist name with my name in it?
Yes. Type your first name and choose With a name for names like Sam's Car Songs or Sam on Repeat.
