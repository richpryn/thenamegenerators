How the Running Club Name Generator Works

It works from 453 names and name parts, which combine into over 1,500 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style:
    • Any style, e.g. The Tuesday Breakfast Runners
    • Classic, e.g. Hampton Mill Runners
    • Funny, e.g. Slower Than Our Shadows
    • Women and girls, e.g. Mum Miles
    • Coffee crew, e.g. Laps & Coffee
    • Relay & charity, e.g. Fast Lane Flyers
  • Your town or area: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Hampton").

Where the names come from: Every name is invented. Names are built from running words, puns, food and the patterns real clubs use, such as Harriers, Striders and Road Runners. They aren't the names of well-known clubs or brands, but a town name plus Harriers may already belong to a local club, so check before you commit.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Most running clubs are named after their town plus a word like Harriers or Striders, while newer run crews go for something social or silly. This Running Club Name Generator makes both, plus names for women's groups, relay teams and charity runs.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are some funny running team names?
Puns and honest jokes about pace go down well: Wheezy Does It, Calf Measures, The Agony of De-Feet or Will Run for Cake. Slow runners can own it with names like The Plodding Penguins or Back of the Pack. Choose Funny for more.
+What should I call a running group chat on WhatsApp?
Something short that fits the group: Laps & Lattes for a coffee crew, Sole Sisters for a women's group, or your town plus Striders for a club. If you meet at the same parkrun each week, a name like The Saturday Breakfast Runners works well, but keep the word parkrun itself out of the name, as it's a trade mark.
