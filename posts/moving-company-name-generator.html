What makes a removals name work

People hand movers everything they own, so the name has to earn trust:

  • Use the local word. In the UK it's "Removals" or "man and van"; in the US, "Movers" or "Moving & Storage".
  • Say your size. "Man with a Van" sets expectations for small moves; "Removals & Storage" suggests a full service with a warehouse.
  • Keep it legible on a van. Your lorry is your biggest advert, so short names in big letters work hardest.

In the UK, membership of a trade body such as the British Association of Removers reassures customers. In the US, movers who cross state lines need a USDOT number from the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, shown on the vehicle.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Press Check next to any name, or paste it into the Business Name Checker, to see whether the domain and social handles are free. Then search the company register (Companies House in the UK, your state's business register in the US) and the trade mark register for your kind of business. Removals firms often share similar names, so search your area on a map app before you letter the van.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for movers

  • Think about storage. If you'll add storage later, a name like "Removals & Storage" saves a rebrand.
  • Add your area if you work locally: "Hampton Movers" ranks well in local search.
  • Make the phone number big. On a van, the name gets attention and the number gets the job.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good moving company names?
Names that sound careful and reliable, like Royle Removals & Storage or Careful Man with a Van, or a friendly pun such as Lift Off Movers. Use Removals in the UK and Movers in the US.
+How do I check a moving company name is free?
Press Check next to any name to see if it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. Then make sure: in the UK, search Companies House and the UK IPO trade mark register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. A quick map search for the name in your area helps too.
+What's a good name for a moving company?
Movers are trusted with everything a family owns, so names that sound careful and dependable work well: Hartley & Sons Removals, Careful Hands Movers or Steve's Man & Van. A friendly pun like Sofa So Good looks great on the side of a van. In the UK, “Removals” is the usual word; in the US, “Movers” or “Moving & Storage”.
+What's the difference between a removals company and a man and van?
A removals company usually offers a full service with a crew, packing and often storage. A man and van is a smaller, cheaper service for single items or small moves, so name your business for the size of job you'll take.
