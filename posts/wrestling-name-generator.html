How the Wrestling Name Generator Works

It works from 608 names and name parts, which combine into over 68,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, Hero or face, Villain or heel, Funny, Monster, High-flyer, Tag team, Female, From your name.
  • Your first name: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Sam").

Where the names come from: Every name is invented. Names are built from ring nicknames, strong-sounding made-up surnames, posh villain titles, British snacks, wrestling moves and simple Spanish words, in the patterns real ring names use. The ring names, nicknames and tag teams of well-known wrestlers (WWE, AEW, WCW, NJPW, lucha libre and British wrestling) were left out.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A ring name has to work when an announcer bellows it across an arena, and it should tell the crowd whether to cheer or boo. This wrestling name generator makes ring names for heroes and villains, comedy acts, monsters, high-flyers, tag teams and female wrestlers, or builds one from your own first name. It's independent and isn't connected to WWE, AEW or any other promotion, and the names of real wrestlers are left out.

  • The Duchess of Dropkicks: a title for a woman who rules the ring.

How ring names work

In wrestling, the hero is the face (from "babyface") and the villain is the heel. A ring name is the first clue to which is which. Faces get short, bright, friendly names: a nickname and a first name, or "Kid" with a word like Courage or Comet. Heels get titles, sneers and long posh names, because a crowd loves to boo someone who thinks he is better than them.

Most ring names follow a few patterns, and the generator uses them all: nickname and first name (Fearless Leo), first name and strong surname (Ruby Stormfield), title and surname (Baron Fenwick), and the single huge word for a monster (Gravelgut). In Mexican lucha libre, masked luchadores often take a Spanish name for an animal, a star or the weather, which is where El Cometa Azul and La Tormenta Veloz come from.

Tag teams, comedy acts and the British style

  • Tag teams need a name that works for two people at once. "The" with a plural (The Copper Hounds), a shared surname (The Thunderfield Brothers) or a pair of things that belong together (Salt & Vinegar) all work.
  • Comedy wrestlers get their laughs from things that don't belong in a ring: an ordinary job (The Masked Crossword Setter), a snack (Bourbon Biscuit Gary) or a gentle title on a painful move (Grandad Suplex).
  • British wrestling had its own golden age on ITV's World of Sport on Saturday afternoons, with big characters like Big Daddy and Giant Haystacks. Those names and the famous ones from American and Japanese wrestling are all left out of the generator.

Before you use a ring name

The names here are free to use for a character, a video game or a real wrestling career. Big promotions often own the ring names they create, which is why wrestlers sometimes change names when they move on. If you plan to perform, search the name with "wrestler" first, and check social handles and trade marks before printing a T-shirt. For a full stage persona, try the Stage Name Generator, or the Superhero Name Generator for a masked hero.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I come up with a wrestling name?
Decide first whether you are a hero or a villain, then pick a pattern: nickname and first name for a hero (Fearless Leo), a title and surname for a villain (Lord Vexley). Choose Hero or face, or Villain or heel.
+What is a good heel name?
Something posh, smug or sneaky, like Lord Vexley, Smarmy Rupert Vexley or Tarquin the Rule-Breaker. Choose Villain or heel.
+What are some funny wrestling names?
Mix wrestling with everyday life: Grandad Suplex, Custard Cream Barry or The Masked Crossword Setter. Choose Funny.
+What is a good tag team name?
Use "The" and a plural, a shared surname or a famous pair: The Copper Hounds, The Thunderfield Brothers or Salt & Vinegar. Choose Tag team.
+What are some good female wrestler names?
A strong first name and surname, like Ruby Stormfield, or a title such as The Duchess of Dropkicks. Choose Female.
+How do I make a luchador name?
Use El or La with a Spanish word for an animal, a star or the weather, plus an adjective: El Cometa Azul or La Tormenta Veloz. Choose High-flyer.
+Can I make a ring name from my own name?
Yes. Type your first name and choose From your name for ring names like Fearless Sam or Bulletproof Sam Swiftwood.
