Good temple names tell you who is worshipped there and how: a sunlit Sanctuary of Gentle Rain, a druid's Rowanmere Henge, a cult's Veilfane. This temple name generator makes temple names for D&D and fantasy writing in six styles, from sunlit sanctuaries and druid circles to cult fanes and mountain monasteries, and can tell you which invented god is worshipped there.

How temples get their names

Temples have usually been named after the god inside them. The Parthenon in Athens takes its name from Athena Parthenos, "Athena the maiden", and the Romans called a god's temple an aedes, a house. Several old English words for holy places still turn up on maps:

  • Fane, from the Latin fanum, a sacred place. It's the word the generator uses for dark and ancient temples.
  • Minster, from the Latin monasterium by way of Old English: York Minster, Westminster.
  • -church and -kirk, from English and Norse: Ormskirk, Falkirk.
  • Henge, which archaeologists borrowed from Stonehenge itself.

The styles follow the kind of faith: holy abbeys and sanctuaries, dark fanes and undercrofts, druidic groves and stone rings, elemental spires and fonts, ancient ziggurats and pyramids, and eastern pavilions and pagodas. Choose With its god to add who is worshipped, like Hartstone, sacred to a tree goddess whose roots hold up the land.

Using a temple in your world

  • Let the god shape the building. A god of the sea gets a harbour shrine; a god of secrets gets an undercroft.
  • Give the temple a rule. No iron inside, no speaking after dusk: one custom makes it memorable.
  • Ruined temples are adventures. An ancient fane that nobody remembers is a ready-made dungeon.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good temple names for D&D?
Names that tell you about the god: The Abbey of the Hundred Lamps for a god of light, Veilfane for a goddess of secrets, Rowanmere Henge for a nature cult. Choose With its god to add the deity.
+What is a fane?
An old word for a temple, from the Latin fanum, a sacred place. It sounds older and darker than temple, which suits cults and lost faiths.
+Are the gods real?
No. Every god the generator mentions is invented, so you can build your own pantheon around them.
