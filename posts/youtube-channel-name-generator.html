A YouTube channel name has to work as a title, a handle and something viewers can say to a friend. This YouTube channel name generator builds names for five topics in the shapes that work on the platform: a mood and a subject (The Lazy Kitchen), a subject and a format (Pixel Lab), and a few in between.

What makes a channel name work

  • Say what the channel is about, or at least the mood of it. Viewers decide in a second whether to click.
  • Keep it short. Since 2022 every channel has a unique @handle, and shorter handles are easier to type, say and remember.
  • Leave room to grow. A name tied to one game or one gadget can feel narrow in a year. "Retro Arcade TV" can cover more than "Minecraft Builds Daily".
  • Use your own name if the channel is really about you. Many of the biggest channels do.

Check the handle is free

Before you settle on a name, check that the @handle is available on YouTube, and ideally on Instagram and TikTok too, so viewers find you everywhere under the same name. If your first choice is taken, a small change (adding TV, HQ or Daily) often frees it.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I choose a YouTube channel name?
Make it short, easy to say and clear about the topic or mood, then check the @handle is free on YouTube and other social platforms.
+Can two YouTube channels have the same name?
Display names can repeat, but @handles are unique, so check the handle you want is available.
+Can I use these names for my channel?
Yes, free and with no credit needed.
