How the Daedric Weapon Name Generator Works

It works from 532 names and name parts, which combine into over 17,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Name format:
    • Name only, e.g. Sorrex
    • With its weapon type, e.g. Halthal, a Daedric mace
    • With a title, e.g. Vesax, the Unkind Gift

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Daedric weapons are black, red-veined and slightly alive, and their names should sound like a bargain you'll regret. This Daedric weapon name generator makes new names for Elder Scrolls characters, mods and roleplay, with a weapon type or a title, and leaves the real artefacts of the games alone.
