How the Village Name Generator Works

It works from 1,029 names and name parts, which combine into over 27,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, English, Fantasy and D&D, Medieval, Cosy.
  • Name Format:
    • Name only, e.g. Cenricham Regis
    • With a description, e.g. Leofwinford, that owes the castle forty archers in time of war

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Good village names sound as if they've been there for a thousand years, because real ones are built from old words: a farm, a stream or a clearing, with Little, Upper or a saint's name to tell neighbours apart. This village name generator builds names the way real ones grew, from Little X and X-on-the-Hill to -by, -thorpe and -wick, and adds medieval manors, fantasy hamlets for your D&D campaign and cosy villages for your stories and games.

How English villages got their names

Most village names began as plain descriptions in Old English or Old Norse:

  • -ham and -ton are a homestead and a farmstead, -stead a place, and -cote a cottage.
  • -by and -thorpe are Norse farms and villages, and -thwaite a Norse clearing. Grimsby is "Grim's village".
  • Many names start with a person: the medieval style uses real Old English and Norse personal names like Wulfric and Leofwin.

When two places had the same name, an extra word told them apart:

  • Great and Little, Upper and Lower, or Latin Magna and Parva. Much means great.
  • Regis means the land belonged to the king, as in Lyme Regis. Abbas and Abbots mean it belonged to an abbey, as in Milton Abbas and Abbots Langley.
  • A saint's name comes from the parish church. Suffolk has Bradfield St George and Bradfield St Clare.
  • A family name comes from the lord of the manor, as in Stoke Mandeville.

Choose English for villages like these, Medieval for manors with Regis and Abbas, Fantasy and D&D for hamlets like Goblin's Stile, or Cosy for somewhere with a teashop. With a description adds a line of village life.

Village or town?

A village is small: a church, a green, a few farms and a pub. A town has a market and trades. If your settlement has a guild or a city wall, the Fantasy Town Name Generator is a better fit; if it's a capital, try the Fantasy City Name Generator.

  • Name villages in clusters. Neighbouring villages often share a name with a different ending: Upper and Lower, Great and Little.
  • Give each one a quirk, which the description format does for you. Players remember the village with a feud over the best marrow.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good fantasy village names?
Names that sound old and English, like Much Padford or Gamalsthwaite, or a fantasy hamlet named after a landmark, like Three Cairns or Druid's Barrow. Choose a style to match your world.
+What does Regis mean in a place name?
Of the king. It's Latin, and it means the place once belonged to the Crown, as in Lyme Regis and Bognor Regis.
+What's the difference between this and the Fantasy Town Name Generator?
This one is for small places: hamlets, villages and manors. The Fantasy Town Name Generator names larger settlements built around a trade, like mining towns and ports.
