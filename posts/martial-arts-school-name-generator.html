How the Martial Arts School Name Generator Works

It works from 1,153 names and name parts, which combine into over 36,000 possible results.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a martial arts school name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, also search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as another business nearby with a similar name can confuse clients.
+What is a karate school called?
A karate school is called a dojo, a Japanese word meaning "place of the way". A taekwondo school is a dojang (the Korean equivalent) and a kung fu school is a kwoon. Jiu-jitsu and MMA schools usually call themselves an academy, and Muay Thai schools a gym or camp. Many clubs simply use the art and the town ("Hampton Karate Club"), which is also what parents search for.
