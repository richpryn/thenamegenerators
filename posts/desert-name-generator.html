How the Desert Name Generator Works

It works from 1,316 names and name parts, which combine into over 26,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, Fantasy, Sand sea, Salt & badlands, Cursed, Sci-fi planet.
  • Name Format:
    • Name only, e.g. Koroth Major
    • With a description, e.g. the Honey Dunefield, whose ridges are shaped like crescent moons

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Sand, salt and silence can still have a name worth remembering. This desert name generator names deserts for D&D campaigns, novels and sci-fi worlds, from sweeping sand seas and cracked salt flats to cursed wastes and whole desert planets, with a short description if you want one.
