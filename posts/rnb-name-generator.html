How the R&B Name Generator Works

It works from 619 names and name parts, which combine into over 42,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • What are you naming?:
    • Singer, e.g. Lennox Osei
    • R&B group, e.g. Uptown Groove
    • Album, e.g. Overflow
  • Singer: depends on the what are you naming?:
    • Singer: Any, Female, Male
    • R&B group and Album: Any

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

R&B names sound smooth and personal: a real name with a little shine, a single first name, or a vocal group named after a feeling. This R&B name generator makes singer names, group names and album titles in the shapes real R&B acts use.
