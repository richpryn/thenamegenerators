Good R&B names sound smooth and personal: a real name with a little shine, a single first name, or a vocal group named after a feeling. This R&B name generator makes singer names, group names and album titles in the shapes real R&B acts use.

How R&B names work

  • One name. Usher, Brandy and Aaliyah are first names; SZA is a stage name for Solána Rowe. The generator's single names include Dorian and Laila.
  • A real name with a shine. Choose Singer for full names like Ayana Marsh or Jalen Sterling.
  • Groups named after a feeling or a family. R&B has always had vocal groups, from family acts to groups like En Vogue and Boyz II Men. TLC took its name from its members' initials. The generator makes Honeycomb, Mellow Rhythm and The Calloway Brothers.
  • Album titles are personal and a little late-night: Low Light, Basement Sessions, Letters to Whoever Needs It.

Using the names

  • Make it sound like you. R&B is personal, so a name close to your own often works better than a big invention.
  • Check it's free on Spotify, Apple Music and social media before you release anything, and check for trademarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good R&B singer names?
Smooth, personal names: a single first name like Jericho, or a full name with a little shine like Zuri Valentine. Many R&B stars, from Usher to Aaliyah, are known by their first name alone.
+What's a good name for an R&B group?
A name about a feeling or a sound, like Velvet Voices or Mellow Rhythm, or a family name like The Ingram Sisters. Choose R&B group for names built that way.
+Can I use these names?
Yes, they're free to use. Search the name on streaming services first, since single names are often taken.
