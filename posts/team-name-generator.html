How the Team Name Generator Works

It works from 734 names and name parts, which combine into over 3,400 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, Funny, Cool, Work, Kids, Any sport, Football, Netball, Cricket, Fantasy league, Darts and pool, Your town.
  • Your town, a word or a name: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Hampton").

Where the names come from: Every name is invented. Original puns and team names built from sport, office and everyday words. Real clubs, their nicknames, famous players and teams from films and TV were left out, so no name copies a real team.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A team name gets shouted from the touchline, printed on the bibs and read out with the results, so it has to be short and easy to say. This team name generator gives you funny, cool, work and kids' team names, plus names for football, netball, cricket, fantasy leagues and pub darts and pool teams, or names built from your town.

Eight good team names

All eight came from the generator, from eight different styles:

  • Sock Drawer Rovers: a proper football club ending with a joke in front, for a five-a-side team that doesn't take itself too seriously.
  • Midnight Raptors: a time and an animal, the classic pattern for a team that wants to sound fierce.
  • Quarterly Heroes: office language turned into a team name everyone at work will get.
  • Mighty Minnows: small fish, big spirit, and easy for young children to cheer.
  • Ring Leaders: a netball pun on the goal ring, and a promise to lead the league.
  • Rain Stopped Play: the phrase every British cricketer knows too well.
  • Wrong Captain Again: a fantasy football name that every mini-league will recognise.
  • The Red Lion Darts Team: the pub and the game, the way pub league teams have always been named.

What makes a team name work

  • Shout it first. Try "Come on, …!" with the name on the end. If it's too long to shout, it's too long for a team.
  • Two patterns cover most teams. A colour or a mood with an animal (Emerald Dragonflies, Iron Griffins) sounds like a real club. A joke with a club ending (Packed Lunch Wanderers) says you're there for fun. Choose Cool or Any sport for the first and Funny or Football for the second.
  • Match the level. A Sunday league side can laugh at itself; a team chasing a title may want something that looks good on a trophy.
  • Keep it original. Real club names and nicknames, players and teams from films were left out, so no name copies a real team.

Names for leagues, netball, cricket and fantasy football

Most leagues ask for a team name when you sign up, and the name goes on fixture lists and results tables, so check your league's rules before you choose a rude or cheeky one. Junior leagues and school sports often ask for names that are clean and kind.

  • Netball: the puns come from the rules. Players must pass or shoot within three seconds, must keep the foot they land on grounded (lifting it and putting it down again is stepping), and can't throw the ball over a whole third of the court, hence names like Three Second Rule and No Stepping.
  • Cricket: names often borrow the game's language: a sticky wicket is a pitch drying out after rain, and a leg bye is a run scored off the batter's body. Choose Cricket for names ending in CC or XI, as village clubs use.
  • Fantasy football: a name built on one player goes stale when he moves club, so Fantasy league gives names about the game itself: transfers, captains and the group chat.

Picking a name the whole team likes

Generate a list, let everyone pick a favourite and vote on the top three. Check nobody else in your league already uses it, and read it out loud once more before you send it in.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good team names?
Short names that are easy to shout and fit the team: Midnight Raptors, Sock Drawer Rovers, Mighty Minnows or Quarterly Heroes. Choose a style, from funny and cool to work, kids and sports.
+What is a good team name for work?
Something everyone in the office will recognise: Quarterly Heroes, Calendar Invite, Hot Desk Rebels or Thanks for Joining. Choose Work, and keep it friendly, as your manager will hear it.
+What are some funny team names?
Names that laugh at the team before anyone else can: Sorry Ref, Here for the Snacks, Fashionably Late Legends or We Came Second. Choose Funny for more.
+What should I call my five-a-side or Sunday league team?
Put a joke in front of a club ending, like Tea Break Wanderers or Hot Bath FC, or use football language, like Top Bins or Jumpers for Goalposts. Choose Football.
+What are good netball team names?
Puns on the court and the rules work best: Ring Leaders, Net Results, Three Second Rule or Pivot & Pass. Choose Netball.
+What is a good fantasy football team name?
One about the game rather than a player, so it lasts all season: Wrong Captain Again, Kneejerk XI or Mini-League Menace. Choose Fantasy league.
+How do I split people into teams?
Use the Random Team Generator: paste in the names, choose how many teams, and it splits them into even, random teams. Then come back here to name each team.
