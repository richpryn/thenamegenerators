A good clothing brand name is short, easy to say and looks good on a label: a pun, an image, a heritage-style name or your own. This clothing brand name generator gives you names in eight styles, from punny to heritage, and you can check each one is free before you print a single tag.

Picking a style for your label

Clothing names follow the look of the clothes, so choose the style that matches what you'll sell:

  • Streetwear names sound like a club or a crew: Detention Club, Locker 7, Extra Life Supply.
  • Luxury names borrow French and old-English words: Maison (house), Atelier (workshop), House of….
  • Heritage names use Clothiers, Outfitters, Shirtmakers and & Sons, words that suggest a long-established shop.
  • Y2K names look back to the early 2000s: rhinestones, butterflies, logo tees and bubble fonts.
  • Modern names are one or two words, often with Studio or Label.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Woven labels, swing tags, packaging and a logo all carry the name, and each costs money the moment it's printed, so check the name first. Press Check next to any name to open the Business Name Checker, which looks it up in one go:

  • The company register: Companies House in the UK, or your state's business register in the US. A limited company's name can't be the same as one already registered.
  • Trade marks: the UK IPO and USPTO registers. Registering a company doesn't stop someone else owning the name as a trade mark.
  • A domain and social handles, so customers can find you under the same name everywhere.

Clothing is one of the busiest areas for trade marks: it falls in class 25 of the international classification, which covers clothing, footwear and headwear. Search that class in particular before you commit.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, read our guide, Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for a small clothing brand

  • Check the handle first. Most clothing brands live on Instagram and TikTok, so the handle matters as much as the domain.
  • Avoid big brands' words. Names close to a famous label invite a letter from their lawyers, however different your clothes.
  • Say it out loud. If customers can't spell it after hearing it, they won't find you.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good clothing brand names?
Short names that fit the look of the clothes: a pun like Thread Carefully, streetwear like Night Bus Home Club, luxury like Atelier Duval, or a calm modern name like The Overcast Label. Pick a style, then check the name is free.
+How do I check a clothing brand name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it is free as a company name, a web address and a social media handle. In the UK, search the Companies House register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Do this before you pay for a logo, labels or signage.
+How do I find clothing brand names that are not taken?
Short, common words such as Supply or Studio are almost always registered somewhere, so pair a word with something of your own: your town, your name or an unusual image. Then press Check and look at the trade mark registers too, because clothing (class 25) is one of the busiest trade mark classes.
+What's a good name for a streetwear brand?
Something that sounds like a club or a shared moment, like Night Bus Home Club, Detention Club or Locker 7. Choose Streetwear for more names in that style.
