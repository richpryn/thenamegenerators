How the River Name Generator Works

It works from 1,371 names and name parts, which combine into over 36,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, Fantasy, British, Elven, Dark, Magical.
  • Name Format:
    • Name only, e.g. the River Wenlas
    • With a description, e.g. the Ferrin, that sings over the stones loud enough to hear from the road

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Every map needs a river or two, and a good name makes the land around it feel real. This river name generator gives you fantasy rivers for your D&D campaign, old Celtic-sounding British rivers, becks, burns and brooks, flowing elven names, dark and haunted waters and magical streams, with a short description if you want a story to go with one.
