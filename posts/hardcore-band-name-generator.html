How hardcore names work

Hardcore took punk and made it faster and shorter. In Washington, DC, Bad Brains and Minor Threat led the way, and Minor Threat's Ian MacKaye co-founded Dischord Records in 1980. The term "straight edge" comes from a 1981 Minor Threat song. In New York, the Sunday matinees at CBGB were home to bands like Agnostic Front and the Cro-Mags.

The names are about loyalty, strength and keeping your word:

  • One word: Stalwart, Bruise, Grit.
  • Two hard words: Raw Stance, Fixed Line.
  • Mottos and phrases, like Have Heart or Youth of Today: Hold Your Ground, Strength of the Last Train.

Choose Stage name for real names with crew nicknames, like Kev 'Bear' Sullivan, and Album title for records like Out of Excuses or Faith in Each Other.

Using a hardcore name

  • Short is everything. Hardcore names go on flyers, patches and the back of a jacket.
  • One-word names are popular, so they're often taken. Search hard before you commit.
  • Search before you commit. Look the name up on Spotify, Bandcamp and YouTube, and check the social handles are free. Two acts with one name confuse fans and streaming services alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good hardcore band names?
Short, blunt and about loyalty or strength: Iron Truth, Gatebreaker, Clearsight, Heavy Grip, or a motto like Hold Your Ground.
+What does straight edge mean?
A part of hardcore that rejects alcohol, tobacco and drugs. The name comes from "Straight Edge", a 1981 song by the Washington, DC band Minor Threat.
+Are these real hardcore bands?
No, they're new names in the style of the scene. One-word names in particular are often already used by a band somewhere, so search Bandcamp and Spotify first.
