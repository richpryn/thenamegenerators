How the Cleaning Business Name Generator Works

It works from 744 names and name parts, which combine into over 730,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, Catchy and punny, Cute and friendly, Classic, Professional, Modern, short, Luxury, From my town, From my name.
  • Your town: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Hampton").
  • Your first name: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Sarah").
  • Format:
    • Name only, e.g. Lavender & Bloom Cleaners
    • Name with a slogan, e.g. Maid to Order – Fresh homes, happy people

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a cleaning business name is free?
Press Check next to any name to see if it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. Then make sure: in the UK, search Companies House and the UK IPO trade mark register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. A quick map search for the name in your area helps too.
+What's a good slogan for a cleaning business?
Keep it short and promise the result, not the work: We clean, you relax or Your home, spotless. Choose Name with a slogan in the Format menu to see names paired with one.
