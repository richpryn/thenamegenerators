A short history of the snowman

People have been building snowmen for at least 600 years.

  • The oldest picture of a snowman is in the margin of a book of hours from around 1380, now in the Dutch national library in The Hague.
  • The Miracle of 1511: during a bitterly cold winter in Brussels, people filled the city with snowmen, many of them rude jokes about the politics of the day.
  • The usual look (coal for eyes and buttons, a carrot nose, twig arms) is just what was to hand in a garden or a coal shed.

Naming your snowman

The best snowman names are puns, and the worse the pun, the better.

  • Puns: Snowcrates, Brrrnard, Snowtorious.
  • Grand titles: Lord Frostbottom, for the snowman that took all afternoon.
  • Cute names: Marshmallow, Sprinkle, Twinkle, for the little ones.
  • Add a look to describe it on a card or a photo caption: "with a paper crown from a cracker".

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's a funny name for a snowman?
A snow pun: Snowcrates, Brrrnard, Snowtorious or Winterbottom. Choose the puns style.
+When was the first snowman built?
Nobody knows, but the oldest known picture of one is in a book of hours from around 1380.
+What do you need to build a classic snowman?
Wet, packable snow, coal or stones for eyes and buttons, a carrot for the nose, two twigs for arms, and a hat and scarf if you can spare them.
