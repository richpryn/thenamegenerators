  • Buff & Shine: does exactly what it says.
  • Bubblegum Nail Studio: cute and colourful.
  • Lacquer: one sleek word for a modern nail bar.
  • The Digit Room: modern and a little witty.
  • Maison Vernis: luxury French: vernis means varnish, the French word for nail polish.
  • Hampton Pedicure Bar: your town plus a speciality.

How nail salon names work

  • Nail words are made for names: gel, gloss, polish, lacquer, tips, buff, file and coat. Manicure comes from the Latin manus (hand) and pedicure from pes (foot).
  • "Nail bar" suggests walk-in, quick appointments; studio and atelier suggest booked, detailed nail art.
  • Mobile techs and press-on brands often use their own name, because clients follow the artist.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A name from a generator is a starting point, not permission to use it. Before you order the sign, the boxes or the van livery:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; you can't register a limited company with the same name as an existing one. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK trade mark register (or the USPTO in the US) for the goods and services you'll sell.
  • Domain and handles. Check the .co.uk or .com and the Instagram and TikTok handles you'll need.
  • Your area. Search a map app for businesses with the same or a similar name nearby.

Press Check on any name, or use the Business Name Checker, to run most of these at once. For the full process, read our guide, Business Naming Best Practices. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

Making the name work

  • Check the handle. Nail art sells on Instagram and TikTok, so the handle matters as much as the sign.
  • Leave room for toes. If you'll offer pedicures, a name that only mentions hands can feel limiting.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good nail salon names?
Short, fun names that look good on Instagram: witty ones like The Final Coat, cute ones like Bubblegum Nail Studio, or luxury names like Maison Vernis, French for nail varnish.
+How do I check a nail salon name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, also search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as another salon nearby with a similar name can confuse clients.
+Should I name my nail business after myself?
It works well if you're a nail tech building a personal following: "Nails by Sarah" tells clients exactly who they'll see. If you plan to take on staff, open a second salon or sell one day, a name that isn't tied to you, such as "Tips & Toes", is easier to grow and pass on.
+What's the difference between a nail bar and a nail studio?
A nail bar usually suggests quick, walk-in appointments; a studio suggests booked appointments and more detailed nail art. Pick the word that matches how you work.
