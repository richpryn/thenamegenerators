How spa names work

The word itself comes from Spa, a town in Belgium famous for its mineral springs, which gave its name to spa towns everywhere.

  • Classic spa names borrow an old building or a court: Kingsley Court Spa, Old Chapel Day Spa.
  • Luxury names use soft, rich words: Velvet Spa Rooms, Onyx Spa & Wellness.
  • Catchy names are gentle puns: Knot Today Spa Rooms, Rest Assured.
  • Med spa names use skin, laser and clinic: Ellery Skin Clinic. Only use these words if you really offer medical treatments with qualified staff.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Press Check on any name, or paste one into the Business Name Checker, to see whether the domain and social handles are free and to jump to the company register and trade mark searches. Then:

  • Search the company register. In the UK that's Companies House; in the US, your state's business register.
  • Search trade marks with the UK Intellectual Property Office or the USPTO, especially if you'll sell products or open more than one site.
  • Look locally. Search Google Maps for spas near you: a similar name a few streets away will confuse customers even if it's legally fine.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For more on choosing and checking a name, read our guide: Business Naming Best Practices.

Choosing between Spa, Rooms and Wellness

  • Spa suggests a full menu of treatments.
  • Rooms or Treatment Rooms suits a smaller place with one or two therapists.
  • Wellness covers massage, yoga and holistic therapies together.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good spa names?
Calm names that say what kind of spa you are: Room to Breathe Spa & Wellness, The Orangery Health Spa, Satin Spa & Wellness, or Refine Skin & Laser for a med spa.
+What's the difference between a day spa and a med spa?
A day spa offers relaxing and beauty treatments such as massage, facials and body wraps, and you go home the same day. A med spa (medical spa) offers treatments such as injectables, lasers and skin peels, so it needs qualified medical staff, and in the US a supervising doctor under state rules. In the UK the same business is usually called an aesthetics clinic. Only put "med", "medical" or "clinic" in your name if the business really is one.
+How do I check a spa name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, also search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as another business nearby with a similar name can confuse clients.
+Where does the word spa come from?
From Spa, a town in Belgium known for its mineral springs. Its name came to mean any resort or place for health treatments.
