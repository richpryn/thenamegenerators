How the Warrior Cat Name Generator Works

It works from 3,297 names and name parts, which combine into over 550,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Rank: Any rank, Kit, Apprentice, Warrior, Medicine cat, Leader, Whole life.
  • Fur colour: depends on the rank:
    • Any rank, Warrior and Medicine cat: Any colour, Black, White, Grey or silver, Ginger, Brown tabby, Tortie or calico, Golden or cream
    • Kit, Apprentice, Leader and Whole life: Any colour
  • Name format:
    • Either, e.g. Lilyshine
    • Name only, e.g. Patchblaze
    • With a Clan, e.g. Poppypetal of MossClan

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

In the Warriors books every Clan cat has a two-part name: a prefix that stays for life and a suffix that changes as the cat grows up. A kit called Yewkit becomes the apprentice Yewpaw, earns a warrior name like Yewsong and, if they lead their Clan, becomes Yewstar. This warrior cat name generator makes original names in that style for every rank, from a fur colour if you like, and can show a whole life from kit to warrior. It's a fan-made, unofficial tool inspired by Erin Hunter's Warriors books, and it never gives you the name of a cat from the books.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do warrior cat names work?
A warrior name is a prefix plus a suffix, like Fern and song. Kits end in -kit, apprentices in -paw, and leaders swap their suffix for -star. The prefix never changes.
+Can I use these names in my fan fiction?
Yes. Every name here is original and checked against the cats in the books, so use them for your own Clan, story, roleplay or drawings.
