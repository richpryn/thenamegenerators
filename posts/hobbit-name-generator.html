How the Hobbit Name Generator Works

It works from 234 names and name parts, which combine into over 3,500 possible results.

The choices:

  • Gender: Any, Male, Female.
  • Family:
    • Ordinary Shire family, e.g. Topaz Furrowell
    • A Took, e.g. Harebell Took
    • A Brandybuck of Buckland, e.g. Lanadoc Brandybuck

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

Tolkien's hobbits have some of the most comfortable names in fantasy: Bilbo, Frodo and Samwise, Baggins, Took and Gamgee. They follow clear patterns, and Tolkien explained several of them himself. This hobbit name generator builds new names in those patterns: plain Shire names, grand old Took names, Buckland names like Meriadoc's, and the flower and jewel names hobbits gave their daughters.

How hobbit names work

  • Male names are often short and end in -o: Bilbo, Frodo, Drogo, Otho, Lotho. They sound homely and a bit old-fashioned.
  • Female names are often flowers or jewels. In the appendices to The Lord of the Rings, Tolkien notes that hobbit girls were commonly given such names: Belladonna, Lobelia, Primula, Rose, Pearl.
  • Family names are plain English-sounding words, often about the land or the home: Baggins (from Bag End), Proudfoot, Burrows, Hornblower, Brandybuck.

All the names in this generator are new. None belong to a hobbit in Tolkien's books, so your character won't turn up in a family tree.

Tooks and Brandybucks

Two great families break the pattern, and the generator's Family choice follows them:

  • The Tooks were the Shire's grandest and most adventurous family, and they gave their sons grand, old-sounding names: Bandobras, Fortinbras, Isengrim, Ferumbras. The Took option makes a new Took with a name like those.
  • The Brandybucks of Buckland, across the Brandywine river, had names that sound different from the rest of the Shire: Meriadoc, Gorbadoc, Marmadoc, Saradoc, Rorimac. The Brandybuck option makes a new Brandybuck in that -doc and -mac style.

A Took name says your hobbit comes from an old family with a taste for adventure; a Buckland name says they're from the edge of the Shire, by the river and the Old Forest.

Naming your own hobbit

  • Match the family to the character. A cautious, respectable hobbit has a plain Shire name; a Took is expected to wander off.
  • Use a nickname. Hobbits shorten names all the time: Peregrin is Pippin, Meriadoc is Merry, Samwise is Sam.
  • Give the surname a place. Many Shire family names come from where the family lived: a hill, a burrow, a bank by the river.

Frequently Asked Questions

+Why do so many hobbit names end in -o?
It's the commonest pattern for male hobbits in Tolkien's books (Bilbo, Frodo, Drogo, Otho), and gives them a homely, old-fashioned sound.
+How did hobbits name their daughters?
Often after flowers or jewels, as Tolkien notes in the appendices to The Lord of the Rings: Belladonna, Lobelia, Primula, Rose, Pearl.
+Are these names from The Lord of the Rings?
No. They're new names in Tolkien's patterns, chosen so none of them belongs to a hobbit in the books.
