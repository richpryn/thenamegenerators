How the Post-Apocalyptic Settlement Name Generator Works

It works from 1,532 names and name parts, which combine into over 36,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Kind of Settlement: Any kind of settlement, Scrap town, Fortified, Nomad camp, City ruins, Rebuilders.
  • Name Format:
    • Name only, e.g. Bluebird Crossroads
    • With a description, e.g. Chainlink Heights, whose council of elders meets in the old guardhouse

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Good post-apocalyptic town names are named after whatever is left: a car park, a motorway exit, a shopping trolley or a hope. The Post-Apocalyptic Settlement Name Generator names the towns people build after the end of the world: scrap towns, walled compounds, nomad camps, squats in the ruins of old cities and hopeful villages starting again, with a short description if you want one.

All eight came from the generator, across its five styles:

  • Hubcapville: a scrap town named after what it's built from.
  • Kettle Cross, where every roof is made from a different road sign: a scrap settlement with a picture in its description.
  • Chainlink Keep: a fortified town that's mostly fence.

  • Green Shoots: a village of rebuilders, named after a hope.

How survivors name their settlements

After the end of the world, nobody has time for a grand name. Settlements are named after a landmark, a leader or a joke, and the names stick. Fiction has some famous ones: Bartertown in Mad Max Beyond Thunderdome (1985), and Megaton in Fallout 3, a town built around an unexploded bomb.

  • Scrap towns are named after the junk: Toaster Ridge, Jerrycan Flats, Bottlecap Cross.
  • Fortified towns are stockades, forts and keeps: Keogh's Stockade, Barricade Hill.
  • Nomad camps take road names and mile markers: Mile Fifty-Five, Camp Long Road.
  • City ruins keep the old building's name: Museum Hill, Department Store Steps, Arrivals.
  • Rebuilders choose hopeful names: Daybreak Commons, Brighter Days.

Settling the wasteland

  • Name it after what's left. A post-apocalyptic town should tell you what the world used to be.
  • Mix the moods. A map with one hopeful village among the stockades and scrap towns has somewhere worth protecting.
  • Let names be practical. Survivors name places so others can find them: by road, by sign, by landmark.

For the survivors themselves, try the Post-Apocalyptic Name Generator. For cities before the fall, there's the Sci-Fi City and Cyberpunk City name generators.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good post-apocalyptic town names?
Names that come from what's left: Hubcapville, Exit 36, Under the Car Park or Old Planetarium. For a hopeful village, try names like Green Shoots or Daybreak Commons.
+How do survivors name a settlement?
Practically: after a landmark, a road sign, a leader or the junk it's built from. Grand names come later, if at all.
+Can I use these in a game or story?
Yes. They're free to use. Choose With a description for a line about life in each settlement.
