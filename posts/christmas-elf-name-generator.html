Christmas elves in folklore

Santa's busy workshop elves are a fairly modern idea, but they grew out of much older household spirits.

  • The Scandinavian nisse or tomte is a small farm spirit who looks after the house and animals. On Christmas Eve families leave him a bowl of rice porridge with butter; forget, and he plays tricks.
  • Iceland's Yule Lads are thirteen mischievous brothers who visit one by one in the thirteen nights before Christmas, each with his own habit, like Spoon-Licker and Door-Slammer.
