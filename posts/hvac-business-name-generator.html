HVAC, heating engineer or air conditioning?

HVAC stands for heating, ventilation and air conditioning. It's the usual name for the trade in the US, where many homes have central air and a furnace. In the UK, most homes have a boiler and radiators, so customers search for a heating engineer, boiler repair or air conditioning installer, and those words belong in the name. The generator's local and classic styles use the UK terms.

  • Gas work: in the UK, anyone working on gas boilers and appliances must be on the Gas Safe Register.
  • Air conditioning and heat pumps: working on the refrigerants inside them needs F-gas certification.
  • Heat pump grants: customers using the Boiler Upgrade Scheme in England and Wales need an MCS-certified installer, so Heat Pumps in the name only helps if you're certified.

Check it's free before you print the sign

An HVAC name ends up on a van, a website and a Google Business Profile, so it's worth a few minutes' checking before anything is printed:

  • Press Check on any name above. The Business Name Checker looks up the domain, links to the company register (Companies House in the UK) and opens the trade mark search and social handles.
  • Search where you'll trade. Look the name up on maps, marketplaces and social media. Two businesses with the same name confuse customers and reviews.
  • Show your Gas Safe, F-gas or MCS credentials beside the name, not in it, so the name still works if your services change.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, from shortlisting to registering, read our guide to Business Naming Best Practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good HVAC company names?
Names that mention comfort or temperature and the trade, like Degrees of Comfort Heating & Air, Cooler Heads Heating & Air or Evergreen Heat Pump Solutions. In the UK, Heating Engineers or Heating & Air Conditioning reads more naturally than HVAC.
+What are some catchy HVAC company names?
Catchy HVAC names play on comfort and temperature: Keep Your Cool HVAC, Degrees of Comfort, Goldilocks Heating & Air or Pilot Light Heating & Cooling. In the UK, “Heating & Air Conditioning” or “Heating Engineers” reads more naturally than HVAC. Choose Catchy in the Style menu for more.
+How do I check an HVAC business name is free?
Press Check next to any name to see if it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. Then make sure: in the UK, search Companies House and the UK IPO trade mark register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. A quick map search for the name in your area helps too.
+Do I need to be registered to run a heating business in the UK?
For gas work, yes: you must be on the Gas Safe Register. Working with air conditioning or heat pump refrigerants needs F-gas certification, and heat pump installers need MCS certification for customers to claim the Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant.
