Where the names come from: Every name is invented. Names are built from sweets, party words, silly compound surnames, old-fashioned first names and simple French words, in the patterns real clowns use. Clown names are meant to be said with a grin: sweets, squeaks, old-fashioned titles and surnames that go on a bit too long. This clown name generator makes names for the circus ring, children's parties, silly characters, mild Halloween scares and French-style mimes, and can turn your own first name into a clown name too.

  • Professor Twinkles: a grand title on a silly name is one of the oldest clown jokes.
  • Polly Popcorn: easy for small children to shout at a birthday party.
  • Auntie Bunting: friendly and festive, for a party clown who brings the decorations.
  • Gladys Biscuitwellies: an old-fashioned first name and a ridiculous surname.
  • Nigel Jellyboots: silly enough for a slapstick act with a lot of falling over.
  • The Music Box Clown: spooky for a Halloween party without being gory.
  • Madame Grenouille: a mime name that means Madam Frog in French.

How clown names work

Circus clowns traditionally come in three types. The whiteface wears a fully white face and is usually the clever one in charge. The auguste has a pink or red face, a big nose and baggy clothes, and is the one who gets the custard pie. The character clown plays a type, such as a tramp, a policeman or a fussy waiter. Names follow the type: neat and dainty for a whiteface (Pipkin the Clown, Jolly Tiptop), messy and loud for an auguste (Nigel Jellyboots).

Most clown names use one of a few patterns, and the generator follows them: one bouncy word and "the Clown" (Pipkin the Clown), a title on a silly word (Auntie Confetti, Professor Gumdrop), or an ordinary first name with a made-up surname (Dizzy Wobblesocks). The English clown Joseph Grimaldi (1778–1837) was so famous that clowns are still called "Joeys" after him.

Party clowns, scary clowns and mimes

  • Children's parties: choose a name a four-year-old can say and remember. Sweets, balloons and party words work well, and a title like Auntie or Uncle makes the clown feel friendly rather than strange.
  • Halloween: plenty of people find clowns a little unsettling, and that does the work for you. The Scary style keeps to creaks, whispers, music boxes and old carousels, so the names suit a family Halloween party.
  • Mimes: mime grew out of French theatre, from the sad white-faced Pierrot to Marcel Marceau in the 20th century, so a French title and one simple French word fits: Monsieur Pomme is Mr Apple.

Using a clown name for work

If you perform as a clown, your name and face are your brand. Since the 1940s, members of Clowns International have recorded their make-up on painted eggs in the Clown Egg Register, so that no two clowns copy each other. Before you print business cards, search for the name with "clown" and your area to make sure nobody local is already using it. For a circus or a whole act, try the Stage Name Generator too.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What is a good clown name?
Something bouncy and easy to say, like Pipkin the Clown, Professor Twinkles or Polly Popcorn. Choose a style to match your act.
+What are some funny clown names?
An old-fashioned first name with a silly surname is hard to beat: Gladys Biscuitwellies or Nigel Jellyboots. Choose Silly for more.
+What is a good name for a kids' party clown?
Something friendly and sweet, like Auntie Confetti, Polly Popcorn or Granny Trifle. Choose Kids' party.
+What are some scary clown names for Halloween?
Spooky but not gory: Mister Creak, Old Tatters or The Music Box Clown. Choose Scary but mild.
+What is a good mime name?
A French title and one French word, like Monsieur Pomme or Madame Grenouille, or a quiet French first name such as Hushed Solange. Choose Mime.
+How do I make a clown name from my name?
Type your first name into the box and choose From a name. Sam becomes Sam Sprinkles, Sam Wigglesocks or Captain Sam Dimples.
+Are famous clowns included?
No. Famous clowns, real and fictional, are left out, so every name here is new.
