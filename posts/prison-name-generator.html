How the Prison Name Generator Works

It works from 786 names and name parts, which combine into over 1,800,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Name format:

    • With a description, e.g. The Forgotten Tolbooth of Fellwick, built on an island in a lake that freezes solid every winter

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A good prison name tells prisoners what they're in for: a rock, a gaol or an oubliette in fantasy (Bitterholt Rock), a code and a number in sci-fi (Facility Beta-113), a seal or a cage for monsters. Every villain needs somewhere to be locked up, and every hero needs somewhere to break out of. This prison name generator makes names for fantasy dungeons and D&D gaols, sci-fi prison planets and stations, magical prisons built to hold monsters, and funny lock-ups, with an optional one-line description.

How prisons get their names

Real prisons were usually named after where they stood. London's Newgate prison took its name from Newgate, one of the gates in the old city wall, and the Tower of London held prisoners for centuries. Alcatraz in San Francisco Bay was named by Spanish explorers after the seabirds on the island. In Scotland a tolbooth was a town hall that also served as the jail, and in the 18th and 19th centuries Britain kept prisoners on hulks, old ships moored in rivers and harbours.

  • Fantasy prisons are gaols, rocks, oubliettes and hulks: The Sunless Lockhouse, The Drowned Tolbooth of Drearwick.
  • Sci-fi prisons sound official: Correctional Orbital Drevan, Holding Station Zaltor.
  • Monster prisons are seals, circles and vaults: The Salt Circle, The Riddle-Locked Cage.
  • Funny prisons are for goblins and pixies: Wobblecombe Secure Unit for Cheeky Pixies.

Using a prison in your game

  • Give it one weakness and hide it in the description.
  • Name the warden as well. A prison with a person in charge feels more real.
  • Decide who it holds: debtors, rebels, monsters or someone the party needs to free.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good names for a fantasy prison?
Names that sound cold and permanent: Bitterholt Rock, The Leaden Oubliette, or The Sunless Lockhouse. For a sci-fi prison, try Penal Asteroid Sereth or Correctional Orbital Drevan.
+What is an oubliette?
A dungeon cell reached only by a trapdoor in the ceiling. The name comes from the French oublier, to forget.
+Can I get a prison for monsters?
Yes. Choose For monsters for seals, cages and vaults, like The Seven-Locked Wraith Seal or The Salt Circle.
