How insurance agencies name themselves

Insurance is sold on trust, so most agencies use a surname, the town, or an image of shelter: roofs, umbrellas, keys and steady ground. Funny names work for a friendly local broker, as long as the joke is about protection rather than disaster.

Words carry rules here. In the UK, selling or arranging insurance is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, and Insurance and Assurance are sensitive words that need the FCA's view before Companies House accepts them in a company name. In the US, agents are licensed by each state, and some states have rules about agency names. Use Brokers only if you act for your customers, and stay well away from any big insurer's name or slogan.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Every name here has a Check button, which opens the Business Name Checker: it looks up domains, social handles, the company register and trade mark searches in one place. Before you order signs or cards:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; a limited company's name can't be the same as one already registered. In the US, search your state's business register. Insurance and Assurance are sensitive words that need the FCA's view first.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK Intellectual Property Office or the USPTO for marks in your line of work. Registering a company doesn't give you trade mark rights.
  • Domain and handles. Try the .co.uk or .com and the main social handles at the same time, and adjust the name rather than settle for a messy handle.
  • Local search. Look the name up with your town, as two similar firms a few miles apart confuse clients and reviews.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good insurance agency names?
Steady, reassuring names: a surname like Westbrook Insurance Partners, an image like Under One Roof Insurance Agency, or a friendly line like Policy in Plain English.
+What should I name my insurance agency?
Trust matters most, so many agents use their own surname ("Pemberton Insurance Agency") or their town; a friendly line ("Rain or Shine Insurance") stands out on a busy street. Steer clear of anything close to a big insurer's name or slogan. In the UK, "insurance" is a sensitive word in a company name, so you'll need the FCA's view before Companies House accepts it; in the US, check your state's rules for agency names.
+Can I use Insurance in a UK company name?
Yes, but it's a sensitive word, so Companies House needs the Financial Conduct Authority's view before it accepts it. You'll also need FCA authorisation to sell insurance.
+How do I check an insurance agency name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as two similar names nearby confuse customers.
