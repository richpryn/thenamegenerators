A good kids' clothing brand name is soft, easy for parents to say and spell, and hints at little ones: a small animal, a playful pun or a natural fabric. Starting a baby clothing line, an organic babywear label or a children's clothes shop? This kids clothing brand name generator gives you names in seven styles, and you can check each one is free before you order labels.

Eight good kids' clothing brand names

All eight came from the generator:

  • Scamps & Jumpers: a gentle pun that sounds like a busy playground.
  • Wellies & Wonders: for outdoor clothes and puddle weather.
  • Sleepy Moon Kids: cute and calm, good for sleepwear.
  • Honey Gosling Babywear: a baby animal and a warm colour.
  • Recess Kids: short and modern, with a school-days feel.
  • Hallam Heirlooms: a classic name for clothes that get handed down.
  • Heather & Flax Baby: natural fibres for an organic range.
  • Hampton Cub Baby Co.: a local name with a small animal.

What makes a children's clothing name work

  • Parents buy, children wear. The name has to reassure an adult while still feeling playful.
  • Little words: Little, Wee, Tiny, Minis, Cub, Nipper and Tiddler all say small.
  • Fabric words such as Linen, Flax, Wool and Cotton suit organic and natural ranges.
  • Heirloom names sound like a family firm, for classic smocking and knitwear: Ashby & Little.
  • Your child's name is a common choice, and the With my name style builds around a first name.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Before you pay for a logo, labels or a stall banner, press Check on the name you like, or paste it into the Business Name Checker. It looks for:

  • Company names: in the UK, Companies House won't register a limited company name that's the same as one already on the register. In the US, check your state's business register.
  • Trade marks: search the UK Intellectual Property Office and, if you sell abroad, the USPTO. A registered company name doesn't give you trade mark rights.
  • Web and social: the .com or .co.uk, plus the Instagram, TikTok and Etsy handles parents customers will look for.

Children's clothing also has safety rules. In the UK, children's nightwear must meet flammability requirements and carry the right labels, so if you'll sell pyjamas or dressing gowns, check those rules before your first order. Avoid characters and big labels in the name.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full checklist, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for a kids' brand

  • Think about age range. Baby in the name can feel wrong once you sell clothes for eight-year-olds.
  • Say it out loud to a parent. If they have to ask how it's spelled, it'll be hard to find online.
  • Pick a name that suits gifts. A lot of babywear is bought as presents.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good names for a kids clothing brand?
Soft, playful names parents can spell: Scamps & Jumpers, Sleepy Moon Kids, Heather & Flax Baby for organic clothes, or Hallam Heirlooms for classic pieces.
+How do I check a kids clothing brand name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it is free as a company name, a web address and a social media handle. In the UK, search the Companies House register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Do this before you pay for a logo, labels or signage.
+What is a good name for a kids clothing brand?
One that parents can say and spell after hearing it once, and that hints at little ones: a small animal (Little Acorn Babywear), a playful pun (Onesie Upon a Time) or a soft, natural pair for organic ranges (Oat & Linen Baby). Many founders name the brand after their child. Avoid characters and big labels such as Peppa, Bluey or Mini Boden.
+Should a baby clothing brand say "baby" in the name?
Only if you'll stay with babywear. If you plan to sell clothes for older children too, a word like Kids, Minis or Little leaves you room to grow.
