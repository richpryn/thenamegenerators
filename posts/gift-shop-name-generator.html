What makes a gift shop name work

  • Warm words: happy, little, cosy, golden, ribbon, parcel. A gift shop sells a feeling as much as things.
  • Say cards if you sell them. "Cards & Gifts" is a classic high-street pairing because people look for a card first.
  • Gift boxes and hampers sell online, so the name needs to work as a web address: Teatime Boxes, Box of Good Things Co.
  • Local names suit tourist towns: visitors like a shop that's clearly from where they are.

Check it's free before you order the bags

Before you pay for a sign, a logo or printed bags, check the name is free:

  • Company register: if you form a limited company in the UK, Companies House won't accept a name that's the same as one already registered. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks: search the UK IPO or USPTO register for your kind of goods, so you don't clash with an existing brand.
  • Web and social: check the domain and the handles you'll use, since many customers will find you on Instagram first.

Press Check on any name, or use the Business Name Checker, to run those searches in one place. The Business Naming Best Practices guide covers the rest. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

Tips for a gift shop

  • Keep it short enough for a paper bag and a gift tag.
  • Avoid names close to big card and gift chains.
  • Seasonal? Pick a name that still works in July as well as December.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are some cute gift shop names?
Soft, cheerful names such as Cheery Owl Gift Shop, Honey Puffin Gift Shop, Tied with a Bow or Sunny Lemon Gift Co. Choose Cute for more.
+How do I check a gift shop name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it is free as a company name, a web address and a social media handle. In the UK, search the Companies House register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Do this before you pay for a logo, labels or signage.
+What is a good name for a gift shop?
One that feels warm, says gifts or hints at them, and is easy to remember when someone needs a present in a hurry. A pun such as Many Happy Returns suits a fun card shop, a classic name such as Langford Cards & Gifts suits the high street, and a gift box business often uses Box Co. or Hampers. Avoid names close to big chains such as Clintons, Card Factory or Paperchase.
+What should I call a gift box business?
Say box, hamper or parcel so people know what you sell: Box of Good Things Co., Teatime Boxes or Boxes & Bows. Choose Gift boxes and hampers for names like these.
