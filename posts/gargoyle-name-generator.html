How the Gargoyle Name Generator Works

It works from 151 names and name parts, which combine into over 4,400 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any, Gothic, Cathedral guardian, Cute.
  • Name format:
    • Name only, e.g. Sister Theodosia
    • With a title, e.g. Guttergrin of the Long Night

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

A gargoyle is, strictly, a carved waterspout: the word comes from the French for throat, and rain pours out of its mouth. Over centuries they became the stone guardians of cathedrals. This gargoyle name generator makes gothic names like Grimspout, cathedral guardians named for their tower, and cute names for a garden gargoyle.
