What makes a name sound cold

Cold names lean on a few sounds. Hissing s and sh sounds suggest wind and snow; hard k and sharp v sounds suggest ice cracking. Long, open vowels (Isvara, Nevuna) feel still and quiet, like a frozen lake. Most of the names here use one or two of those, which is why they sound wintry even though they're invented.

Titles do the rest. A cold place (the Frozen Spire, the Glass Peaks) or a cold word (Frostbound, Rimeskull) turns a plain fantasy name into an ice mage's name, so if you like a first name from another generator, try adding a title from this one.

Names for each kind

  • Ice mages: short, cool names that would suit any mage, made wintry by a title.
  • Frost elves: flowing elvish sounds with family names like Snowsong or Coldthorn.
  • Frost giants: heavy Norse-sounding names. In Norse myth the frost giants were the hrímþursar, "rime giants", so rime and frost suit their titles.
  • Ice dragons: longer, rolling names with y and v sounds, and titles that warn people off.
  • Winter witches: an old-fashioned first name and a wintry surname, for a witch who could live at the edge of an English village.

Using an ice mage name in a game

The names work for a frost mage in an online game, an ice sorcerer or wizard in a tabletop campaign, or a character in a story. If you're naming a game character, try the name without the title first, as many games limit name length. For a campaign, the title can be something the character earns, so the name can grow with them.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What is a good name for an ice mage?
Look for crisp sounds, such as s, v, k and long vowels, and a title that ties the mage to a cold place: Isvara of the White Ash or Kalden the Frostbound. Names like these suit a frost mage in World of Warcraft, an ice sorcerer in D&D or a winter wizard in a story.
+What are some names for frost giants?
Frost giants in Norse myth and in D&D have heavy, Old Norse-sounding names. This generator makes new ones in that style, such as Hjalmund the Rimebeard or Skalhild, Jarl of the Bleak Fells, rather than reusing the giants of the myths.
+What is a good name for an ice dragon?
A long, rolling name with a title: Vyrnoth, Rhimyss the Snowfang or Yskynn, Terror of the Ice Fields. Choose Ice dragon for more.
+What is a good female ice mage name?
Soft vowels and a cold title work well: Isvara, Astalie of the Silent Gate or Ilvuna. Choose Female and With a title for names like these.
+Are these names from games or books?
No. They're original names, made from cold sounds and wintry words, so they won't belong to a character from a game, book or myth.
