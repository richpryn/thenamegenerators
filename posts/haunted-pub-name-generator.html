Every old inn worth its sign has a ghost story: a coachman who still waits in the yard, a fiddler heard after closing time. This haunted pub name generator makes spooky, old-English pub names in classic, gothic and comic styles, and can give each one its own resident ghost.

  • The Sleepless Smuggler: haunted by a smuggler who hides lace and tea under the floorboards.
  • The Watchman's Rest: for a ghost who never finished his shift.

Why so many pubs have a ghost

Old inns had everything a ghost story needs: centuries of travellers, coaching yards, cellars and, on the coast, smugglers. Jamaica Inn on Bodmin Moor gave its name to Daphne du Maurier's 1936 smuggling novel and has had a ghostly reputation ever since.

  • Classic names follow real pub patterns, with a figure from the inn's past: a pedlar, a landlady, a fiddler.
  • Gothic names use the old "A and B" sign shape with darker things: The Rook and Coffin.
  • Comic names are ghost puns for a Halloween party: The Overworked Spectre.

Choose With its ghost and every pub comes with a resident spirit and the odd thing it does.

Using a haunted pub

  • In a story or a game, the ghost is a ready-made rumour: the barkeep can tell it, and the players can investigate.
  • For a Halloween party, name the room or the bar after one and print a sign.
  • For a real pub or event, search the name first, as many classic pub names are already in use.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good haunted pub names?
Old-English pub names with a ghost in them: The Huntsman's Lament, The Raven and Scythe, The Sleepless Smuggler. For a party, comic names like The Spook and Umbrella.
+Why are so many old pubs said to be haunted?
They're old, busy places full of travellers' stories, often with cellars, coaching yards or smuggling history, which is just what a ghost story needs.
+Can I use these names for a D&D tavern?
Yes. Choose With its ghost and you get a rumour for your players at the same time.
