What makes food kawaii

Kawaii is the Japanese word for cute, and Japan has been giving food faces for decades. Sanrio, the company behind Hello Kitty, created Gudetama, a lazy egg yolk, in 2013, and he became one of its best-known characters.

  • Japanese endings: -chan is an affectionate ending used for children and friends, and -kun is often used for boys, so Lassi-chan and Pretzel-chan sound friendly straight away.
  • Japanese treats: mochi is a soft cake made from pounded sticky rice, and taiyaki is a fish-shaped cake usually filled with sweet red bean paste.
  • Doubling and diminutives: Bubble Bubble, Daisy Kiwikins, Otto Peachling. Repeating a sound or adding -kins or -ling makes a name smaller and sweeter.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What does kawaii mean?
Cute, in Japanese. Kawaii culture gives faces and personalities to everything, including food.
+What are cute names for food characters?
The food plus something soft or sweet, like Mochi-Mochi, Cocoa Cloud, Strawbelle or Pom-Pom Teaspoon. Choose a kind of food to get names to match.
+Can I use these names for my drawings or plushies?
Yes, they're free to use. If you're selling them, search the name first to check nobody else is using it for a similar product.
