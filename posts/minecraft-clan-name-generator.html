Minecraft faction, clan and team names

Minecraft clan names change with the group: factions sound like orders, PvP clans sound fierce, and friend groups sound like friends.

  • Factions: on factions servers, groups claim land, build bases and raid each other, so faction names sound like an order or a pact: the Obsidian Pact, the Void Order.
  • PvP clans: names that look good next to a kill feed: Void Reapers, Wither Titans.
  • Teams: for Bedwars, Skywars and server events, short and loud.
  • Friends: for your group on an SMP, something you'd actually say out loud: the Redstone Rascals, the Dirt Hut Gang.

How to choose a Minecraft clan name

A good Minecraft clan name is short enough to abbreviate into a clan tag and fits the server's rules.

  • Make it short enough for a tag. Many servers show a clan tag of a few letters next to your name, so pick something with a good abbreviation.
  • Check the server's rules before you pick something edgy: most servers ban offensive clan names.
  • Match your members' names for bonus points.
