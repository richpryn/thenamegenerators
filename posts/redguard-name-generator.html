How the Redguard Name Generator Works

It works from 590 names and name parts, which combine into over 1,600,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Gender: Any, Male, Female.
  • Name format:
    • Any, e.g. Ikkadi
    • Single name, e.g. Nadhir
    • Family name, e.g. Sukkadar at-Jalim
    • Epithet, e.g. Urradu the Wise
  • Starts with: Any letter, A, B, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O, Q, R, S, T, U, Y, Z.

Where the names come from: Some names are real and some are invented. First names are a mix of new names built from the sounds of the Redguard names in the games and real Arabic and Persian given names that fit alongside them, leaving out the names of religious figures. Family names are new, and the homelands after al- are cities of Hammerfell. Every name is checked against the characters on the Elder Scrolls wiki, so none belongs to a Redguard from the games.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Most Redguards in Skyrim go by a single name: Nazeem, who never lets you forget he's been to the Cloud District; Saadia at the Bannered Mare; Kematu, who comes looking for her. This Redguard name generator makes new names in that style, and the family names with al-, at- and af- you meet in Elder Scrolls Online, for Skyrim, ESO or your own story. It's a fan-made, unofficial tool, not connected to Bethesda Softworks or ZeniMax, and it never gives you the name of a character from the games.

How Redguard names work

Redguard names in the games come in three shapes:

  • One name. This is the usual Skyrim pattern: Nazeem and Amren in Whiterun, Saadia and Kematu, whose feud you can settle either way.
  • A family name with a particle. Elder Scrolls Online gives many Redguards a family name joined by a short lowercase particle: Kamiran at-Kalan in Sentinel, Khosren al-Bergama. Skyrim has one too, in Lu'ah Al-Skaven. Lore writers and fans read al- as 'from', followed by a home city, so Khosren al-Bergama is Khosren from Bergama. What at- and af- mean exactly isn't settled, but both tie a person to their family.
  • An epithet. A few Redguards are known by a description: Majid the Mad and Rochelle the Red in Skyrim.

The generator's Family name format follows the ESO pattern: at- and af- come before a new family name, and al- before a real city of Hammerfell such as Taneth, Rihad or Gilane.

Eight good Redguard names

  • Hadira: a single name with the soft, open sound of Saadia.
  • Korrazim: double consonants and a strong ending, for a warrior.
  • Basim al-Taneth: from Taneth, a city in the south of Hammerfell.
  • Mehrnaz at-Suhrad: a family name for a merchant house.
  • Fazrim al-Elinhir: from Elinhir, the city in Craglorn.
  • Kirzan the Hawk: an epithet for a scout or an archer.
  • Makali the Sword-Dancer: Redguards are famous as Tamriel's most gifted warriors, and some of them know it.
  • Zabbadar the Stargazer: a navigator, or a dreamer.

Yokuda, Hammerfell and the sword

The Redguards' ancestors came to Tamriel from Yokuda, a lost continent to the west, and took Hammerfell for themselves. Their warrior culture came with them, including the sword-singers of old Yokuda, and it's why Redguards in every game are known for blades.

Hammerfell's politics give a character a ready-made background. The Crowns are traditionalists who trace their line to Yokuda's nobility; the Forebears descend from the warriors who first conquered the land and are readier to deal with the Empire. A Crown might insist on a family name; a Forebear sailor might never use one.

Many of the names in the games sound Arabic or Persian, and so do the names here. Redguards are a fictional people, though, so this page doesn't give the names meanings: very little of the Yokudan language is known, and the names are made for Tamriel, not translated.

Frequently Asked Questions

+Do Redguards have last names?
In Skyrim most go by one name, like Nazeem and Saadia. In Elder Scrolls Online many have a family name with al-, at- or af-; choose Family name for one, or Single name for the Skyrim style.
+What does al- mean in Redguard names?
It's generally read as 'from', followed by a place, so Basim al-Taneth is Basim from Taneth. The meanings of at- and af- are less certain, but both link a person to their family.
+What are good female Redguard names?
Set the gender to Female for names like Hadira and Mehrnaz. Add Epithet for something like Makali the Sword-Dancer.
+Are Redguard names Arabic?
Many sound Arabic or Persian, in the games and here, and some first names here are real Arabic and Persian names. Redguards are a fictional people, so the names aren't given meanings.
+How do I give a Redguard an epithet?
Choose Epithet for a name with a title, like Kirzan the Hawk or a name ending of the Sands. It suits a sellsword or a sailor known up and down the coast.
+Can I use these names in Elder Scrolls Online?
Yes. ESO names must be unique on your server, and a family name with al-, at- or af- makes it much easier to find one that's free.
+Is this an official Elder Scrolls tool?
No. It's a fan-made, unofficial generator, not connected to Bethesda Softworks or ZeniMax. The names are new, and free to use for your characters.
