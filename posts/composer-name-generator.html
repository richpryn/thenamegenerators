How the Composer Name Generator Works

It works from 791 names and name parts, which combine into over 5,600,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • What are you naming?:
    • Anything, e.g. Matthew J. Sutton
    • Full name, e.g. Jana K. Kraus
    • Alias or mononym, e.g. Midnight Arcade 1983
    • Studio, e.g. Oakley Music
  • Gender (full names): depends on the what are you naming?:
    • Anything and Full name: Any, Female, Male
    • Alias or mononym and Studio: Any

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Most screen composers credit their own name, but plenty release library, ambient or game music under an alias, and nearly everyone needs a name for their studio. This composer name generator gives you all three: credible professional names, short aliases for side projects, and production company names that look right on a cue sheet.
