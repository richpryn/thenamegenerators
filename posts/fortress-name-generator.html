How the Fortress Name Generator Works

It works from 1,140 names and name parts, which combine into over 170,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style of fortress: Any style, Fantasy, Dwarven, Orcish, Dark, Frontier, Sci-fi.
  • Name format:
    • Name only, e.g. Griffingate
    • With a line of history, e.g. Firebase Mantis, held for a decade against an alien swarm

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A castle is a home with walls, but a fortress exists to hold the line. This fortress name generator builds names for heroic bastions, dwarven fastnesses, orc strongholds, dark citadels, frontier forts and sci-fi fortress worlds, with an optional line of history for your campaign.
