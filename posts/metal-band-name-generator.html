How the Metal Band Name Generator Works

It works from 1,487 names and name parts, which combine into over 150,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Metal style:
    • Any style, e.g. Crowns of Nattmyr
    • Black metal, e.g. Starless Mountain
    • Death metal, e.g. Pit of Decay
    • Doom metal, e.g. Catafalque
    • Thrash metal, e.g. Meltdown
    • Power metal, e.g. Radiant Knights
    • Metalcore, e.g. As the Ocean Fades
  • What are you naming?: Band name, Stage name, Album title.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Every metal style names itself differently: black metal wants frost and forests, death metal wants the crypt, power metal wants dragons and glory. This metal band name generator gives you band names, stage names and album titles for black, death, doom, thrash and power metal and metalcore, following the patterns real bands use without borrowing their names.
