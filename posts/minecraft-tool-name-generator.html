How to rename an item in Minecraft

To rename an item in Minecraft, put it in an anvil, type the new name and take it out.

  1. Put the tool or weapon in the first slot of an anvil.
  2. Type the new name in the box at the top. Names can be up to 50 characters.
  3. Take it out. Renaming an item that hasn't been on an anvil before costs one level of experience; more if it has.

A custom name shows in italics and stays with the item, even if you enchant or repair it later. It's the best way to tell your Fortune III pickaxe from your Silk Touch one at a glance.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do you rename an item in Minecraft?
Put it in an anvil, type the new name and take it out. It costs one level of experience for an item that hasn't been on an anvil before, and names can be up to 50 characters.
+What's a good name for a Minecraft pickaxe?
Choose Pickaxe: epic names like Skybreaker, or funny ones like Gravel Regrets. Add an enchantment for something like Deepcutter (Fortune III).
+What's a good name for a trident?
Something stormy or salty: choose Trident for names like Saltfury, and add Channeling or Riptide.
+What are good Minecraft bow names?
Choose Bow for epic names like Starflight or funny ones like Missed Again, and add Power or Infinity to show the enchantment.
