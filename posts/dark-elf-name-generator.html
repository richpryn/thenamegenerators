Dark elf names are sharp and sibilant, full of z, x and ph, and they rarely stand alone: a drow is their house before they are anything else. This dark elf name generator gives you drow first names, male and female, with a noble house and a title to match, from Weapons Master to Matron Mother.

Who the dark elves are

  • In D&D, dark elves are the drow, who live in the Underdark and worship the spider goddess Lolth. Their society is ruled by women: the Matron Mothers of the great houses and the priestesses beneath them.
  • In Tolkien, "Dark Elves" meant something gentler: elves who never saw the light of Valinor. Not evil, just elves who stayed behind.
  • In the Elder Scrolls, the Dunmer are dark elves too, and not underground at all. See the Dunmer Name Generator.

Houses and titles

Drow cities like Menzoberranzan are ruled by noble houses, ranked against each other and always plotting. A drow is introduced by house, and the house name often comes with an apostrophe, as in Drizzt Do'Urden.

  • Female titles: Matron Mother (the head of the house), High Priestess, First Daughter.
  • Male titles: Weapons Master (in charge of the house's fighters), Archmage, Patron (the Matron Mother's consort).

None of the house names here belong to a house from the Forgotten Realms, so you can rank yours anywhere you like.

Playing a drow

  • Decide where you stand with your house. Loyal, exiled, or secretly working against it: every drow story starts there.
  • Female drow outrank male drow in traditional drow society. A male drow with ambition, or a female drow who walked away from power, is an instant character.
  • The surface is strange. Sunlight blinds them in many settings. A drow who has come up into the light has given something up to do it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are dark elves the same as drow?

In D&D, yes: drow is the dark elves' own name. In other settings, dark elves can be quite different, like the Dunmer of the Elder Scrolls.

Are these names from the Forgotten Realms books?

No. They're in the drow style, but famous drow like Drizzt, Jarlaxle and Viconia, and the great houses like Baenre and Do'Urden, are left out.

Can I use these names in D&D?

Yes, they're free to use and you don't need to credit the site.
