How the Gamertag Generator Works

It works from 186 gamertags and name parts, which combine into over 26,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style:
    • Any style, e.g. Shadowseer
    • Cool, e.g. GhostReaper
    • Funny, e.g. ProfessorTurnip
    • Fantasy, e.g. Boneweaver88
    • Short, 4 letters, e.g. Quin
  • Include your own word: No, Yes, use the word below.
  • Your word or name: type a name and it's built into every result.

Each press gives you 8, 16 or 32 names (choose under "How many"), and you won't see the same one twice until you've been through everything for the choices you've made.

A good gamertag is short, easy to read in a kill feed and still free on the platform you play on. This gamertag generator makes tags in four styles (cool, funny, fantasy and short four-letter tags) and can mix in a word or name of your own, so you end up with something that's yours.

Each platform's name rules

  • Xbox: gamertags can be up to 12 characters and include spaces. Since 2019, if the name you want is taken, Xbox lets you keep it with a number suffix (like #1234) shown after it.
  • Discord: since 2023, every account has a unique username of 2 to 32 characters, using lowercase letters, numbers, underscores and full stops, plus a separate display name that can be anything.
  • Instagram: usernames can be up to 30 characters, with letters, numbers, full stops and underscores.
  • TikTok: usernames can be up to 24 characters, with letters, numbers, underscores and full stops.

Rules change from time to time, so check the platform's own sign-up page if a name won't go through.

What makes a good gamertag

  • Short. Teammates need to call it out in a hurry. Two parts at most.
  • Readable. Avoid swapping letters for numbers (Sh4d0w) unless it's a joke; it's hard to say and easy to misspell.
  • Yours. Add a word you actually like, a nickname or an in-joke, with Include your own word.
  • Clean. Every name here is family-friendly, and platforms do remove offensive names.

If your favourite is taken

  • Add a number that means something to you, not a random one.
  • Try another style. A fantasy tag (Runeblade) is less likely to be taken than a cool one (ShadowWolf).
  • Go short. Four-letter tags are rare on old platforms but you'll often find an invented one free.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can an Xbox gamertag be?

Up to 12 characters, including spaces. If the name is already taken, Xbox adds a number suffix so you can still use it.

What are Discord's username rules?

Since 2023, usernames are unique, 2 to 32 characters, and use lowercase letters, numbers, underscores and full stops. Your display name can be different.

How do I make a gamertag with my name in it?

Choose "Yes, use the word below", type your name or nickname, and press Generate Names.
