How crochet names work

  • Craft words: hook, loop, stitch, skein and yarn all make good name parts. Amigurumi, the Japanese word for small crocheted or knitted stuffed toys, is a search term in its own right.
  • UK or US terms: the same stitch has different names on each side of the Atlantic: a UK treble is a US double. A name like Treble Maker reads as British.
  • Your own name is the most common shape, like Crochet with Sarah, and it suits a one-person shop.
  • Toys have rules. Crocheted toys sold in the UK must meet the toy safety regulations and carry UKCA or CE marking, handmade ones included.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A crochet name ends up on your shop, your tags and your Instagram, so it's worth a few minutes' checking before anything is printed:

  • Press Check on any name above. The Business Name Checker looks up the domain, links to the company register (Companies House in the UK) and opens the trade mark search and social handles.
  • Search where you'll trade. Look the name up on maps, marketplaces and social media. Two businesses with the same name confuse customers and reviews.
  • Etsy shop names have to be unique, 4 to 20 characters, with no spaces, so check your favourite still fits.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, from shortlisting to registering, read our guide to Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for makers

  • Match the name to what you sell. Cute suits toys, Aesthetic suits blankets and homeware, and Classic suits a yarn shop.
  • Think about patterns. If you'll sell patterns as well as finished pieces, a name without Shop in it travels better.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What should I name my crochet business?
Many makers use their own name, as in Hooked by Sarah or Sarah Crochets, which is warm and easy to find. If you want something catchier, try a pun on hooks, loops or yarn, or a soft pair such as Moss & Merino for an aesthetic Instagram shop. Avoid names close to yarn and kit brands such as Hobbii or The Woobles.
+What are cute crochet business names?
Soft, small names like Loopy Lambs, Pip & Purl, Snuggle Stitch or Wee Woollies. Choose Cute in the Style menu, or add your own name for something like Sarah's Crochet Nook.
+How do I check a crochet business name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it is free as a company name, a web address and a social media handle. In the UK, search the Companies House register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Do this before you pay for a logo, labels or signage.
+How long can an Etsy shop name be?
Etsy shop names must be 4 to 20 characters long, with no spaces, and unique on Etsy. Check your favourite fits before you design a logo.
