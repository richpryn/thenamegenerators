How the Book Club Name Generator Works

It works from 599 names and name parts, which combine into over 3,100 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style:
    • Any style, e.g. The Sleepless Sisters
    • Funny, e.g. The Spoiled It for Everyone Club
    • Wine & cake, e.g. The Bakes and Tomes Book Club
    • Cosy, e.g. The Fireside Circle
    • Literary, e.g. The Limerick Society
    • Women and mums, e.g. The Women Who Read
    • Kids, e.g. The Bookmark Detectives
    • Your town, e.g. The Marginalia Society of Hampton
  • Your town or area: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Hampton").

Where the names come from: Every name is invented. Names are built from book words, puns, days of the week and cosy places, in the patterns real reading groups use. They aren't the names of famous book clubs, and none is an author's name or a whole famous book title.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A good book club name tells people what kind of evening to expect: puns and cake, a quiet literary circle, or a gang of young readers. This Book Club Name Generator makes names in all of these styles, and can build one from your town too.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are some funny book club names?
Puns on book words work best: Novel Gazers, Sequel Opportunities, Book, Line and Sinker or Shelf Help Group. Honest ones are funny too, like The Watched the Film Instead Club. Choose Funny, or Wine and cake for names like Read Between the Wines.
+What is a good book club name for women or mums?
Something warm with a wink, like Mum's the Word Book Club, The Dog-Eared Dames or Mummy Needs a Chapter. Choose Women and mums for more, or add your town for a name that's easy to find in a local Facebook group.
