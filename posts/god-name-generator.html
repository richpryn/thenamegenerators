How the God Name Generator Works

It works from 250 names and name parts, which combine into over 1,100,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • God or goddess:
    • Either, e.g. Wadisa, Keeper of Smiths
    • God, e.g. Seyoth, Father of Longing
    • Goddess, e.g. Astene, Goddess of Dawn
  • Sounds like: Any, Greek-sounding, Norse-sounding, Egyptian-sounding, Celtic-sounding, Otherworldly.
  • Domain: Any domain, War, Love, Death, The sea, The sun, The moon, Harvest, Wisdom, Trickery, Storms, The forge, Nature.
  • Name format: With their domain, Name only.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

Every fantasy world needs gods, and a pantheon feels real when its gods share a sound. This god name generator makes new gods and goddesses in Greek, Norse, Egyptian, Celtic or otherworldly styles, each with a domain: war, love, death, the sea, the harvest and more.
