Good elf city names are long, soft and full of vowels, and they usually mean something: a hall, a haven or a grove, named after dawn, the sea or the stars. Every name from this elf city name generator is built from a small elvish tongue invented just for this page, so each city can come with its meaning: Aethaleth is ‘the court of dawn’ and Ossisaen ‘the haven of dolphins’. Pick high, wood, dark, sea or moon and star elves for your D&D campaign or novel.

How elf cities are named

The most famous elf cities in fiction have two names: one in Elvish and one in the common tongue. Tolkien's Rivendell is the English form of the Sindarin Imladris, "deep dale of the cleft", and Lothlórien adds loth, "flower", to the name of the land. In D&D's Forgotten Realms, the dark elves' great city under the earth is Menzoberranzan, hard-edged and long, which is how drow names have sounded ever since.

The elvish on this page is invented. It isn't Tolkien's Sindarin or Quenya, or any game's official language. It's a small set of word parts made for this generator, so each part always means the same thing:

  • High elves name cities after courts, thrones and dawn: Daeraleth, "the court of summer".
  • Wood elves use groves, boughs and animals: Pelloryth, "the boughs of acorns".
  • Dark elves underground get harsh sounds and secrets: Sevresk, "the gate of secrets".
  • Sea elves name harbours after whales and waves: Maerarra, "the lantern of the waves".
  • Moon and star elves build bridges and towers: Cyrahanys, "the tower of the north star".

Choose Name only for the city on its own, or With meaning to get the translation too.

Building an elven city

  • Use the meaning. A city called "the refuge of the white stag" needs a story about the stag.
  • Give it a common name too. Humans and dwarves won't say Neylatham; they'll call it the Hidden Hall.
  • Keep one kind of elf per region, so neighbouring cities sound related.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good elf city names?
Long, flowing names with a meaning, like Aethaleth, ‘the court of dawn’, Hirrawyn, ‘the refuge of the white stag’, or Ossisaen, ‘the haven of dolphins’. Choose a kind of elf to match the city.
+Is the elvish on this page Tolkien's Elvish?
No. It's a small elvish tongue invented for this generator, so the meanings are consistent but they aren't Sindarin, Quenya or any game's official language.
+What do dark elf cities sound like?
Harder and more hissing than other elf cities, with sounds like zh, x and rr: Nhalarrath, ‘the city of the black river’, or Sevresk, ‘the gate of secrets’.
