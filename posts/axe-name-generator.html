How the Axe Name Generator Works

It works from 693 names and name parts, which combine into over 480,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Kind of Axe: Any, Norse, Dwarven, Brutal, Homely.
  • Name Format:
    • Name and epithet, e.g. Mad Kate, Bane of Brambles
    • Name and description, e.g. Grey Kate, a blood-rusted battleaxe
    • Name only, e.g. Sweet Meg

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

Vikings named their axes after troll-women. That's the kind of fact that makes a good axe name, and this axe name generator has names in four styles: Norse axes like Shieldtroll, dwarven heirlooms like Grudgehewer, brutal cleavers like Skullsplitter, and the homely names people give tools they love, like Old Bess.

Axes with names

  • Rimmugýgr, "battle-ogress", was the axe of Skarphéðinn in Njáls saga. Norse warriors liked to give axes the names of troll-women and giantesses.
  • Hel was the axe of King Olaf II of Norway, named after the goddess of the dead.
  • Kennings, the riddling phrases of Norse poetry, called an axe things like the "troll-woman of the shield". The Norse names here borrow that habit: Helmhag, Byrniewolf.
  • The labrys, the double axe of Minoan Crete, may have given its name to the Labyrinth, "the house of the double axe". That's an old theory, and still argued over.

Four kinds of axe

  • Norse: shield-breakers named like ogresses and wolves. For Vikings, berserkers and anyone with a longship.
  • Dwarven: names about stone, oaths and grudges. Dwarves in fantasy, from Tolkien's Gimli on, love an axe.
  • Brutal: what it does, said plainly. For orcs, warlords and executioners.
  • Homely: people have always given favourite tools and guns pet names, often women's names. Edinburgh Castle's great cannon is Mons Meg. A woodsman's Old Bess or a barbarian's Little Nell says a lot about the owner.

Kinds of axe

Choose "Name and description" and the generator says what kind of axe it is:

  • Bearded axe: a Viking design whose blade hangs below the haft, so you could hook a shield or choke up for close work.
  • Dane axe: the big two-handed war axe of the Viking age, carried by the housecarls at Hastings.
  • Poleaxe: a late medieval weapon for fighting in armour, with an axe head, a hammer and a spike.
  • Felling axe and hatchet: working tools, which in stories have a habit of becoming weapons.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are some famous axe names?
From history and legend, Rimmugýgr (Skarphéðinn's "battle-ogress") and Hel (King Olaf II's axe). In games and comics, the Leviathan Axe of God of War and Thor's Jarnbjorn. The famous fictional ones aren't in the generator.
+What is a good name for a dwarf's axe?
Something about stone, oaths or grudges: choose Dwarven for names like Grudgehewer or Runebreaker, and add an epithet like "Grudge of the Clan".
+Can I use these names in D&D or my book?
Yes, they're free to use and you don't need to credit the site.
