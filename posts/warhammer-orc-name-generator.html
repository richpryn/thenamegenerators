Warhammer's greenskins don't do quiet. Their names are short, their titles are long, and every boss has a nickname they made up themselves. This Warhammer orc name generator works for 40K Orks and for the Orcs of the Old World and Age of Sigmar: pick a setting, then add a title, a clan, a nickname, or the lot.

How greenskins are named

  • Short and hard. Two blunt syllables, lots of g, k and z: the sound of something being hit.
  • Titles do the talking. An Ork's rank is part of their name: Warboss, Big Mek, Painboy, Weirdboy. The bigger the Ork, the longer it gets. The most famous of all is Ghazghkull Mag Uruk Thraka.
  • Nicknames in Ork-speak. 40K Orks talk in a kind of comic Cockney, dropping their hs and turning -er into -a: a smasher becomes a Smasha, a head-banger an 'Eadbanga.
  • Nothing subtle. If the name could be shouted at the start of a charge, it's right.

The 40K clans

  • Goffs: the biggest, meanest and most fond of hand-to-hand fighting, in black and white.
  • Evil Sunz: obsessed with speed, because red ones go faster.
  • Bad Moons: the rich ones. Their teeth grow fastest, and Orks use teeth (teef) as money.
  • Deathskulls: looters and scavengers, painted blue for luck.
  • Snakebites: old-fashioned, riding boars and keeping squigs.
  • Blood Axes: the ones who've picked up tactics from humans, which other Orks find suspicious.

Orcs of the Old World and Age of Sigmar

In Warhammer Fantasy, now the Old World, Orcs fight in tribes under a Warboss, with Black Orcs as the toughest of all; Grimgor Ironhide is the most famous. In Age of Sigmar they're Orruks, split into warclans like the armoured Ironjawz and the sneaky Kruleboyz, led by Megabosses.

Choose "Old World and Age of Sigmar" for fantasy titles (Big Boss, Megaboss, Shaman) and tribes instead of 40K clans.

Naming your army

  • Name the boss first, with the full title, then give the mob nicknames that fit around them.
  • Let the nickname come from the table. The Nob who survived a whole game on one wound has earned "Lucky".
  • Your Waaagh! In 40K a Waaagh! is a war cry and a whole crusade at once, named after the boss who leads it. Once your Warboss has a name, the Waaagh! has one too.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Orks and Orcs in Warhammer?

Orks, with a k, are the Warhammer 40,000 kind: sci-fi greenskins with guns and vehicles. Orcs, and Orruks in Age of Sigmar, are the fantasy kind, with axes and boars. The names sound much the same, but the titles and clans differ, so pick your setting.

Are these names from Warhammer lore?

No. They're built in the same style, but famous characters like Ghazghkull, Grimgor and Snikrot are left out. The 40K clans are the real ones; the fantasy tribes are invented.

Can I use these for my army or a Kill Team?

Yes, they're free to use and you don't need to credit the site. Generate a batch and give each model its own name.
