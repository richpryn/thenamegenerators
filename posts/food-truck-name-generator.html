How the Food Truck Name Generator Works

It works from 677 names and name parts, which combine into over 2,300 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, Catchy and punny, Funny, Cute, Modern and unique, Tacos and Mexican, Burgers and BBQ, From your town, From your name.
  • Your first name: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Sarah").

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A food truck name has to work painted on the side of a van, shouted across a festival field and typed into Instagram. This food truck name generator gives you names in eight styles, from punny to taco and BBQ names, for trucks in the US and food vans in the UK.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a food truck name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Before you commit, search the official registers too: in the UK, Companies House and the UK trade mark register; in the US, your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Look on a map app for businesses with the same name nearby.
+Should a food truck name say what food you sell?
It helps. People choose a truck from across a car park or a festival field, so a name like Fuego Tacos or Hickory Smoke BBQ sells itself. If you might change the menu, pick a catchy name and put the food on the van underneath it, e.g. Wheel Good Food: burgers and loaded fries.
