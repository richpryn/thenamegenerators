What makes a good quiz team name

A Christmas quiz team name should be a pun the quizmaster has to read out loud.

  • Carol puns work best because everyone knows the tune: Away in a Ranking, Oh Come All Ye Quizzers.
  • Cracker-joke puns earn a groan: Elf Assessment, Yule Never Guess.
  • Keep it short enough to fit on the answer sheet.
  • Office party? The festive style gives safe, friendly names like the Mulled Brainboxes.

Team names for kids and family Christmas parties

Choose Kids and family under Who's playing? for names that work at a school party, a family quiz or a children's party game:

  • Team… names split a party into teams in seconds: Team Mince Pie, Team Snow Angel, Team Red Arctic Fox. Give each team a colour and a sticker to match, and keeping score is easy.
  • Puns children can get: Elf Service, The Jingle Belles, Snow Way José.
  • Festive names that sound like a gang: The Gingerbread Gang, The Sparkly Crackers, The Woolly-Jumpered Mince Pies.

For a family quiz with mixed ages, put an adult and a child in each team, and let the youngest pick the team name.

Running a Christmas quiz

A Christmas quiz is a British pub and office tradition, and a good team name is the easiest point of the night.

  • Give a prize for the best team name: it gets everyone into the spirit before the first round.
  • Christmas crackers were invented by the London sweet-maker Tom Smith in the 1840s, and their jokes make a good tie-breaker round.
  • Teams of four to six work best for a pub or office quiz.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are some funny Christmas quiz team names?
Puns on carols and Christmas words: Away in a Ranking, Quiztletoe, Elf Assessment, Yule Never Guess. Choose the puns style for more.
+What are good Christmas team names for kids?
Simple, cheerful names like Team Mince Pie, Team Snow Angel, Elf Service or The Gingerbread Gang. Choose Kids and family, then Team… for colour-team names that split a party quickly.
+What are good family Christmas team names?
Puns everyone at the table gets, like The Jingle Belles or Snow Way José, or festive names like The Sparkly Crackers. Choose Kids and family so the names suit every age.
+What's a good team name for an office Christmas quiz?
Something friendly and safe for work, like the Mulled Brainboxes or the Merry Baubles. Choose the festive style.
+Who invented Christmas crackers?
Tom Smith, a London confectioner, in the 1840s.
