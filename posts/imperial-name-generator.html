How the Imperial Name Generator Works

It works from 721 names and name parts, which combine into over 10,000,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Gender: Any, Male, Female.
  • Name format:
    • Any, e.g. Tribune Ermelinda Belario
    • First name and surname, e.g. Sevanus Velianus
    • Legion, e.g. General Arvenio Branicci
    • First name only, e.g. Fiorenzo
    • Surname only, e.g. Vitianus
  • Starts with: depends on the name format:
    • Any, First name and surname, Legion and First name only: Any letter, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, I, L, M, N, O, P, Q, R, S, T, U, V
    • Surname only: Any letter, A, B, C, D, F, G, L, M, N, O, P, Q, R, S, T, V

Where the names come from: Some names are real and some are invented. First names are real Latin and Italian names, with some made-up ones in the same style. Surnames are new, built from Latin and Italian sounds, with a few real Italian surnames. Every name is checked against the characters on the Elder Scrolls wiki, and none of the classical Roman names from this site's Roman Name Generator is used, so the two pages don't overlap.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Imperials come from Cyrodiil, the heart of the Empire, and their names sound like Rome with an Italian accent: General Tullius, commanding the Legion in Skyrim, sits alongside Vittoria Vici and Adrianne Avenicci. This Imperial name generator makes new names in that style, a first name and surname, a single name or a name with a Legion rank, for Skyrim, Elder Scrolls Online or your own story. It's a fan-made, unofficial tool, not connected to Bethesda Softworks or ZeniMax, and it never gives you the name of a character from the games.

How Imperial names work

Most Imperials in the games have a first name and a surname, and they come in two flavours:

  • Latin, like a Roman citizen: General Tullius, and Proventus Avenicci, the steward of Whiterun.
  • Italian, as if the Empire had moved on a few centuries: Adrianne Avenicci at Warmaiden's in Whiterun, Carlotta Valentia on its market stalls, and Adelaisa Vendicci of the East Empire Company in Solitude.

The two flavours mix freely, even in one family: Proventus's daughter is Adrianne. This generator does the same, so you might get a Latin first name with an Italian surname, like Placidus Castellani, or the other way round.

Imperials in the games rarely use the three-part Roman name of praenomen, nomen and cognomen. For real Romans built that way, try the Roman Name Generator.

Eight good Imperial names

  • Marcello Castellani: Italian through and through, for a merchant of the Imperial City.
  • Placidus Orvianus: placidus is Latin for 'calm', a good name for a steward.
  • Ulpiana Cervianus: a Latin name for a noblewoman of an old family.
  • Graziella Tavetti: an Italian name for a tavern keeper or a smith.
  • General Fabiola Merenzi: a Legion commander with a name to match.
  • Captain Pacatus Pallantius: pacatus means 'peaceful', which is an odd thing to call a soldier.
  • Peregrinus: Latin for 'foreigner' or 'traveller', for an Imperial a long way from home.
  • Vesperius: a surname from vesper, Latin for 'evening'.

The Legion, the Emperor and the two halves of Cyrodiil

Imperials run the Empire, and plenty of them serve it in the Imperial Legion. Skyrim's Legion has General Tullius in command, Legate Rikke in the field and Commander Maro of the Penitus Oculatus guarding the Emperor; join the Legion yourself and you start as an Auxiliary. The Legion format puts Captain, Commander, General or Tribune in front of a name.

Cyrodiil itself has two halves: the Colovian west, with its highlands and soldiers, and the Nibenese east around the Nibenay Valley. The lore gives them different customs, but the games don't give them different naming rules, so this generator doesn't either. If you want a Colovian, lean Latin and plain; for a Nibenese courtier, pick something longer and more ornate.

For the Empire's neighbours, try the Breton Name Generator and the Nord Name Generator.

Frequently Asked Questions

+Do Imperials have last names?
Yes, most do, like Vittoria Vici and Adrianne Avenicci. Choose First name and surname, or Surname only to go with a first name you have already.
+Are Imperial names Roman?
They're Roman and Italian in style, but they don't follow the real Roman three-part name. The first names here are real Latin and Italian names; the surnames are mostly new.
+What are good female Imperial names?
Set the gender to Female for names like Ulpiana Cervianus or Graziella Tavetti. Choose Legion for an officer, like General Fabiola Merenzi.
+How do I make an Imperial Legion name?
Choose Legion to put a rank in front: Captain, Commander, General or Tribune, as in Captain Pacatus Pallantius.
+Is there a difference between Colovian and Nibenese names?
The games don't give the two halves of Cyrodiil different naming rules. Choose any format, and lean Latin and plain for a Colovian or longer and more ornate for a Nibenese character.
+Can I use these names in Elder Scrolls Online?
Yes. ESO names must be unique on your server, and a first name with a surname is much easier to find free than a single name.
+Is this an official Elder Scrolls tool?
No. It's a fan-made, unofficial generator, not connected to Bethesda Softworks or ZeniMax. The names are new, and free to use for your characters.
