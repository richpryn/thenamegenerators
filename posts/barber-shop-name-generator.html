Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a barber shop name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, also search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as another business nearby with a similar name can confuse clients.
+What's a good name for a barber shop?
A good barber shop name is short, easy to say and easy to spell, so it fits on the window, the pole sign and an Instagram handle. Classic shapes such as "Hartley & Sons Barbers" or "Church Street Barbers" feel trustworthy; a pun such as "Fade Expectations" gets remembered. Avoid well-worn puns that dozens of shops already use, and check nobody nearby trades under something similar.
