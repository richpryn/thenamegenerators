Gandalf has a different name in almost every language of Middle-earth: Mithrandir to the Elves, Tharkûn to the Dwarves, Olórin in the West. This LOTR wizard name generator builds names the way the Elves named the Istari, from a colour and a Sindarin word, and tells you what they mean.

The Istari and their names

  • Gandalf the Grey is Mithrandir, "Grey Pilgrim", to the Elves; Tharkûn to the Dwarves; and Olórin in Valinor, a name linked to dreams. "Gandalf" itself is from Old Norse, roughly "wand-elf".
  • Saruman the White is Curunír to the Elves, "man of skill". His name in the tongue of Rohan means much the same.
  • Radagast the Brown cared for birds and beasts more than for people.
  • The two Blue Wizards, Alatar and Pallando, went into the far East and were never heard of again in the story.

Five wizards came to Middle-earth. Choose a colour none of them took, and yours could be a sixth.

How the Elvish names work

Each name joins a Sindarin colour word to a word for a person:

  • Colours: mith grey, glan and nim white, baran brown, luin blue, calen green, ruin red, morn black, celeb silver.
  • People: randir wanderer (from Mithrandir), dír man, hîr lord, ion son, ben one.

So Calenrandir is "Green Wanderer" and Mornhîr "Black Lord": a name to be careful with. In real Sindarin, sounds can change where words join; these keep the parts whole so the meaning is clear.

Using a wizard of your own

  • Give them a name for each people. Gandalf's many names show how far he travelled. Your wizard might be a colour to Men, an Elvish name to the Elves, and something rude to the orcs.
  • Give them a cause. Each of the Istari had a purpose, and a weakness: Saruman's pride, Radagast's love of beasts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the Elvish names real Sindarin?

They're built from real Sindarin words from Tolkien's own writings, and the meaning shown is what those words mean. Mithrandir itself is left out, so your wizard isn't Gandalf.

How many wizards are there in Lord of the Rings?

Five Istari came to Middle-earth: Gandalf the Grey, Saruman the White, Radagast the Brown, and the two Blue Wizards, Alatar and Pallando.
