How drill names work

Drill began in Chicago in the early 2010s, with Chief Keef among its first stars. It took root in south London soon after, where groups like 67 and Harlem Spartans shaped UK drill. Then it crossed back to New York: Brooklyn drill grew in the late 2010s, and Pop Smoke worked with UK producers such as 808Melo.

The names differ a little on each side of the Atlantic:

  • UK drill tags are short nicknames, often ending in -zy, -o or -z, with a letter or number: Kwamz C, Javo Seven, Y-Zero.
  • NY drill leans on Lil, Big and Baby, a first name and a flashy surname: Big Teddy, Rocco Flossy, Baby Ricky.
  • Duo names are two tags joined with x or &, and mixtapes are about the cold and the climb: From the Cold, No Struggle, No Glory.

Every name here is kept clean, with nothing about violence or real people.

Using the names

  • Make sure nobody else has it. Short tags are popular, so search YouTube, Spotify and Instagram before you upload anything.
  • Keep it easy to say on a beat. One or two syllables and a letter works best in an ad-lib.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good drill rapper names?
Short, cold tags: a nickname with a letter or number (Tezzy T5, Kemzy V) for UK drill, or a Lil, Big or Baby name (Big Teddy, Baby Ricky) for New York drill.
+What's the difference between UK and NY drill names?
UK drill names tend to be nicknames with an initial or number on the end. New York drill names use more Lil, Big and Baby, and flashy surnames like Dinero. Choose a style for names in each.
+Where did drill music come from?
Chicago, in the early 2010s. It spread to south London, where UK drill developed its own sound, and from there to Brooklyn in the late 2010s.
