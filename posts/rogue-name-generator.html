A rogue's name is often short, easy to shout across a rooftop and easy to change if things go wrong. This rogue name generator gives D&D 5e rogues a quick, memorable first name and an epithet for their Roguish Archetype, from the light-fingered Thief to the dashing Swashbuckler.

The rogue in D&D

A D&D rogue wins on precision: Sneak Attack deals extra damage when they catch a target off guard.

  • Archetypes: the Thief, Assassin and Arcane Trickster in the Player's Handbook, and the Inquisitive, Mastermind, Scout, Swashbuckler, Phantom and Soulknife in later books.
  • Thieves' Cant: rogues know a secret language. The real thieves' cant was the slang of London's underworld in the 1500s and 1600s, recorded in pamphlets warning honest folk.

Naming a rogue

A rogue's name should be short, easy to say in a hurry and easy to drop.

  • The name (Fen, Ivy, Sly, Kestrel) works as an alias.
  • The epithet follows the archetype: a Thief "Master of Locks", an Inquisitive "the Unfoolable", a Swashbuckler "the Duellist".
  • A codename suits an assassin: try the Assassin Name Generator.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's a good rogue name?
Short and easy to drop, like Fen or Sly, with an epithet for the archetype, like "Master of Locks" for a Thief.
+What is thieves' cant?
In D&D, a secret language rogues use. In history, it was the slang of the criminal underworld in 1500s and 1600s England.
+What are the rogue subclasses?
Thief, Assassin and Arcane Trickster in the Player's Handbook, plus Inquisitive, Mastermind, Scout, Swashbuckler, Phantom and Soulknife.
