Good slowcore names are as quiet as the songs: one small word, a far-off town, or a plain phrase about a house and the people in it. This slowcore band name generator makes band names, artist names and album titles for slowcore and sadcore, from Thaw to Grey Barn Choir.

How slowcore names itself

Slowcore grew out of American indie rock in the late 1980s and early 1990s, when bands like Codeine, Red House Painters and Low slowed rock right down. Low, from Duluth, Minnesota, are the best-known example of the naming style: one plain word that describes the music. Codeine and Bedhead did the same.

The generator follows that habit in three formats:

  • Band names are a single quiet word (Sill, Ebb) or a small scene: Grey Church Farmers, Green Kitchen Sleepers.
  • Artist names are plain real-sounding names, or a solo project named like a place: Mount Lonely, Lantern Slide.
  • Album titles are long and specific, often a northern town and a cold month: Thunder Bay in January, We Drove Home in Silence.

Sadcore is the slightly bleaker cousin, and the same names suit it.

Using the names

  • Check before you commit. Search the name on Spotify, Bandcamp and social media. Short one-word names are often taken, so a two-word name is easier to own.
  • Keep the album titles plain. A slowcore title should read like something you'd say out loud, not a riddle.
  • Thinking of selling merch or registering a label? Check the name isn't trademarked first.

If you write slowcore yourself, I've shared five tips for making better slowcore on my music site: two-chord progressions, 7th and 9th chords, rolling off the highs, sparse drums and keeping the imperfect takes.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good slowcore band names?
Quiet, plain names: a single word like Parlour or Sill, or a small scene like Grey Church Farmers. The best-known slowcore band, Low, set the pattern with one word that describes the music.
+What's the difference between slowcore and sadcore?
They overlap a lot. Slowcore describes the tempo, slow and spare, while sadcore describes the mood, which is usually bleaker. The same kind of name suits both.
+How do I name a slowcore album?
Make it specific and ordinary: a town and a month (Albany in December), a plain fact (It Gets Dark at Four) or something someone might say (Leave the Hall Light On). Choose Album title for names built that way.
