A rap name is a persona as much as a label, and most fit a few shapes: a prefix like Lil, Young or MC; a single bold word; or a word spelled your own way. This rapper name generator builds names in all three, so you can try a persona on before you step up to the mic.

How famous rap names were made

  • From initials: Eminem comes from Marshall Mathers' initials, M&M.
  • From a pun: Ludacris is a play on "ludicrous".
  • From a nickname: Snoop Dogg's mother called him Snoop because he looked like Snoopy. MC Hammer got "Hammer" from a resemblance to the baseball player Hank Aaron, "Hammerin' Hank".
  • With a prefix: Lil Wayne, Lil Nas X, Young Thug. Both signal youth and confidence.

Choosing your rap name

  • Make it yours. The best rap names come from something real: a nickname, initials, where you're from.
  • Say it in a verse. You'll rhyme with it and shout it, so it needs to sound good at speed.
  • Make it searchable. A stylised spelling (Rhymez rather than Rhymes) is easier to find online and to claim on streaming services.
  • Check it's free on Spotify, SoundCloud and social media before you release anything.

Frequently Asked Questions

+Why do so many rappers use Lil or Young?
They signal youth and confidence, and they're instantly recognisable as rap names. Today they're simply a classic shape, used by artists from Lil Wayne to Young Thug.
+How do I check a rap name is free?
Search Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud and YouTube for the exact name, and check social media handles. A stylised spelling often makes a name easier to claim.
