Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a home care name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Before you commit, search the official registers too: in the UK, Companies House; in the US, your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search.
+Is home care the same as domiciliary care?
Yes. Domiciliary care is the formal UK term for care in a person's own home, while most agencies call it home care or care at home in their name because it is clearer to families. In the US, home care and senior care are the usual words. In England a home care agency giving personal care must register with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), so check your name there too.
