A guild tells you what it does by its name, and how it wants to be seen: a thieves' guild called the Velvet Hand is selling discretion, a merchants' Honest Ledger Consortium is protesting too much. This guild name generator has names for five kinds of guild, each in its own style.

Real guilds

  • Medieval guilds controlled a trade in a town: who could practise it, at what price and to what standard. A craftsman went from apprentice to journeyman to master.
  • The masterpiece was originally the piece of work a journeyman submitted to the guild to be made a master.
  • London's livery companies are guilds that survive today, many with grand names like the Worshipful Company of Goldsmiths or of Clockmakers. That's the style the craft guild names here borrow.

Six kinds of guild

  • Adventurers' companies: named like mercenary bands, after an emblem or a place. The Gilded Lanterns, Ravenscar Company.
  • Thieves' guilds: polite names for impolite work. The Velvet Hand, Brotherhood of the Open Window. In Terry Pratchett's Ankh-Morpork the Thieves' Guild is perfectly legal and issues receipts.
  • Assassins: named like a respectable house or a quiet society, so nobody guesses the trade. The House of the Quiet Hour, the Order of the Snuffed Candle, the School of the Gentle Knife.
  • Mages: circles, conclaves and orders, named after a symbol. The Conclave of the Burning Key.
  • Merchants: companies named after trade routes or goods. The Saltroad Company, Fellowship of Amber Merchants.
  • Craft guilds: the honest trades. The Worshipful Company of Cartographers, the Patient Loom Guild.

Using a guild in your game

  • Give it a hall and a rule. Every guild has a building and at least one rule outsiders find strange.
  • Give it a rival. The mages' circle and the alchemists' guild arguing over who owns fire is a session in itself.
  • Online games: for an MMO guild or a team, the Clan Name Generator has gaming names with tags.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What is a good name for a thieves' guild?
Something that sounds respectable, or at least discreet: the Velvet Hand, the Quiet Market, the Brotherhood of the Locked Door. Choose "Thieves' guild" for more.
+What's the difference between a guild and a clan?
A guild is joined for a trade or purpose; a clan is a family or people. In online games the words are often used for the same thing.
+Can I use these names in D&D or my book?
Yes, they're free to use and you don't need to credit the site.
