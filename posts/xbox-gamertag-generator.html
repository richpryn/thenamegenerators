How the Xbox Gamertag Generator Works

It works from 719 gamertags and name parts, which combine into over 270,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style:
    • Any style, e.g. Thunder Lead
    • Two words, e.g. Ashen Hornet
    • Squad callsign, e.g. Bison Seven
    • Funny, e.g. Muddy Toast
    • Old-school, e.g. xOnslaughtx
  • Numbers: No numbers, With a number.
  • Include your own word: No, Yes, use the word below.
  • Your word or name (short is best): type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Sam").

Where the names come from: Every name is invented. Gamertags are built from word lists written for this page: everyday words that pair well with a space, radio-style callsigns, silly British words and the xX…Xx wrappers of the Xbox 360 years. Every one is 12 characters or fewer, using letters, numbers and spaces and starting with a letter. Brands, game characters and famous players were checked for and left out. This page is not made by or connected with Microsoft or Xbox.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Xbox gamertags can have spaces in them, which most platforms don't allow, so a tag can read like a real name or a callsign. This Xbox gamertag generator makes tags of 12 characters or fewer in four styles (two words with a space, squad callsigns, funny British tags and old-school xX…Xx tags) and can mix in a word or name of your own. It's an unofficial tool, not made by or connected with Microsoft or Xbox.

Eight good Xbox gamertags

All eight came from the generator, two from each style:

  • Quiet Comet: two short words with a space, easy to say in party chat.
  • Brisk Heron: a calm animal name that won't date.
  • Rook Nine: a callsign, for players who like to sound like a squad on the radio.
  • Kilo Actual: NATO alphabet plus "Actual", the radio word for the person in charge.
  • Soggy Scone: funny and very British.
  • No Idea Mate: a whole phrase that fits in exactly 12 characters.
  • xXHavocXx: the xX…Xx wrapper that was everywhere in the Xbox 360 years.
  • iiFrosty: another old-school style, with the double i in front.

Xbox gamertag rules

  • Length: a gamertag can be up to 12 characters, and spaces count.
  • Spaces: allowed, so Quiet Comet works as it is.
  • Taken names: since 2019 you can choose a gamertag even if someone else has it. Xbox adds a # and a number after it (Quiet Comet#4821) so everyone stays unique, and shows the number smaller than the name.
  • The number: Xbox picks it, not you. The name and number together can be up to 16 characters, # included, so a full 12-character tag gets a 3-digit number and shorter tags usually get 4 digits.
  • Older tags: players who already had a gamertag before the change kept it with no number, and those classic tags could be up to 15 characters.

Every tag here is 12 characters or fewer, uses only letters, numbers and spaces, and starts with a letter. Xbox also accepts letters from many other alphabets and accents, which this generator leaves out.

Spaces, callsigns and xX tags

  • Two words: because Xbox allows spaces, an adjective and a noun (Lost Otter, Brisk Heron) reads cleanly in a lobby and on a scoreboard. Keep each word short: two six-letter words with a space make 13, one too many.
  • Callsigns: they come from military radio, and squad shooters made them familiar. A NATO letter or a bird of prey plus a number word (Rook Nine, Osprey Lead) sounds like it belongs in a squad, and friends can take one each.
  • Funny: British food and grumpy adjectives (Soggy Scone, Damp Welly), or a whole phrase like No Idea Mate.
  • Old-school: xX…Xx, ii and "Its" in front, or HD on the end, are the styles people remember from the Xbox 360 years. Wearing one now is half nostalgia, half joke.

Want a number on the end? Choose With a number for tags like Otter 99 or Rook 27. The numbers are never 69, 420 or other numbers with a second meaning.

Before you change your gamertag

The tags here are free to use, but check yours is available first: try it in the Xbox app or on the Xbox website before you change your gamertag. If the plain name is taken, Xbox adds a number, so decide whether you're happy with Quiet Comet#4821 or would rather choose a tag that's still free on its own.

If you stream as well, the Twitch Name Generator makes names that fit Twitch's rules (no spaces there), and the PSN Name Generator does the same for PlayStation. For a name that works on any platform, try the Gamertag Generator, and for younger players the Kids' Gamertag Generator.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How long can an Xbox gamertag be?
Up to 12 characters, including spaces. If Xbox adds a number because the name is taken, the whole thing, # included, can be up to 16 characters.
+What does the # and number after a gamertag mean?
It means someone else chose the same gamertag. Xbox adds a number so every player is unique, and you can keep the name you wanted.
+Can an Xbox gamertag have spaces?
Yes. Choose Two words for tags like Quiet Comet, or Squad callsign for tags like Rook Nine. The space counts towards the 12 characters.
+What are some cool Xbox gamertags?
Try Squad callsign for tags like Kilo Actual and Osprey Lead, or Old-school for xXHavocXx and iiFrosty.
+What are some funny Xbox gamertags?
Choose Funny for tags like Soggy Scone, Damp Welly and No Idea Mate.
+How do I make a gamertag with my name in it?
Choose "Yes, use the word below" and type your name. With Sam you get tags like Sam Nine and Sam Ate Pie. A short name leaves more room, since the limit is 12 characters.
+Is this an official Xbox tool?
No. It's a free, unofficial generator and isn't connected with Microsoft or Xbox. Check a tag in the Xbox app before you use it.
