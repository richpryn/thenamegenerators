How to name a pet in Minecraft

To name a pet in Minecraft, tame it, rename a name tag on an anvil, then use the name tag on the pet.

  • Taming first: wolves are tamed with bones, cats with raw fish, parrots with seeds, and horses and llamas by riding them until they stop throwing you off. Camels don't need taming: just put a saddle on. Axolotls and frogs can't be tamed, but you can carry axolotls in a bucket and name them.
  • Naming: rename a name tag on an anvil (one level of experience), then use it on the pet. Named mobs don't despawn, and the name makes it easier to find in a crowd of cats.
  • Allays will follow you once you give them an item, so they deserve a name too.

Secret Minecraft name tag names

Some names change how a mob looks or behaves when you put them on a name tag.

A few names do something special when you use them on a mob (most of these are Java Edition):

  • Dinnerbone or Grumm: the mob turns upside down.
  • jeb_: a sheep cycles through every colour of the rainbow.
  • Toast: a rabbit gets a special coat.
  • Johnny: a vindicator attacks almost every mob it sees, so keep it away from your pets.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What should I name my Minecraft dog?
Choose Wolf: cute names like Biscuit, cool ones like Ranger, or funny ones like Not a Creeper.
+What is the pink axolotl called in Minecraft?
In the game's files the pink (leucistic) axolotl is called lucy. The others are wild, gold, cyan and the rare blue.
+What happens if you name a mob Dinnerbone?
It turns upside down. Grumm does the same.
+Do name tags stop mobs despawning?
Yes. Any mob with a name from a name tag won't despawn.
+What are good Minecraft cat names?
Choose Cat for names like Pepper, Mittens or Sir Pounce. Cats in Minecraft are tamed with raw cod or salmon.
+What are good Minecraft horse names?
Choose Horse for names like Thunder, Clover or Sir Gallops. Horses are tamed by riding them until they stop throwing you off.
