How the Group Name Generator Works

It works from 1,540 names and name parts, which combine into over 10,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style:
    • Any style, e.g. Sunday Night Posse
    • Funny, e.g. Duvet Club
    • Cute, e.g. Cuddle Friends
    • Cool, e.g. HQ
    • Family, e.g. Biscuit Tin Massive
    • Work, e.g. Spreadsheet Support Group
    • Best friends, e.g. Our Little Gang
  • How Many People: Any number, Two, Three, Four, Five or more.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Good group chat names are short, a bit of an in-joke, and easy to spot in a long list of chats. Every group chat starts as "New Group" until someone has a better idea. This group name generator is that better idea: pick a style and how many of you there are, and it gives you names to paste into WhatsApp, from the Biscuit Brigade to Table for Four.

Eight good group names

All eight came from the generator:

  • Fourmidable: a name for four people that's also a pun.
  • Garlic Bread Patrol: funny, specific and about food, which is what most group chats end up discussing.
  • Three Peas Club: a cute name for a trio.
  • Egg and Soldiers: a name for two people who go together.
  • Dad's Taxi: a family chat name every parent will recognise.
  • Could Have Been an Email: a work chat name everyone in an office understands.
  • Streetlight Collective: a cool name for a group that stays out late.
  • Fearless Five: an alliterative name for five or more.

Names by style and size

Pick a style for the kind of group you are:

  • Funny: food, in-jokes and mild chaos: Sausage Roll Fan Club, The Easily Distracted Quad.
  • Cute: soft and sweet: Sprinkle Squad, Sweet Peas.
  • Cool: short and stylish: Wavelength, Dark Horses.
  • Family: home life: Sunday Roast, Tea's Ready.
  • Work: office life, kept polite: Coffee Machine Alliance, Shift Swap.
  • Best friends: loyalty and history: Joined at the Hip, Pinky Promise Posse.

Then pick a size. Names for two people play on pairs (Cheese and Crackers), names for three on trios, and names for four or five use the number itself: Pancake Four, Five for Tea.

Picking a name everyone likes

  • Make it about the group. The best names refer to something you all share: a holiday, a joke or a place you always meet.
  • Keep it short so it shows in full in the chat list on a phone.
  • Add an emoji if your app allows it, so the chat is easy to spot.
  • Keep work chats safe for work. Assume anyone could see the name over your shoulder.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's a good group name for 4 people?
Something that uses the number or suits a foursome, like Fourmidable, Pancake Four or Table for Four. Choose Four under group size for more.
+What are good group chat names?
Short, a little bit of an in-joke, and easy to spot in a list: Garlic Bread Patrol for friends, Dad's Taxi for family, or Could Have Been an Email for work.
+What are good friend group names?
Names about loyalty or shared history, like Joined at the Hip, Day Ones or Pinky Promise Posse. Choose Best friends for more.
+Do these work for WhatsApp and other apps?
Yes. Copy the name and paste it into your group's name or subject. Shorter names show in full in the chat list.
