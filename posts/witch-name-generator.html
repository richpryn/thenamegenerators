How the Witch Name Generator Works

It works from 298 names and name parts, which combine into over 100,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Witch style: Any, Classic, Hedge witch, Modern, Halloween costume.
  • Name format:
    • Full name, e.g. Hexie Toadstool
    • First name only, e.g. Raven
    • With an epithet, e.g. Saffron Wychwood Keeper of Bees
    • With a familiar, e.g. Granny Grimble and her owl, Hoot

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

Witches in folk tales are named for where they live and what they know: an old mother at the end of the lane, a herb-wife at the edge of the wood. This witch name generator makes names in four styles, from classic fairy-tale witches to funny names for a Halloween costume.
