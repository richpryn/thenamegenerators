Halfling names sound like the people: warm, short and a little bit cheeky. In D&D a halfling has a given name, a family name and possibly a nickname, and the family name is often a nickname that stuck so well it was handed down. This halfling name generator builds all three, so you can meet Hetty Kettlewhistle, or her cousin Pip “Butterfingers” Puddlefoot.

Given name, family name, nickname

The fifth-edition Player's Handbook (2014) describes halfling names in three parts:

  • A given name. Short and friendly, often the kind of name you'd find in an English village.
  • A family name. Often a nickname that stuck so tenaciously it was passed down the generations: a great-grandmother who was quick on her feet becomes the Quickstep family.
  • A nickname, which halflings may or may not have. It's earned, usually by something they did or keep doing.

The book also splits halflings into lightfoot (sociable wanderers) and stout (hardier, said to have dwarven blood). Both use the same kind of names.

What makes a family name sound halfling

Halfling family names are compound words built from home and countryside:

  • Food and drink: Honeypot, Oakbarrel, Teapot.
  • Plants and fields: Thistledown, Bramblewick, Wheatfield.
  • Water and land: Fernbrook, Willowbank, Riverbend.
  • Feet and moving about: Barleyfoot, Puddlefoot, Quickstep.

Say the name and picture the ancestor it came from. If the picture makes you smile, it's a halfling name.

Halflings and Tolkien's hobbits

D&D's halflings began as a stand-in for Tolkien's hobbits, and the names still show it. Tolkien gave hobbits family names like Baggins, Took, Brandybuck and Proudfoot, and hobbit girls were often named after flowers or jewels: Belladonna, Lobelia, Primula.

This generator's female names lean the same way (Marigold, Poppy, Primrose) without using Tolkien's own characters. For names from Middle-earth itself, see the LOTR Wizard and LOTR Orc generators, or the Tolkien style in the Elf Name Generator.

Picking a nickname

  • Make it about something they did. "Two-Pies" has a story behind it, and someone at the table will ask.
  • Let it be a bit unfair. Nicknames are given by others. A careful rogue called "Butterfingers" has a reason to prove everyone wrong.
  • Watch it become a family name. If your halfling becomes famous, their grandchildren might carry the nickname as a surname, the way the book describes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do halfling names work in D&D?

A halfling has a given name, a family name and sometimes a nickname. In the fifth-edition Player's Handbook, family names are often old nicknames that stuck so well they were passed down.

Are halflings the same as hobbits?

D&D's halflings were inspired by Tolkien's hobbits, so the names sound alike, but halflings are D&D's own people with their own lore, such as the lightfoot and stout halflings.

What are good halfling last names?

Compound words from home and countryside: Thistledown, Kettlewhistle, Puddlefoot, Honeypot. Set Name Format to "Family name only" to see a list.

Are these names from the Player's Handbook?

No. They're new names in the same style, though a few given names, like Bree and Daisy, are ordinary English names that may appear elsewhere.
