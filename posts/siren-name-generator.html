How the Siren Name Generator Works

It works from 102 names and name parts, which combine into over 100,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any, Greek, Song-name.
  • Name format:
    • Name only, e.g. Galene of the Isle of Flowers
    • With a title, e.g. Pearllilt, Singer of the Last Song

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

In Greek myth, the sirens sang from their island so beautifully that sailors steered onto the rocks to reach them. Odysseus had himself tied to the mast to hear them and live. This siren name generator makes Greek-sounding names, song-names like Wavecall, and sirens named for the shore they haunt.
