Ravenclaw witches have names that suggest wisdom, creativity, and intelligence—names like Odessa Silverthorn, Cassiopeia Nightbreeze, and Selene Stormfire have an intellectual, scholarly feel. Hufflepuff witches have names that feel warm, friendly, and down-to-earth—names like Olive Starling, Juniper Honeywick, and Primrose Goodfellow suggest kindness, loyalty, and a connection to nature. Slytherin witches have names that suggest ambition, cunning, and sometimes darkness—names like Astra Malfoy, Belladonna Vexley, and Morgana Shadowmire have a mysterious, powerful feel. Name Styles by House

Gryffindor names emphasize bravery and adventure (Briony Stormglen, Morgana Wildflame, Freya Lionbourne). They often incorporate fire, storms, lions, and elements that suggest courage. Last names suggest strength and heroism (Stormglen, Wildflame, Lionbourne).

Ravenclaw names feature scholarly and mystical elements (Odessa Silverthorn, Cassiopeia Nightbreeze, Selene Stormfire). They may reference stars, wisdom, or elemental magic. Last names suggest places of learning or intellectual pursuits (Silverthorn, Nightbreeze, Stormfire).

Hufflepuff names emphasize warmth and nature (Olive Starling, Juniper Honeywick, Primrose Goodfellow). They often use plant names, gentle sounds, and references to the natural world. Last names suggest cozy, welcoming places (Starling, Honeywick, Goodfellow).

Slytherin names have a darker, more mysterious quality (Astra Malfoy, Belladonna Vexley, Morgana Shadowmire). They often incorporate serpentine, shadow, or dark elements. Last names suggest darkness, power, or cunning (Malfoy, Vexley, Shadowmire).
