Hogwarts Legacy is set in the 1890s, a hundred years before Harry Potter, and its names belong to that era: Poppy Sweeting, Natsai Onai, Matilda Weasley. This Hogwarts Legacy witch name generator builds names the same way, with a Victorian or wizarding first name and a surname chosen to suit your house.

Witches of Hogwarts Legacy

The game's cast is a good guide to the style. Among the students and staff you meet:

  • Natsai "Natty" Onai (Gryffindor), a classmate who joins you on adventures.
  • Poppy Sweeting (Hufflepuff), who'd rather be with magical beasts than people.
  • Imelda Reyes (Slytherin), the school's fastest flyer.
  • Nellie Oggspire (Hufflepuff).
  • Professor Matilda Weasley, who teaches Transfiguration and is deputy head.
  • Professor Mirabel Garlick (Herbology), Professor Dinah Hecat (Defence Against the Dark Arts), Professor Satyavati Shah (Astronomy) and Professor Chiyo Kogawa (Flying).

Notice the mix: everyday English surnames (Sweeting, Garlick), a famous wizarding family (Weasley), and names from around the world (Onai, Shah, Kogawa, Reyes).

Victorian first names

The first names here are ones a witch born around 1880 might really have had. Some were among the most popular girls' names in late Victorian Britain: Florence, Edith, Ethel, Mabel, Alice. Others have the grand, old-fashioned ring the wizarding world loves, in the spirit of Minerva McGonagall, Bathilda Bagshot and Hepzibah Smith: Araminta, Euphemia, Honoria, Perpetua.

Like the game's own cast, a handful come from further afield: Anjali, Mei, Tendai, Saoirse, Esperanza.

Surnames by house

The books don't give the houses naming rules, so this generator leans into each house's character instead. Every surname list is made of real-sounding British names:

  • Gryffindor: bold, outdoorsy names (Hardwick, Wolfe, Fairweather).
  • Hufflepuff: warm, homely, slightly whimsical names (Applegate, Merriweather, Kettle).
  • Ravenclaw: bookish, airy names (Inkpen, Featherstone, Lark).
  • Slytherin: old-money, aristocratic names (Cavendish, Mordaunt, Ravenscroft), in the tradition of Malfoy, Rosier and Selwyn.

Choose Any house to mix them all.

Naming your character in the game

  • The game asks for a first and last name, and won't accept every word, so keep it simple.
  • Say it the way a professor would. "Miss Sweeting" and "Mr Sallow" are how you'll be addressed in class, so make sure the surname works with a title.
  • Think about family. A surname like Gaunt or Weasley tells everyone your background. A new name, like the ones here, lets your character's story be their own.
  • Match the decade. Names like Kayden or Brooklyn would stand out in the 1890s; Florence, Edith, Arthur and Albert fit right in.

Frequently Asked Questions

+When is Hogwarts Legacy set?
In the 1890s, about a hundred years before Harry Potter arrives at Hogwarts. Names that were common in late Victorian Britain fit the setting best.
+Are these names from the game?
No: they're new names in the game's style, so your witch won't share a name with someone you meet. The characters named in the article are from the game.
+Does my house affect my name?
Not in the game: any name works in any house. This generator just leans each house's surnames towards its character, so a Slytherin sounds a little more aristocratic and a Hufflepuff a little more homely.
+Can I use a famous wizarding surname?
For your own character, of course; the game already has a Weasley and a Gaunt. A family name like Black or Malfoy gives your witch a ready-made history, so make sure it's one you want.
