How the Gospel Choir Name Generator Works

It works from 470 names and name parts, which combine into over 200,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • What are you naming?:
    • Choir, e.g. Jamal Mensah & the Oakland Inspirational Choir
    • Singer, e.g. Brother Cedric Crenshaw
    • Album, e.g. A Day of Comfort
  • Singer's gender: depends on the what are you naming?:
    • Choir and Album: Any
    • Singer: Any, Male, Female

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Gospel choirs are usually named for their city or church, and the old quartets for something grand and joyful. This gospel choir name generator makes choir and group names, gospel singer names and album titles in that tradition, with respect for the faith behind it.
