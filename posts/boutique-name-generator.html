How boutiques are named

Boutique is French for a small shop, and the best boutique names still sound personal and small-scale rather than like a chain.

  • A woman's name is a long tradition in dress shops: Shop Mabel, Chez Margot, Simply Sarah.
  • "The Edit" has become a popular way to say the stock is chosen with care.
  • The building or the street works well for a high-street shop: The Granary, The Old Post Office, Hampton Mews Boutique.
  • Western boutiques lean on spurs, fringe, rhinestones and saddles.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A shop sign, carrier bags, labels and a window all carry the name, and each costs money the moment it's printed, so check the name first. Press Check next to any name to open the Business Name Checker, which looks it up in one go:

  • The company register: Companies House in the UK, or your state's business register in the US. A limited company's name can't be the same as one already registered.
  • Trade marks: the UK IPO and USPTO registers. Registering a company doesn't stop someone else owning the name as a trade mark.
  • A domain and social handles, so customers can find you under the same name everywhere.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, read our guide, Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for a boutique

  • Think about the sign. A boutique name is read from across the street, so keep it short.
  • Online too? Make sure the name works as a web address and a handle as well as over the door.
  • Add "Boutique" only when you need to: if the rest of the name could mean anything, it tells people what you sell.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good boutique names?
Short, stylish names that feel personal: The Gilded Rail, Wren & Twine, The Edie Edit or Chez Mathilde. A woman's name, the building or your street also work well for a high-street shop.
+How do I check a boutique name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it is free as a company name, a web address and a social media handle. In the UK, search the Companies House register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Do this before you pay for a logo, labels or signage.
+Should a boutique name include the word "boutique"?
Only if the rest of the name could mean anything. Chez Margot or Hanger Management already sound like a clothes shop, so they can stand alone; a plain name like Ivy works better as Ivy Boutique, which is also easier to find in a map search and more likely to be free as a handle.
+What does 'chez' mean in a shop name?
'At the home of' in French, so Chez Mathilde means 'at Mathilde's'. It gives a boutique a personal, welcoming feel.
