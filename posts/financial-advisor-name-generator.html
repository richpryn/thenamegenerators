How advice firms are named

People are trusting you with their savings, so names lean towards stability: surnames, places, trees and birds, and words like Planning, Wealth and Advice. "Wealth Management" suggests investment management for larger portfolios; "Financial Planning" suits advice on pensions, savings and retirement. Avoid anything that promises returns or sounds like a big bank.

Regulated words: UK firms giving financial advice must be authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority. Under FCA rules, a firm may only describe its advice as "independent" if it covers the whole market without bias or restriction. In UK company names, "Bank", "Trust", "Fund", "Chartered", "Insurance", "Assurance" and "Society" are sensitive words that need approval. In the US, investment advisers register with the SEC or their state, and "CFP" and "Certified Financial Planner" are certification marks for those certified by the CFP Board.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Every name here has a Check button. Press it before you order business cards, and run through these yourself:

  • The company register: Companies House in the UK, or your state's business register in the US.
  • Trade marks: search the UK IPO or USPTO for the same or a similar name in your field.
  • Domains and handles: a matching .co.uk or .com, and the same handle on the platforms your clients use.
  • The FCA register (UK): search it for firms with similar names, and make sure your trading name is listed once you're authorised.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good financial advisor business names?
Calm, trustworthy names: a surname with Financial Planning (Wheeler Financial Planning), a steady word (Osprey Financial Planning), or a plain promise of clear advice (Money, Plainly). Avoid anything that promises returns.
+What should I name my financial advisory business?
Most advisers use their surname with "Financial Planning" or "Wealth Management", because clients are trusting a person with their savings. A town or a calm, steady word ("Kestrel Wealth", "Second Spring Financial Planning") works too. Avoid anything that sounds like a promise of returns, and anything close to a big bank or wealth manager.
+Which words can't a financial firm use in its name?
In the UK, "Bank", "Trust", "Fund", "Chartered", "Insurance", "Assurance" and "Society" are sensitive words that need approval before Companies House will register them, and under FCA rules a firm may only call its advice "independent" if it covers the whole market without restriction. In the US, "CFP" and "Certified Financial Planner" are certification marks for those certified by the CFP Board. The names here leave these out. You also need FCA authorisation (UK) or SEC or state registration (US) to give advice.
+How do I check a financial advisor name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as two similar names nearby confuse customers.
