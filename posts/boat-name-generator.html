A boat name gets painted on the hull, said over the radio and shouted across the harbour, so it has to sound right out loud. How boat names work

Most boat names follow one of a few old habits:

  • A person. Boats have long been called "she", and plenty are named after a wife, mother or daughter. In the West Country it's common to name a fishing boat Girl, Boy or Our followed by a name, so Girl Rosie or Our Tom.
  • A pun. Sea words bend easily: reel and real, knot and not, sea and see, pier and peer. Reel Therapy, Knot on Call and Pier Pressure are favourites for a reason. Plenty of boat puns have a rude second meaning, so this generator leaves those out.
  • The water itself. Wind, tide, birds and stars give sailing names like Restless Gull or Song of the Isles.
  • A place. Adding a harbour town, as in Aurelia of Fowey, turns a common name into one nobody else has.

Short names are easiest to paint, read from the bank and say clearly over a radio. Two or three words is plenty.

Narrowboat and canal boat names

Canal boat names tend to be gentler than seagoing ones. Wildlife and wildflowers are popular (Heron's Rest, Lazy Otter), as are the double first names of old working boats (Ellen May) and jokes about the pace of life. Most Canal & River Trust canals have a 4mph speed limit, which is why names like Four Miles an Hour make boaters smile. Gongoozler, canal slang for someone who stands and watches the boats go by, is a nice one too.

On Canal & River Trust waters, the licence terms say the boat's name, index number and licence must be shown on both sides of the boat, where staff on the towpath can see them. Pick a name that's short enough to paint clearly twice.

If your boat flies a skull and crossbones, try the Pirate Name Generator, which also names pirate ships.

Is it bad luck to rename a boat?

Sailors' superstition says so. The story goes that the sea god keeps a record of every boat's name, and a renamed boat confuses him. The traditional fix is a renaming ceremony:

  • Take the old name off everything first: the hull, life rings, paperwork and the log. Many owners keep the old log ashore rather than destroying it.
  • Hold a short ceremony asking for the old name to be struck from the records, then a second one for the new name.
  • Pour some champagne over the bow (and share the rest), and don't bring anything with the new name aboard until it's done.

It's tradition rather than a rule, but it's a fun excuse to gather friends at the marina.

Check the name before you paint it

The names here are free to use. Before you pay for lettering, check a few things:

  • Registering the boat? On Part 1 of the UK Ship Register, names must be unique and can't sound the same as another registered vessel's name, and offensive or confusing names can be refused. The UK Ship Register has a name check, and lets you reserve a name for three months.
  • Your mooring or marina. Two boats with the same name on one pontoon causes muddles with post and radio calls.
  • Read it out loud. If it sounds like something else when shouted across the water, the harbour will notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are some funny boat names?
Puns on sea words work best: Reel Therapy, Seas the Day, Knot on Call or Pier Pressure. Owner jokes are funny too, like Dad's Escape Plan. Choose Funny for more.
+What should I name my fishing boat?
Choose Fishing boat for names like Tight Lines, Bass Whisperer or Morning Cast. You can also follow the West Country habit of Girl, Boy or Our and a family name, like Girl Rosie or Our Tom.
+What are good narrowboat names?
Gentle ones with canal wildlife or an easy pace: Kingfisher Dawn, Heron's Rest, Lazy Otter or Gongoozler. Choose Narrowboat, and keep it short enough to paint on both sides.
+How do I name a boat after my wife or partner?
Type their name in the box and choose From a name. You'll get names like Lady Sarah Rose, Sarah's Pride, Spirit of Sarah or Sarah of Salcombe.
+What are good names for a sailing boat or yacht?
Wind, tide and seabirds suit a sailing boat: Fair Winds, Restless Gull or Song of the Isles. Choose Sailing & yacht, or Classic for elegant names like Halcyon and Aurelia of Fowey.
+Is it unlucky to change a boat's name?
Only by sailors' superstition. If you rename one, the tradition is to remove the old name from everything aboard and hold a renaming ceremony first.
+Can I use these boat names?
Yes, they're free to use. If you're registering the boat, check the name is available with the register first.
