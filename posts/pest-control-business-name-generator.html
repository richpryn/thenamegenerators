What makes a pest control name work

Customers call you when they're worried, so the name should calm them down:

  • Promise the outcome: clear, free, safe or protected, rather than dwelling on the pest.
  • Professional names (Benchmark, Bastion, Sentinel) suit commercial clients such as restaurants and offices, who need a contractor they can list on an audit.
  • Puns work on vans and leaflets for homeowners, but keep them light.
  • Old trade titles like "mole catcher" and "rat catcher" are still searched for, especially in the countryside.

In the UK, membership of the British Pest Control Association (BPCA) tells customers you meet an industry standard. In the US, pesticide applicators are licensed by each state, so check your state's rules before you advertise.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Press Check next to any name, or paste it into the Business Name Checker, to see whether the domain and social handles are free. Then search the company register (Companies House in the UK, your state's business register in the US) and the trade mark register for your kind of business. If you'll bid for commercial work, a limited company name that matches your website looks more established on a tender.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for pest controllers

  • Name the main pest if you specialise: wasp, mole or bed bug services are searched for directly.
  • Unmarked vans: some customers prefer discreet visits, so offer a plain vehicle option even if your brand is fun.
  • Keep it reassuring on your website, too: a calm name loses its effect next to alarming photos.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good pest control business names?
Reassuring names that promise the result, such as All Clear Pest Control or Benchmark Pest Control, or a friendly pun like Pest in Show. Choose Professional for names that suit commercial clients.
+How do I check a pest control business name is free?
Press Check next to any name to see if it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. Then make sure: in the UK, search Companies House and the UK IPO trade mark register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. A quick map search for the name in your area helps too.
+What are some funny pest control names?
Funny pest control names usually swap “best” for “pest” or play on bugs and buzzing: Pest Kept Secret, The Bug Stops Here, Mice to Meet You or Holy Moley. Keep the pun light so customers still trust you with their home. Choose Funny in the Style menu for more.
+Should a pest control name be funny or serious?
Serious names win commercial contracts; a gentle pun can work for homeowners. Many firms use a professional name and save the jokes for the van.
