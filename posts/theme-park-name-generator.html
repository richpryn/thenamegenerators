How the Theme Park Name Generator Works

How real parks are named

  • Pleasure gardens came first. London's 18th-century Vauxhall Gardens had music, lights and walks, and the name lived on in seaside parks.
  • Blackpool Pleasure Beach, founded in 1896, kept the old word "pleasure". Seaside parks across Britain followed with piers, waltzers and scenic railways.
  • Disneyland, which opened in California in 1955, split the park into themed lands, such as Frontierland and Tomorrowland. That's why so many parks now name their zones as well as the park itself.
  • Thrill parks name themselves after speed, height or danger, and their rides after weather and wild animals.

Pick a theme first, then a name in the same style: a fantasy kingdom, a sci-fi park or a spooky fairground. Choose With a famous ride to name your first big coaster at the same time.
