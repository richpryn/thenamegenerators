How the Car Detailing Business Name Generator Works

It works from 925 names and name parts, which combine into over 2,000,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, Catchy, Funny, Classic, Modern, short, Luxury, Mobile, From my town, From my name.
  • Your town: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Hampton").
  • Your first name: type a name and it's built into every result (leave it blank to get "Jay").
  • Format:
    • Name only, e.g. The Old Mill Car Care Company
    • Name with a slogan, e.g. The Ashcombe Detailing Studio – Interiors refreshed, paint perfected

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a car detailing business name is free?
Press Check next to any name to see if it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. Then make sure: in the UK, search Companies House and the UK IPO trade mark register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. A quick map search for the name in your area helps too.
+What's a good name for a mobile car detailing business?
Tell people you come to them: Shine on Wheels, Doorstep Valeting or Detail on the Go. In the UK, “mobile valeting” is the phrase customers search; in the US it's “mobile detailing”. Choose Mobile in the Style menu for more.
