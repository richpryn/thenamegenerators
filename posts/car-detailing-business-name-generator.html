Valeting or detailing?

In the UK, valeting usually means a thorough clean, inside and out. Detailing goes further: paint correction to remove swirls and scratches, and protection such as wax or ceramic coatings. In the US, detailing covers both. Use the word for the work you do, because customers search for it.

  • Studio names sound like a workshop: Pearson Detail Studio.
  • Luxury names borrow smart places and words like Diamond or Imperial, for paint protection on high-value cars.
  • Mobile names promise convenience: Detail on the Go, The Detail Van. Choose Mobile in the Style menu.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A detailing name ends up on a van, an Instagram grid and a lot of shiny photos, so it's worth a few minutes' checking before anything is printed:

  • Press Check on any name above. The Business Name Checker looks up the domain, links to the company register (Companies House in the UK) and opens the trade mark search and social handles.
  • Search where you'll trade. Look the name up on maps, marketplaces and social media. Two businesses with the same name confuse customers and reviews.
  • If you go mobile, say so in the name or slogan: people search for mobile valeting near them.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, from shortlisting to registering, read our guide to Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for a detailing brand

  • Make it work as a handle. Detailing is sold on Instagram and TikTok, so a short name that's free as a handle is worth more than a long clever one.
  • Mind the water. Mobile detailers shouldn't let dirty wash water into surface-water drains, which usually lead to streams and rivers.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good car detailing business names?
Names that sound like a great finish, like Glass & Gloss or Henley Ceramic Coatings, puns such as Interior Motives Car Care, or mobile names like Valet to You. Choose a style for more.
+What's a good name for a mobile car detailing business?
Tell people you come to them: Shine on Wheels, Doorstep Valeting or Detail on the Go. In the UK, “mobile valeting” is the phrase customers search; in the US it's “mobile detailing”. Choose Mobile in the Style menu for more.
+What's the difference between valeting and detailing?
In the UK, valeting is a thorough clean inside and out. Detailing goes further, with paint correction and protection such as ceramic coatings. In the US, detailing covers both.
+How do I check a car detailing business name is free?
Press Check next to any name to see if it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. Then make sure: in the UK, search Companies House and the UK IPO trade mark register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. A quick map search for the name in your area helps too.
