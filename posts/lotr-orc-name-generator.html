The Black Speech

Sauron devised the Black Speech for his servants, and the Ring's inscription is the best-known line of it: Ash nazg durbatulûk, "one ring to rule them all". A few words are clear enough to borrow:

  • nazg, ring, as in the Nazgûl, the Ring-wraiths.
  • uruk, orc. The Uruk-hai are the "orc-folk".
  • snaga, slave: what the big orcs call the small ones.
  • ghâsh, fire, and Lugbúrz, the orcs' name for Barad-dûr, the Dark Tower.

Tolkien also wrote that orcs of different tribes could barely understand each other's dialects, so they used the Common Speech among themselves. That's why their names are a mix: some Black Speech, some just ugly.

Tolkien's orcs and their names

  • Shagrat, captain of the Tower of Cirith Ungol, and Gorbag of Minas Morgul, who fight over Frodo's mithril shirt.
  • Uglúk, leader of Saruman's Uruk-hai, and Grishnákh, the Mordor orc who tries to steal the hobbits for himself.
  • Azog and his son Bolg, goblins of the Misty Mountains, and Golfimbul, whose head Bandobras Took knocked off with a club, inventing golf.

None of these are in the generator, so your orc won't be confused with one of them.

Three kinds of orc

  • Mordor orcs serve Sauron directly, from the Tower, the Black Gate and the vale of Udûn.
  • Uruk-hai of Isengard are Saruman's: bigger, stronger, unafraid of the sun, and loud about it. They carry the White Hand.
  • Goblins of the Misty Mountains live in tunnels at Gundabad, Moria and Goblin-town. In The Hobbit Tolkien calls them goblins; it's the same creature.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Mordor orcs, Uruk-hai and goblins?

Mordor orcs serve Sauron; the Uruk-hai are Saruman's larger orcs from Isengard, who can march in daylight; goblins are the orcs of the Misty Mountains, called goblins in The Hobbit. Pick the kind and the rank and place will suit.

Why do some names have û or á?

Tolkien used accents to mark long vowels, and you'll see them in Black Speech words like Lugbúrz and durbatulûk. A few of the names here use them the same way.

Are these names from the books?

No. They're built from Black Speech-style sounds, and the names of Tolkien's own orcs are left out.

Can I use these names in my game or story?
