The Old West ran on nicknames. Henry McCarty became Billy the Kid, James Butler Hickok became Wild Bill, and Martha Jane Cannary became Calamity Jane. This Old West name generator uses first names common in 19th-century America, with a surname and the kind of nickname that followed people from town to town.

Real names behind the legends

  • Billy the Kid was born Henry McCarty, and also used the name William H. Bonney.
  • Wild Bill Hickok was James Butler Hickok.
  • Calamity Jane was Martha Jane Cannary.
  • Butch Cassidy was Robert LeRoy Parker; his partner, the Sundance Kid, was Harry Longabaugh, nicknamed after the jail in Sundance, Wyoming.
  • Annie Oakley, the sharpshooter, was born Phoebe Ann Mosey.

A nickname could hide a past, make a reputation or both, which is why so many outlaws had several.

Making your western name

  • The nickname does the work. Two-Gun, Deadeye and Quickdraw make a reputation; Lefty and Dusty make a character.
  • Plain first names fit the period: Eli, Wade and Silas; Etta, Hattie and Pearl.
  • Give it a story. How did Lucky get the name, and is it still true?

Frequently Asked Questions

What was Billy the Kid's real name?

Henry McCarty. He also used the name William H. Bonney.

How did the Sundance Kid get his name?

Harry Longabaugh was jailed in Sundance, Wyoming, as a young man, and the nickname stuck.

What are good cowboy nicknames?

Short and descriptive: Two-Gun, Deadeye, Lucky, Dusty, Lefty, Tex. Use "First name and nickname" for names like Lucky Etta.
