How the Goliath Name Generator Works

It works from 1,338 names and name parts, which combine into over 1,100,000,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Gender: Any, Male, Female.
  • Name format:
    • Full name, e.g. Kalothea "Anvilsplitter" Peka-Ilaro
    • Birth name only, e.g. Menea
    • Nickname only, e.g. Loud-Laugh
    • Clan name only, e.g. Selalumivai
  • Starts with: depends on the name format:
    • Full name and Birth name only: Any letter, A, E, G, H, I, K, L, M, N, O, P, R, S, T, U, V
    • Nickname only: Any letter, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, L, N, O, P, R, S, T, W
    • Clan name only: Any letter, A, E, H, I, K, L, M, N, O, P, R, S, T, U, V

Where the names come from: Every name is invented. Birth names and clan names are built from short sound parts in the style the D&D goliath writeups describe: short birth names, long clan names ending in a vowel. Nicknames are new deed-names in plain English. None is an example name from the books, and well-known fan characters are left out.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Goliaths are the mountain-dwelling, giant-blooded people of Dungeons & Dragons, and each one carries three names: one from their parents, one from their chief and one from their clan. This goliath name generator builds all three together, or any part on its own. It's a fan-made, unofficial page: D&D and its goliaths belong to Wizards of the Coast, and every name here is new.

  • Ikamei: a female birth name with the open vowel ending goliath names often have.

  • Hailcatcher: short, odd and memorable, which is what a good nickname should be.
  • Hulokathimathi: a clan name in the long, rolling style, ending in a vowel.

How goliath names work

The fifth-edition goliath writeups, first in the Elemental Evil Player's Companion (2015) and then in Volo's Guide to Monsters (2016), give every goliath three names:

  • A birth name, chosen by the parents. It's short, up to three syllables, and the books don't split birth names into male and female.
  • A nickname, given by the tribal chief. It describes the goliath, and it can change during their life as they do new things.
  • A clan name, which is much longer and ends in a vowel.

Written out, the nickname sits in the middle, usually in quotation marks: Thorakan "Hailcatcher" Hulokathimathi. Because birth names aren't gendered in the books, the Male and Female choices here go by sound only: male names end on hard sounds like -vak and -ruk, female names on a vowel. Use Any if you'd rather not choose.

What a nickname says about your goliath

Goliaths are described as fiercely competitive: they keep score of their own deeds and expect a fair contest. The nickname is where that shows. It's the name other people actually use, and it's a running record of what the goliath did last.

  • Deed names like Stonethrower, Cragclimber or Elkcaller mark one moment the tribe remembers.
  • Habit names like Late-Riser, Goat-Counter or Slow-to-Anger come from living alongside someone for years.
  • Character names like Unbowed, Level-Headed or Soft-Spoken are what the chief thinks of them.

Since the chief can change it, the nickname is also a ready-made story arc. Choose the nickname your goliath has now, then choose the one they want to earn, and play towards it.

Giant ancestry and sound

In the 2024 Player's Handbook, goliaths choose a giant ancestry (cloud, fire, frost, hill, stone or storm), which gives them a boon such as Frost's Chill or Stone's Endurance. It doesn't change how the name is built, but it's a good guide when you pick a nickname: a frost-blooded goliath suits Hailcatcher or Glacierwalker, a storm-blooded one Thundersinger.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do goliath names work in D&D?
A goliath has a short birth name from their parents, a nickname from their chief and a long clan name, in that order. Choose Full name (three parts) to get all three, like Haragun "Stonethrower" Vela-Anaki.
+What are some good female goliath names?
Choose Female and Birth name only for names like Ikamei, Arilani or Vekalani. The books don't split goliath birth names by gender, so any birth name here works for any goliath.
+What is a goliath nickname?
It's the name a goliath's chief gives them, describing a deed, a habit or their character, and it can change over their life. Choose Nickname only for ones like Twice-Lucky, Mule-Talker or Peakwatcher.
+What are good goliath clan names?
Long names that end in a vowel, like Hulokathimathi or Ravanekanua, or hyphenated ones like Vela-Anaki. Choose Clan name only for more.
+Are these names from the Player's Handbook?
No. They're new names in the same style, and the example names from the D&D books are left out, so your goliath won't share a name with one from a sourcebook.
+Can I make a goliath name that starts with a certain letter?
Yes. Use Starts with to choose the letter; it works on the birth name for full names, and on the nickname or clan name when you choose those formats.
