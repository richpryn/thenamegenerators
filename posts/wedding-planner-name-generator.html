How wedding businesses name themselves

Couples are planning the most personal day of their lives, so wedding names lean on flowers, ribbons, vows and the language of the day itself: aisles, bouquets, confetti. French is the classic luxury touch: Belle Mariée means beautiful bride, Mon Amour my love, and Pour Toujours forever. Keep the French simple, so couples can say it and spell it.

Say what you offer, because couples search for it: full planning, on-the-day coordination, styling or destination weddings. A name ending in Weddings or Wedding Co. leaves room to grow into more services.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Every name here has a Check button, which opens the Business Name Checker: it looks up domains, social handles, the company register and trade mark searches in one place. Before you order signs or cards:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; a limited company's name can't be the same as one already registered. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK Intellectual Property Office or the USPTO for marks in your line of work. Registering a company doesn't give you trade mark rights.
  • Domain and handles. Try the .co.uk or .com and the main social handles at the same time, and adjust the name rather than settle for a messy handle.
  • Local search. Look the name up with your town, as two similar firms a few miles apart confuse clients and reviews.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good wedding planner names?
Romantic names that say what you do: Honeysuckle Wedding Planning, Knot & Bow Weddings, or a French luxury name like Belle Mariée (beautiful bride).
+Should I call my business wedding planning or wedding coordination?
Use the words for what you actually sell, because couples search for them. A wedding planner helps from the engagement to the day; a wedding coordinator (in the US often "day-of", in the UK "on the day") takes over in the last weeks and runs the day itself; a stylist does the look. If you might grow into full planning, a name ending in "Weddings" or "Wedding Co." leaves room.
+Should a wedding planner name be in French?
It suits a luxury business, but keep it short and easy to say: Belle Mariée or Mon Amour work; long phrases get misspelled. Add Weddings so couples know what you do.
+How do I check a wedding planner name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as two similar names nearby confuse customers.
