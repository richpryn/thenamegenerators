How pizza names work

  • Pizzeria is the Italian word for a pizza shop, and forno means oven: Il Forno is "the oven".
  • Family names like Rossi & Sons or Da Tonino ("at Tonino's") follow how real Italian pizzerias are often named.
  • Sourdough and wood-fired places suit short modern names about fire, flour and char.
  • Takeaways and vans can be punny, because people choose them from an app or a queue.

Choose Italian for names that use the words correctly, and Van for names that suit a converted bus or horsebox.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A name from a generator is a starting point, not permission to use it. Before you order the boxes, sign or van livery:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; you can't register a limited company with the same name as an existing one. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK trade mark register (or the USPTO in the US) for the goods and services you'll sell.
  • Domain and handles. Check the .co.uk or .com and the Instagram and TikTok handles you'll need.
  • Your area. Search a map app and the delivery apps for businesses with the same or a similar name nearby.

Press Check on any name, or use the Business Name Checker, to run most of these at once. For the full process, read our guide, Business Naming Best Practices. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

In the UK, any food business must register with the local council at least 28 days before it starts trading. Registration is free, and the council will inspect you and give you a food hygiene rating.

Tips for pizza businesses

Delivery apps sort by name and photo, so test how your name looks as a small tile next to the big chains. Avoid anything close to a national chain's name, which will be a trade mark.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good pizza shop names?
Names that look good on a box and are easy to find on delivery apps: Romano & Sons Pizzeria, Crust & Embers, One More Slice or Il Forno di Nunzia. Choose a style for catchy, classic, modern, Italian or van names.
+How do I check a pizza business name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Before you commit, search the official registers too: in the UK, Companies House and the UK trade mark register; in the US, your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Look on a map app for businesses with the same name nearby.
+What should I name my pizza shop?
Think about what you sell and who's buying. A family pizzeria often uses a name (Tony's Pizzeria, Rossi & Sons); a sourdough or wood-fired place suits something short (Flour & Fire); a takeaway or van can be punny (Pie Fidelity). Make sure it's easy to say on the phone and to find on delivery apps, where customers search by name.
+What does il forno mean?
"The oven" in Italian. A pizzeria is a pizza shop, and da before a name, as in Pizzeria da Tonino, means "at Tonino's".
