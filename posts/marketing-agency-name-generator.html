How agencies name themselves

Advertising agencies have long been named after their founders: Saatchi & Saatchi was set up by the brothers Charles and Maurice Saatchi, and Ogilvy & Mather carried David Ogilvy's name. A surname still signals a firm that stands behind its work. Newer agencies lean the other way, towards a single memorable word or a pair, like Otter & Thread or Magpie Media, which double as a logo idea.

The last word tells clients what you sell: Creative and Studio suggest design and branding, Media and Social suggest content and channels, Digital suggests SEO and paid search, and Agency or Group suggests a full team. Choose a kind of agency and the generator adds the matching words.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Every name here has a Check button, which opens the Business Name Checker: it looks up domains, social handles, the company register and trade mark searches in one place. Before you order signs or cards:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; a limited company's name can't be the same as one already registered. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK Intellectual Property Office or the USPTO for marks in your line of work. Registering a company doesn't give you trade mark rights.
  • Domain and handles. Try the .co.uk or .com and the main social handles at the same time, and adjust the name rather than settle for a messy handle.
  • Local search. Look the name up with your town, as two similar firms a few miles apart confuse clients and reviews.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Your agency name is also your first case study, so test it the way you'd test a client's: say it on a call, write it as a handle, and see if it still works as a short logo. Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good marketing agency names?
Names that are memorable and say what you do: a founders' name like Ormsby Creative, an idea like The Pitch Room, or something playful like Reply All Creative. Add Digital, Social or Creative if you specialise.
+How do I name a marketing agency?
Agencies usually pick one of three shapes: the founders' surnames ("Hartley & Rowe"), which sounds established; a short brandable name ("Magpie Studio", "Fox & Feather"), which shows creative flair; or a clever line about what you do ("Stop the Scroll Social"). Say "Digital", "Social" or "Creative" if you specialise, and make sure the name works as a handle, because clients will look you up on social media first.
+Should a marketing agency use the founders' names?
It suits a consultancy-style agency where clients hire the people, and it sounds established. A brandable word is easier to grow past the founders and to sell on later.
+How do I check a marketing agency name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as two similar names nearby confuse customers.
