How to rename a sword in Minecraft

  • Use an anvil. Put the sword in the first slot, click the name box at the top and type the new name.
  • Keep it to 50 characters. That's the limit in the Java Edition rename box. Every name here is well under it.
  • It costs experience. Renaming takes at least one level, and more if the sword has been worked on an anvil before. In Survival, anything that would cost 40 levels or more shows "Too Expensive!" instead.
  • Renamed items show in italics, so everyone can tell you named it yourself.

What makes a good funny sword name

  • Puns on mobs and blocks land best with Minecraft players: The Piglin Distractor, Creeper Sweeper, The Warden's Whimper.
  • Understatement is funnier on a strong sword: a fully enchanted netherite blade called The Mild Inconvenience or The Subtle Suggestion.
  • Film and meme jokes: Shank Redemption, Stabby McStabface, Bladey McBladeface (after the boat the public voted to call Boaty McBoatface).
  • Honesty: I Forgot My Bow, The Wrong Tool, The 1 HP Wonder.

Where a funny name works

  • PvP and minigames: a silly name in a kill message is the best kind of trash talk.
  • Survival with friends: name everyone's starter swords and see which survives to the End.
  • Not funny enough? For a serious blade, try the Sword Name Generator.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How long can a Minecraft item name be?
In the Java Edition, the anvil lets you type up to 50 characters. All the names here are shorter than that.
