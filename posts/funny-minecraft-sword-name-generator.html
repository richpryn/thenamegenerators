Frequently Asked Questions

How do I name my sword in Minecraft?

To name your sword in Minecraft, use an anvil. Place your sword in the first slot and a renamed item (or use the anvil's renaming feature) in the second slot. You can also use commands: /give @p minecraft:diamond_sword{display:{Name:'{"text":"The Tickle Stick"}'}} (Java Edition) or rename it directly in an anvil interface.

Will funny sword names show up in death messages?

Yes! When you kill another player with a named sword, the sword's name appears in the death message.
