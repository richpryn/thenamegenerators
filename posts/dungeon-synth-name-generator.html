Dungeon synth is music for imagined places: castles, crypts, forests and far-off realms, played on synths and keyboards, often recorded alone at home. The names do half the work of setting the scene. This dungeon synth name generator has names for projects and albums in four styles, from Throne of Mists to Lantern Vale.

Where dungeon synth comes from

The sound grew out of the 1990s black metal scene, when musicians made atmospheric keyboard side projects between metal albums. Mortiis, who had played bass in Emperor, is the name most often cited from those early years, and Summoning from Austria built their atmosphere around Tolkien.

The name came much later: "dungeon synth" was coined around 2011 on Andy Werth's blog of the same name, which gave a scattered scene a label to gather under. Since then it has grown into a large community, mostly releasing on Bandcamp and on cassette, with projects like Old Tower and Erang among its best known.

Four styles of name

  • Dark and medieval: keeps, crypts and fortresses. Gravenhelm, Sunless Throne, Ashen Vigil. The classic sound.
  • Nature and fantasy: forests, glades and old magic. Lichenwood, Moonveil Glade, Elderglen.
  • Cosy (comfy synth): the warmer, gentler offshoot, all hearths and hollows. Hearthgrove, Candlewick Keep, Mellowbrook.
  • Cosmic: stars, voids and celestial realms. Astral Haven, Nebula's Edge, Moonfall Haven.

How dungeon synth projects are named

  • A place. Many projects are named as if they were a location: a tower, a keep, a realm. The music then becomes a visit there. Old Tower is a good example.
  • A borrowed world. Tolkien is everywhere in the genre: the German project Thangorodrim takes its name from Morgoth's mountains. If you borrow, borrow deep cuts.
  • An invented word. A single strange name (Mortiis, Erang) leaves the world entirely up to the music.
  • Albums as chapters. Album titles often read like places or episodes in the same imagined world, so a project and its albums can share a map.

Before you release

  • Search Bandcamp, Discogs and the streaming services for the name. The scene is large and full of keeps and towers, so a quick search saves a rename later.
  • Check it's searchable. A name that's also a common phrase is hard for listeners to find; adding a word or an unusual spelling helps.
  • Think about the cover. Dungeon synth is a visual genre (old maps, woodcuts, pixel art). A name that suggests an image makes the artwork easier.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is dungeon synth?

A genre of atmospheric, mostly instrumental synth music with a medieval or fantasy feel. It grew from the ambient side projects of 1990s black metal musicians, and the name was coined around 2011.

What is comfy synth?

A warmer, gentler offshoot of dungeon synth: cosy forests, hearths and villages instead of crypts and fortresses. Choose "Cosy (comfy synth)" for names in that style.

Can I use these names for my project?

Yes, they're free to use and you don't need to credit the site. Search Bandcamp, Discogs and the streaming services first to make sure nobody's using the name already.
