Most motorcycle clubs are groups of friends who like a good road with a café at the end of it. This motorcycle club name generator makes names for riding clubs, touring groups, women's riding groups, charity ride-outs and vintage bike clubs, plus funny names and names built from your town.

  • Far Horizons Tourers: tells people this club goes a long way, not just to the next café.

  • Hampton and District Motorcycle Club: your town plus the old club habit of "and District", which welcomes riders from the villages around.

How club names work

Good club names come from a few places:

  • An emblem. Birds and animals make easy badges: Kestrel, Golden Hare, Silver Fox, Barn Owl.
  • The roads you ride. Ridgeway Riders, Coast Road Riders or B-Road Riders say where you'd rather be.
  • The bikes. Throttle, kickstand, chrome and magneto turn up in plenty of names, and older parts suit classic clubs.
  • A place. A town or county in the name makes the club easy to find for new riders nearby.
  • A joke. Café stops, rain and getting lost are part of every club's story, so names like The Bacon Roll Patrol or Lost Again Touring Club land well.

The generator keeps to friendly clubs. It leaves out famous club names and the skulls, devils and outlaw words that belong to a very different kind of club.

Riding club, touring club or motorcycle club?

In parts of the biking world, "MC" and back patches carry a specific meaning. A three-piece patch (the club name on a curved top rocker, a centre logo and the area on a bottom rocker) is linked with traditional motorcycle clubs, and some of those clubs take it seriously when a new group wears one.

That's why many social groups call themselves a riding club, a touring club or simply "Riders", and use a single badge instead. Before you print back patches or call your group an MC, ask riders at local bike nights and clubs what's usual in your area. A friendly word first saves awkward moments later.

Women's groups, charity rides and classic clubs

  • Women's riding groups often pick names with a bit of attitude and alliteration, like Throttle Belles or Kickstand Queens, or a place name, like Women of the Open Road.
  • Charity rides do best with a name that says what they're for. Christmas toy runs, where riders take presents to children's hospitals and charities, are a big tradition in the UK, mostly on the first two weekends of December.
  • Vintage and classic clubs can borrow the language of old bikes: girder forks, magnetos, flat tanks and big singles.

If your club meets in running shoes instead, try the Running Club Name Generator.

Check the name before you print the patches

The names here are free to use. Before you order badges, shirts or a banner:

  • Search Facebook, local listings and club directories to make sure a club near you doesn't already use the name.
  • Make sure your badge doesn't copy another club's logo or colours.
  • If you'll sell merchandise, check the UK IPO or USPTO trade mark databases too. This isn't legal advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What is a good name for a motorcycle club?
Something short with an emblem or a road in it, like Red Kite Riders, Golden Hare Riding Club or Ridgeway Riders. Choose Riding club for more.
+What are good names for a women's motorcycle group?
Try Throttle Belles, Kickstand Queens or Women of the Open Road. Choose Women's for more, or Your town for names like Hampton Ladies Riding Club.
+What are some funny motorcycle club names?
Jokes about café stops, rain and getting lost work best: Pothole Dodgers, The Bacon Roll Patrol or Sat Nav Says No. Choose Funny.
+What should I call a charity ride-out?
Say what it's for: Goodwill Ride-Out, Ride for Smiles or Kind Hearts Toy Run. Choose Charity, then add the cause or your town to the poster.
+What is a good name for a classic or vintage bike club?
Old bike words suit them: Flat Tank Club, Girder Fork Classic Riders or The Brass and Chrome Society. Choose Vintage.
+Should my club call itself an MC?
Many friendly groups use Riding Club or Riders instead, because "MC" and back patches can mean something specific to other clubs. Ask locally before you print patches.
+How do I make a motorcycle club name with my town in it?
Type your town in the box and choose Your town. You'll get names like Hampton Riders, Hampton Classic Bike Club or Kestrel Riders of Hampton.
