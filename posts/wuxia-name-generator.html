How the Wuxia Name Generator Works

It works from 6,000 names and name parts, which combine into over 95,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Gender:
    • Any, e.g. Nangong Jun
    • Male, e.g. Sun Yun
    • Female, e.g. Shen Qiu
  • Name format: Name only, With its meaning, With a title, With a nickname.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

Wuxia names follow Chinese order, surname first, and the genre loves rare compound surnames like Murong and Ouyang. This wuxia name generator pairs real Chinese surnames with given names made from real words, like Yun (cloud) and Xue (snow), with their meanings, a sect title or a jianghu nickname.
