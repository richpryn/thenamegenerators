How the Pony Name Generator Works

It works from 3,712 names and name parts, which combine into over 470,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Kind of pony: Any kind, Earth pony, Pegasus, Unicorn, Alicorn, Bat pony, Sea pony, Crystal pony, Real pet pony.
  • Mare or stallion: Either, Mare or filly, Stallion or colt.
  • Name format: Either, Name only, With a talent.
  • Starts with: Any letter, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, L, M, N, O, P, R, S, T, V, W.

Where the names come from: Every name is invented. Names are made by putting together ordinary English words for food, weather, gems, stars and the sea, chosen to suit each kind of pony. They aren't taken from the shows, films or toys, and every name is checked against the official ponies. Real pet pony names are common, traditional pet names.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Pony names in the style of My Little Pony are two bright words that hint at what the pony loves: a baker might be called Maple Cobbler and a racing pegasus Kestrel Swoop. This pony name generator makes original names like these for earth ponies, pegasi, unicorns, alicorns, bat ponies, sea ponies and crystal ponies, and can add a talent and cutie mark to match. There are names for real ponies too, from Shetlands to riding ponies. It's a fan-made, unofficial tool, not affiliated with Hasbro, and it never gives you the name of a pony from the shows, films or toys.

How pony names work

Most pony names in this style are two words. The first is a thing the pony loves or is like: a food, a flower, a gem, a bird or a star. The second is a lively word for movement, light or sound, such as Swoop, Ripple, Twinkle or Hum. Put together, they read like a short description: Maple Cobbler bakes, Kestrel Swoop flies fast.

The best names say something about the pony's talent. If you know what your pony is good at, start there and work outwards. A pony who loves books might be Sonnet Page; one who mends things might be Iron Mallet. Say the name out loud too. Two words with different first sounds, or a gentle rhyme, are easier to remember than two that clash.

Names for each kind of pony

The generator uses different words for each kind, so the name fits how the pony lives:

  • Earth ponies: food, farming, gardens and crafts. Maple Pudding, Tulip Trellis, Patchwork Purl.
  • Pegasi: weather, sky, birds and speed. Bluebird Breeze, Stratus Shower, Swift Streak.
  • Unicorns: magic, stars, books and fine fabric. Stellar Shower, Wonder Whisper, Cashmere Ruffle.
  • Alicorns: dawn, light, seasons and royal words, sometimes with Princess or Prince in front. Solstice Lustre, Aurora Dawn, Prince Sirius Shine.
  • Bat ponies: night, quiet, caves, fruit and moths. Sable Wing, Ember Hush, Fig Hollow.
  • Sea ponies: shells, tides, reefs and rock pools. Coral Cove, Reef Ripple, Sandpiper Shell.
  • Crystal ponies: gems, frost and things that catch the light. Sapphire Sheen, Citrine Flare, Onyx Facet.

Choose Mare or Stallion to steer the words a little softer or a little bolder. Plenty of names work for either, so Any is often the best place to start.

Talents and cutie marks

Choose With a talent and each name comes with something the pony is good at and a cutie mark to show it: Syrup Crumb is a baker with an iced bun, Sprint Spin does aerobatics with a looping arrow. The talent matches the kind of pony, so pegasi get flying and weather jobs, unicorns get magic and study, and sea ponies get things like tidying rock pools.

A cutie mark works best as one simple picture you could draw in a few lines. If you're designing your own, pick an object linked to the talent rather than the talent itself: a watering can for gardening, a telescope for astronomy.

Names for real ponies

A real pony needs a name you can call across a field, so short names of one or two syllables work best: Bramble, Pepper, Smudge, Hamish. Food names are a long British favourite for ponies (Fudge, Nutmeg, Treacle), and names from the countryside suit a native breed (Bracken, Heather, Thistle).

Small, cheeky ponies such as Shetlands often get grand names for fun, like Duchess Mustard or Sir Waffle. Show and breed-society ponies are usually registered under a longer formal name, often with the breeder's stud prefix, and called something short at home. If you're registering a pony, check the society's rules, as many limit the length of a name and won't accept one that's already in use.

Using your pony name

The names are free to use for an OC, a story, a game or a real pony. They're original, and the generator checks each one against the ponies from the shows, films and toys, so you won't be handed an official character's name. Single words like Sparkle or Breeze are ordinary English, though, so it's worth a quick search to make sure your full name isn't already a well-known fan character.

If you plan to use a name for a product, shop or brand, check that it's available first. My Little Pony is a Hasbro trademark, and this site is a fan-made tool with no link to Hasbro.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I make a name for my MLP OC?
Start with what your pony loves, then pick two words about it: a bat pony who loves fruit could be Fig Flutter, a crafty earth pony Copper Rafter. Choose a kind of pony and With a talent to get a cutie mark that matches. Every name here is checked against the ponies from the shows and toys, so your OC stays your own.
+What is a good name for a real pony?
Something short and friendly that's easy to call across a field, like Bramble, Nutmeg, Hamish or Dotty. Choose Real pet pony for names like these, or Mare or Stallion to narrow them down. Shetlands suit cheeky names like Sir Waffle; show ponies are often registered with a longer name and called something short at home.
+Are these names from My Little Pony?
No. They're original names written in the same style, and none of them is the name of a pony from the shows, films or toys. The site is fan-made and unofficial, and isn't affiliated with Hasbro.
+What's the difference between a pegasus, a unicorn and an alicorn?
In the My Little Pony style, a pegasus has wings and can fly, a unicorn has a horn and can do magic, and an alicorn has both. Earth ponies have neither but are strong and good with plants and crafts. The generator uses different words for each, so a pegasus gets sky words and a unicorn gets magic ones.
+Why do some names start with Princess or Prince?
Alicorns are the royal ponies of the style, so the generator sometimes gives them a title, like Prince Sirius Shine. Real pet ponies can get one too, just for fun: Princess Betty or Duchess Piccalilli suits a small pony with a big personality.
+What is a cutie mark?
In My Little Pony, a cutie mark is the picture on a pony's flank that shows its special talent. Choose With a talent and the generator gives each name a talent and a cutie mark to match, such as baking with an iced bun.
+Can I use these names for my pony or my OC?
Yes, they're free to use. If you want a name for a shop, product or brand, check it isn't already taken before you commit.
