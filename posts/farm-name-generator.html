How the Farm Name Generator Works

It works from 2,031 names and name parts, which combine into over 58,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, British, Ranch, Cosy and cute, Funny, Fantasy.
  • Name Format:
    • Name only, e.g. Startled Alpaca Farm
    • With a description, e.g. W Lazy A Ranch, where the cattle outnumber the people a thousand to one

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Good farm names usually come from the land, an animal or a family, like Willow Beck Farm or Honeypot Farm, or a pun if you're feeling cheerful. Every good farm needs a name on the gate. This farm name generator gives you cosy and cute names for Stardew Valley and other farming games, British farms, American ranches, punny farms and fantasy farms, each with an optional line about the place.

How farms get their names

  • British farms are often named after their place in the parish. Home Farm was the farm that supplied the big house, and Manor Farm belonged to the manor. George Orwell's Animal Farm begins at a Manor Farm.
  • Old words for the land fill British farm names: croft (a small enclosed field), brow (the brow of a hill), carr (wet woodland), fold (an animal pen) and -stead (a farmstead). In Scotland a steading is the farm buildings.
  • American ranches take the name of their cattle brand, read aloud: Flying C is a C with wings, Rocking J a J on a rocker, and C Bar A two letters with a bar between.

Choose a style: British, Ranch, Cosy and cute, Funny or Fantasy. Choose With a description for a line about the place.

Naming your farm in a game

  • In Stardew Valley and other farming games, you name your farm when you start a new game, so it's worth picking one you'll like for a hundred in-game seasons.
  • Match the name to your plans: a cosy name for a crop farm, a funny one for a farm full of animals.
  • Keep it short if the game shows it on screen often.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What should I name my farm in Stardew Valley?
Something cosy you'll be happy to see for a long time, like Butterhollow Farm or Honeypot Farm, or a joke like Unimpressed Cauliflower Farm. Choose Cosy and cute or Funny for names like these.
+What are some traditional British farm names?
Names from the land and the parish: Home Farm, Manor Farm, Hill Farm, or a field word like croft, brow or fold, as in Redcroft Farm. Choose British for names built that way.
+How are ranches named?
Many American ranches take the name of their cattle brand, read aloud, like the Flying C or the Rocking J. Choose Ranch for names like these.
