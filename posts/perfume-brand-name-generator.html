A good perfume brand name sounds like a house, a place or a feeling, and is easy to say at a counter: a French word, a founder's surname, or a note like oud or vetiver. Launching a fragrance line, a perfume oil range or a decant shop?   • Mandarin by Sarah: your name with a note.

Perfume words worth knowing

  • Strengths: from strongest to lightest, parfum (or extrait), eau de parfum, eau de toilette and eau de cologne. Each has a lower share of perfume oil than the one before.
  • Eau de Cologne takes its name from the German city of Cologne, where it was made in the 1700s.
  • Notes: top notes are what you smell first, heart notes come next and base notes, such as musk and vetiver, last longest. The drydown is how a scent settles on skin.
  • Oud comes from agarwood, and an attar is a perfume oil, so the Oud and Arabian style uses both.
  • Maison (house) and Parfums are the usual French ways to start or end a name.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Before you pay for a logo, labels or a stall banner, press Check on the name you like, or paste it into the Business Name Checker. It looks for:

  • Company names: in the UK, Companies House won't register a limited company name that's the same as one already on the register. In the US, check your state's business register.
  • Trade marks: search the UK Intellectual Property Office and, if you sell abroad, the USPTO. A registered company name doesn't give you trade mark rights.
  • Web and social: the .com or .co.uk, plus the Instagram, TikTok and Etsy handles fragrance customers will look for.

Fragrances are cosmetic products in the UK, so before you sell, each one needs a safety assessment, a UK Responsible Person and notification through the government's cosmetic notification service, with ingredients listed on the label. Keep the name clear of big fashion houses and their famous perfume names.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full checklist, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for a fragrance brand

  • Name the house, then the scents. The brand name stays the same while each perfume gets its own name.
  • Check the name without accents for web addresses and handles.
  • Say it out loud. If people can't pronounce a French name, they'll struggle to search for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good perfume brand names?
Names that sound like a house or a feeling: Vickers & Hart for quiet luxury, Brume Blanche or Sillage Clair in French, Timber & Leather for cologne, or Zahra Attar House for oud and attars.
+How do I check a perfume brand name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it is free as a company name, a web address and a social media handle. In the UK, search the Companies House register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Do this before you pay for a logo, labels or signage.
+What are good French words for a perfume brand?
Short words that are easy to say in English work best: Sillage (the trail a scent leaves), Velours (velvet), Plume (feather), Lune (moon) and Soir (evening). Maison (house) and Parfums are the usual ways to start or end the name. Keep the accents in your logo but check the name without them for domains and handles.
+What's the difference between eau de parfum and eau de toilette?
Eau de parfum has a higher share of perfume oil than eau de toilette, so it's usually stronger and lasts longer. Parfum (extrait) is stronger still, and eau de cologne the lightest.
