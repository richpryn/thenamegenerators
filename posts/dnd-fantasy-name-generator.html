How the D&D Fantasy Name Generator Works

It works from 2,516 names and name parts, which combine into over 370,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Gender: Male, Female.
  • Race: Any race, Human, Elf, Dwarf, Halfling, Gnome, Half-elf, Half-orc, Tiefling, Dragonborn.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

Need a name for whoever's just walked into the tavern? This D&D fantasy name generator gives you a first and family name for any of the common races, each in that race's own naming style, or a whole random party at once: a dwarf, a tiefling, a halfling and a dragonborn, with their races beside their names.

Each race names itself differently

  • Humans in the Forgotten Realms have regional styles, like Chondathan and Illuskan, much as real countries do.
  • Elves have a family name that translates into Common, and only take an adult name after about a century.
  • Dwarves carry a clan name and guard their family's honour fiercely.
  • Halflings and gnomes are fond of nicknames; gnomes can have half a dozen names.
  • Dragonborn give their clan name first, before their own.
  • Tieflings often have infernal names, or take a virtue as a name instead.

The generator follows each race's pattern, so a dragonborn comes out clan first.

Names for NPCs on the fly

  • Generate a batch before the session. Keep a list of names by race so you never have to stop and think when a player asks the innkeeper's name.
  • Use "Any race" for crowds. A random party is perfect for a rival adventuring group, a tavern full of strangers or a caravan.
  • Cross out the ones you use, and write a word beside each: "nervous", "owes money". That's an NPC.

More names for each race

Each race has its own page with more options and the story behind its names: Human, Elf, Dwarf, Halfling, Gnome, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, Tiefling and Dragonborn. The D&D names hub has everything else, from taverns to goblins.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What races does it cover?
Human, elf, dwarf, halfling, gnome, half-elf, half-orc, tiefling and dragonborn, the common races of D&D. Choose "Any race" for a random mix.
+Are these the names from the Player's Handbook?
No. They're new names in each race's style, drawn from this site's race generators, so your characters won't share a name with the book's examples.
+Can I use these names in my campaign?
Yes, they're free to use and you don't need to credit the site.
