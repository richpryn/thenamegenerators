How to Use D&D Fantasy Names

When creating D&D characters, consider how the name reflects your character's class, background, and personality. Fighters and Paladins might suit names with strong, heroic first names and last names that reference battle or honor (Thalion Shadowbane, Arion Stormbringer). Rogues and Rangers might prefer names that suggest stealth or nature (Kaelen Duskwhisper, Lyra Windrunner). Wizards and Sorcerers might choose names that sound mystical or scholarly (Balthazar Starweaver, Seraphina Moonbrook).

For NPCs, you can use the same generator to create names that fit their role. A noble might have a more refined name, while a commoner might have a simpler one. The variety in our name lists ensures you can find names for characters of any social class or background.

Remember that D&D names should be memorable and easy to pronounce at the table. Avoid names that are too complex or difficult to say, as they can slow down gameplay. The names generated here strike a balance between unique and accessible.
