How the DJ Name Generator Works

It works from 100 names and name parts, which combine into over 1,100 possible results.

The choices:

  • Name format:
    • Any, e.g. DJ Fable
    • One name, e.g. Juno
    • Two words, e.g. Analog Dusk
    • With DJ, e.g. DJ Fade

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

A DJ name has to work in small type on a crowded flyer, be shouted over a sound system and be easy to find on streaming services. This DJ name generator gives you names in the shapes DJs and producers use: a single striking name, a two-word alias, or the classic DJ prefix.

What makes a good DJ name

  • Short. One or two words is the norm, from single names to two-word aliases.
  • Searchable. A common word on its own is hard to find online; an unusual spelling or a pair of words is easier.
  • Fits the sound. "Velvet Haze" suggests something different from "Bass Voltage".
  • Free to use. Search SoundCloud, Spotify, Mixcloud, Beatport and Resident Advisor before you print anything.

To DJ or not to DJ

Putting "DJ" in front of a name is a classic that never quite went away, but many electronic artists drop it, especially producers who release music as well as play it. If you might produce, pick a name that works without it.

For other music names, try the Band, Stage Name and Rapper name generators, and the Event Name Generator for your club night.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I choose a DJ name?
Keep it short, make it searchable, and make it fit your sound. Then check SoundCloud, Spotify, Mixcloud and social media to make sure nobody else is using it.
+Should my DJ name start with DJ?
It's optional. Many producers leave it off so the name works for releases as well as sets.
