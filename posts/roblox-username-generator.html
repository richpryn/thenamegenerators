How the Roblox Username Generator Works

It works from 425 usernames and name parts, which combine into over 100,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style:
    • Any style, e.g. DramaticPelican

    • Cool, e.g. AstroSpinner
    • Funny, e.g. PleaseWaitForMe

  • Format: Words only, With a number, Underscore.

Where the names come from: Every name is invented. The Roblox username rules

Roblox checks every new username against a few rules, and this generator follows all of them:

  • Length: 3 to 20 characters.
  • Characters: letters and numbers only, plus underscores. No spaces, full stops or symbols, so Mochi Bunny becomes MochiBunny or Mochi_Bunny.
  • Underscores: one at most, and never at the start or the end. Mochi_Bunny works; _MochiBunny_ doesn't.
  • Unique: no two accounts can share a username, which is why the obvious ones are gone.
  • Filtered: Roblox runs new usernames through its filter, so a name that keeps getting rejected may have a word in it the filter doesn't like.

Roblox also has a display name, which is separate from the username and doesn't have to be unique. You can pick something simple there, but the username stays with the account, so choose one you'll still like in a year.

Keep your username safe

Lots of Roblox players are young, and a username is the first thing strangers see. A good one tells other players nothing about you. Before you pick one, check it doesn't include:

  • your real name or your surname;
  • your age, birthday or the year you were born, so no 11 or 2015;
  • your school, town or street;
  • anything that pretends to be Roblox staff, a moderator or a YouTuber.

None of the names here has a real name, an age or a year in it. If you choose From a word, type a nickname or a favourite thing, like Pickle or Comet, rather than your own name. Grown-ups: it's worth choosing the name together and setting up the account's parental controls at the same time.

Which style suits you?

  • Aesthetic names are soft colours and pretty things in lowercase, like velvetmoth or starlit_swan. They suit dress-up, roleplay and building games.
  • Cute names join a sweet treat to a small animal: HoneybunBear, MochiBunny.
  • Cool names pair a space or metal word with something fast: CobaltStreak, NebulaSurfer.
  • Funny names are silly animals and objects, or things that happen to everyone, like PeckishPuffin or LostMyCheckpoint.
  • Games names use the words of obbies, tycoons and lobbies, like TowerClimber or CheckpointChaser, without borrowing any game's title.
  • From a word builds names round a nickname or favourite thing: CaptainPickle, PickleAndPanda.

If the name you want is taken

Type it into the username box when you sign up and Roblox will tell you straight away whether it's free. If it isn't, try one of these:

  • Choose With a number. The numbers here, like 77 or 404, are never ages or years.
  • Choose Underscore: Mochi_Bunny is a different username from MochiBunny.
  • Swap one word and keep the other, so MochiBunny becomes MochiOtter.

The names are free to use, but someone may already have the one you like, so check it on Roblox before you get attached.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What is a good Roblox username?
One that's short, easy to read and tells nobody your real name or age, like MochiBunny or NebulaSurfer. Choose a style and press the button until one feels like you.
+How do I make a cute or aesthetic Roblox username?
For cute, join a sweet treat to a small animal, like HoneybunBear; choose Cute. For aesthetic, put two soft words together in lowercase, like velvetmoth; choose Aesthetic.
+How long can a Roblox username be?
Between 3 and 20 characters. Every name this generator makes fits, even with a number added.
+Can a Roblox username have an underscore?
Yes, but only one, and not at the start or the end. Choose Underscore for names like Mochi_Bunny.
+Should I use my real name in my Roblox username?
No. Keep your real name, age, school and town out of it. Choose From a word and type a nickname or a favourite thing instead.
+What are some funny Roblox usernames?
Names like DramaticPigeon, PeckishPuffin and OofIFellOff. Choose Funny for more.
+Is this an official Roblox tool?
No. It's an independent name generator and isn't connected to Roblox Corporation. Roblox decides which usernames it accepts.
