Good pet dinosaur names are cute (Clover, Pip), funny (Lady Short-Arms) or fierce (Sickleclaw), and the best ones suit the kind of dinosaur. Whether it's a cuddly toy T. rex, a new stuffed triceratops or a pet lizard with big ideas, this pet dinosaur name generator has names for girls and boys, from Trixie and Primrose to Sir Chomps-a-Lot and Stormjaw.

Eight good pet dinosaur names

All eight came from the generator, cute, funny and fierce:

  • Primrose: a gentle flower name for a soft toy.
  • Petal Plates: a stegosaurus name that rhymes with its back.
  • Pip: short and small, for a little pterosaur.
  • Lady Short-Arms: a T. rex joke. Real T. rex arms were only about a metre long on an animal twelve metres from nose to tail.
  • Maureen the Wobbly Lamp-Post: a long-neck with a very ordinary name.
  • Effie Prickle-Tail: a cute stegosaurus named after its spikes.
  • Sickleclaw: a fierce raptor, named after the curved claw on each foot.
  • Thora Tide-Fang: a fierce sea reptile with a Norse first name.

Matching the name to the dinosaur

Pick a kind of dinosaur and the names take its shape into account:

  • T. rex names lean on big teeth and small arms.
  • Stegosaurus names mention its plates and its spiky tail. Palaeontologists call those spikes a thagomizer, a word borrowed from a Gary Larson Far Side cartoon.
  • Raptors get quick, sharp names. The real Velociraptor was about the size of a turkey and had feathers.
  • Long necks like Diplodocus and Brontosaurus get names about being tall: Clive of the Loft, Horace of the Tall Cupboard.
  • Flying and sea reptiles are here because every dinosaur toy box has them, though strictly pterosaurs and plesiosaurs weren't dinosaurs.

Choose With a title for a name with a bit of character, like Zip the Friendly Stegosaurus or Sigrid the Relentless Plesiosaur.

Naming a toy, a pet or a game dinosaur

  • For a toddler's toy, pick a cute name that's easy to say: two syllables, like Gigi or Lenny.
  • For a pet lizard, dog or cat, a funny or fierce dinosaur name suits an animal with attitude.
  • For a game, fierce names like General Redjaw work for a tamed dinosaur that fights.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are cute dinosaur names?
Soft, short names like Clover, Pip, Primrose or Lenny, or a name that fits the dinosaur, like Petal Plates for a stegosaurus. Choose Cute, then Girl or Boy.
+What are funny dinosaur names?
Names that make a joke about the dinosaur, like Lady Short-Arms for a T. rex or Maureen the Wobbly Lamp-Post for a long-neck. Choose Funny for names like these.
+Were pterodactyls and plesiosaurs dinosaurs?
No. Pterosaurs were flying reptiles and plesiosaurs were sea reptiles, both living at the same time as the dinosaurs. They're included here because they're in every dinosaur toy box.
