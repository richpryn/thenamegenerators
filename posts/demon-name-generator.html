Where demon names come from

  • The word comes from the Greek daimōn, which first meant any spirit, good or bad. The evil sense came later.
  • Old gods. Many famous demons began as other people's gods. Baal means "lord" and was a Canaanite god; Beelzebub comes from Baal-zebub, "lord of the flies"; Astaroth echoes the goddess Astarte.
  • The grimoires. The Ars Goetia, the first part of a 17th-century book of magic called the Lesser Key of Solomon, lists 72 spirits, each with a rank, a number of legions and the things it can teach. Most fictional demon names since have borrowed its sound.

Ranks and legions

The Goetia arranges its spirits like a royal court: Kings such as Paimon and Belial, Dukes such as Astaroth, Princes, Marquises, Earls, Presidents and a lone Knight, Furcas. Each entry ends by saying how many legions of spirits it rules, often twenty, thirty or more.

Choose the "Goetia style" format for names written the way the old books write them.

Demons in games

  • D&D splits its fiends: demons are creatures of chaos from the Abyss, and devils are creatures of law from the Nine Hells. Demon lords like Orcus and Demogorgon take their names from old mythology.
  • Video games love the Goetia: its names turn up in Persona, Shin Megami Tensei and many more.
  • Domains give a demon a job, and a weakness. A Duke of Old Wounds can be bargained with; a Prince of Rot cannot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the Goetia demons real names?

They're the names given in the Ars Goetia, a 17th-century grimoire, with the ranks it lists. Spellings vary between versions; these follow the common ones.

What's the difference between a demon and a devil?

In everyday speech, nothing much. In D&D, demons are chaotic fiends from the Abyss and devils are lawful fiends from the Nine Hells, and they're at war with each other.

Can I use these names in D&D or my book?

Yes. The new names are free to use and you don't need to credit the site, and the Goetia's names are centuries old and free for anyone. For angels, try the Angel Name Generator.
