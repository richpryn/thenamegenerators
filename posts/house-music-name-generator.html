How house music names itself

House takes its name from the Warehouse, the Chicago club where Frankie Knuckles played from 1977. Across town and in New York, at the Paradise Garage where Larry Levan was resident, the same mix of disco, soul and drum machines was taking shape, and it gave us the word garage too.

The names have stayed close to those roots:

  • Real names, lightly dressed up. House DJs often go by their own names with a "Lil", a "DJ" or a nickname added. Lil Louis and DJ Pierre are two from the early Chicago days. Choose DJ or artist for names like Kid Terrell or Rosa Haywood.
  • Duos and groups. Masters at Work, the New York duo of Louie Vega and Kenny Dope, set a pattern of production teams with grand names. Duo or group gives you surname pairs and crews: Vance & Gaines, Sapphire Disco Club.
  • Uplifting records. House vocals borrow from gospel, so titles talk about faith, freedom and getting through the night. EP or album mixes those with club words: Midnight Sermon, Warehouse Days, Garnet EP.

Using a house music name

  • Say it over a microphone. A promoter will read your name out and print it in small type, so short and clear beats clever.
  • Keep your EPs in a family. Plenty of house producers name EPs after one word plus "EP" or "Sessions", which makes a run of releases look like a set.
  • Search before you print. Check Beatport, Discogs, Bandcamp and the streaming services, since dance music has thousands of one-word aliases.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good house DJ names?
Short, warm names that are easy to read on a flyer: your own name with a "Lil" or "DJ" in front, like Lil Vince, or one bright word, like Mirrorball. Choose DJ or artist in the generator for names in that style.
+Why is it called house music?
After the Warehouse, the Chicago club where Frankie Knuckles was the resident DJ from 1977. Records that sounded like what he played became known as house music.
+What's the difference between house and garage names?
Not much in style: garage takes its name from the Paradise Garage in New York, and both scenes share the soulful, gospel-touched titles. This generator covers both.
