A wuxia hero's technique names are half the fun: they promise something spectacular before the first blow lands. This cultivation technique name generator makes names for sword arts, palm and fist techniques, movement skills and cultivation methods, with a grade if your story ranks them.

How technique names work

A technique's name promises something spectacular before the first blow lands, and it usually says what kind of skill it is.

  • Sword arts (jian is the straight sword) end in Sword Technique or Sword Method.
  • Palm and fist techniques borrow from real martial arts, which often name moves after animals, like the tiger and the crane.
  • Movement skills are qinggong, "lightness skill": running up walls and across water.
  • Heart Methods (xinfa) are the inner cultivation manuals behind everything else.

Technique grades

Many xianxia stories rank techniques from Heaven grade down through Earth and Mystic to Yellow.

  • The order comes from the first line of the Thousand Character Classic, a traditional Chinese primer: "Heaven and earth, dark and yellow" (tian di xuan huang).
  • Choose With a grade to add one to a technique.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What is a heart method in cultivation novels?
A xinfa, the inner cultivation manual that trains a martial artist's energy. Other techniques are built on it.
+What do Heaven, Earth, Mystic and Yellow grades mean?
A ranking of techniques from best to most common, taken from the opening line of the Thousand Character Classic.
+What is qinggong?
Lightness skill: the movement techniques that let wuxia heroes run up walls, leap across rooftops and cross water.
