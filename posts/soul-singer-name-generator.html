Good soul singer names sound like real people, often with an initial, a "Little" or a "Jr.", like Little Wilbert Ellerbe or Reggie Upshaw Jr., while the groups had names polished to a shine. This soul singer name generator makes soul and Motown-style singer names, group names and album titles.

Eight good soul names

All eight came from the generator:

  • Little Wilbert Ellerbe: "Little" was a common billing for young singers, as with Little Stevie Wonder.
  • Reggie Upshaw Jr.: a real-sounding name with a "Jr.", for a singer from a musical family.
  • Odell "Lover Boy" Pinkney: a nickname for a ballad singer, printed in quotation marks.
  • Deborah Faye Stokes: a first name and a middle name together, Southern style.
  • The Sapphire Hi-Fis: a jewel and a bit of hi-fi gloss, the way vocal groups were named.
  • Jimmy Lyons & the Motor City Revue: a leader and a revue, after the touring shows of 1960s Detroit.
  • The Waller Sisters: a family group with a shared surname.
  • Love Letters from Detroit: an album title that tells you exactly what it sounds like.

How soul names itself

  • Motown, founded by Berry Gordy in Detroit in 1959, gave its groups smart, plural names: the Supremes, the Temptations, the Miracles, the Marvelettes, the Four Tops.
  • A singer and their group: Smokey Robinson and the Miracles, Martha and the Vandellas, Gladys Knight and the Pips.
  • Real names for solo singers, often with an initial or a nickname. Soul came largely out of gospel, and many singers kept the names they'd sung under in church.
  • Memphis soul, from labels like Stax, was rawer, and its names and titles were plainer to match.

Choose Singer for a soloist, with the gender filter, Group for a vocal group or a singer and their band, and Album for titles like Heartache Radio or Back Seat Serenade.

Using a soul name

  • For a retro-soul act, a group name with "the" and a plural (The Uptown Gems) instantly sounds like 1965.
  • For a modern singer, a real-sounding name usually works better than a nickname. Use the initial or the "Jr." if it's true.
  • Check the name on streaming services: soul has a long back catalogue.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What is a good soul singer name?
A real-sounding name, often with an initial, a middle name or "Little": Little Wilbert Ellerbe, Deborah Faye Stokes or Reggie Upshaw Jr. Soul singers usually sang under their own names.
+How were Motown groups named?
With short, smart plural names, like the Supremes or the Temptations, or a lead singer's name followed by the group, like Martha and the Vandellas. The Group format makes names in both styles.
+Are these real soul singers?
No. They're new names in the classic style. Search one before you use it, in case a working singer already has it.
