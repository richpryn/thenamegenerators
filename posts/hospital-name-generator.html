A good hospital name is calm and trustworthy: a saint, a place or a benefactor, plus Hospital, Infirmary or Medical Center. This hospital name generator makes invented hospital names for stories, games and roleplay towns: British infirmaries, American medical centres, fantasy healers' houses, sci-fi medbays and abandoned old hospitals, with an optional one-line description.

How hospitals got their names

Hospital comes from the Latin hospes, which meant both guest and host, and the first hospitals took in travellers and the poor as well as the sick. Many were run by monasteries, which is why so many British hospitals are named after saints. St Bartholomew's in London, founded in 1123, is often described as the oldest hospital in Britain still on its original site.

  • British names: a saint, a monarch or a benefactor, then General, District General, Infirmary (from the Latin for weak) or Memorial.
  • American names: Medical Center, Memorial or Regional, often after a family or a county.
  • Fantasy names: houses of healing, hospices and physic gardens.
  • Sci-fi names: medbays, wards on stations and mobile units with code numbers.
  • Abandoned names: fever, isolation and convalescent hospitals were real places, built to keep infectious diseases away from towns. Their ghosts here are staff, lights and sounds, never patients, and the word asylum is never used.

Using a hospital name

  • For a medical drama, a saint's name or a memorial name sounds right for a British hospital.
  • For a roleplay town, the description gives the hospital a detail players remember.
  • For a ghost story, an abandoned hospital works best when the eeriness is in the building, not in mocking the people who were treated there.
  • Avoid real hospitals. Many saints' names are already in use, so check before you set a story in a real town.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good names for a fictional hospital?
A saint, a place or a benefactor plus the right ending: St Bega's Hospital, Bellingthorpe District General Hospital, The Hubert Nuttall Memorial Hospital. For a fantasy world, try The House of Mended Bones.
+Why are so many British hospitals named after saints?
Many of the first hospitals were founded by monasteries and churches, so they took a saint's name. St Bartholomew's in London dates from 1123.
+Is the abandoned hospital style suitable for a ghost story?
Yes. Its ghosts are staff, lights and sounds rather than patients, and it never uses the word asylum, so it's spooky without being unkind.
