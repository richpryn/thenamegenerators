What lawn care names have in common

Lawn care is about tidy, regular work, so the strongest names promise neatness and reliability.

  • Stripes, edges and cuts are the visual promise. Lawn stripes come from the grass being bent in opposite directions by a roller, so the light reflects differently off each strip.
  • Puns on mow (Mow Regrets, Mow Turning Back) are popular because they're short and funny on a van.
  • Professional names with Turf, Grounds or Maintenance suit commercial contracts and sports grounds.
  • Lawn or yard? British customers say lawn and garden; American customers often say yard. The generator uses both, so pick the one your customers use.

Choose Name with a slogan for a line for your flyer, such as Lawn & Order – The grass is greener here.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A van, a trailer and a stack of flyers all carry the name, and each costs money the moment it's printed, so check the name first. Press Check next to any name to open the Business Name Checker, which looks it up in one go:

  • The company register: Companies House in the UK, or your state's business register in the US. A limited company's name can't be the same as one already registered.
  • Trade marks: the UK IPO and USPTO registers. Registering a company doesn't stop someone else owning the name as a trade mark.
  • A domain and social handles, so customers can find you under the same name everywhere.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, read our guide, Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for a mowing round

  • Keep it short, so it fits on a trailer and a fridge magnet.
  • Add your town if the name is common: it helps local search and makes handles easier to find.
  • Leave room to grow if you might add hedge cutting, leaf clearance or full landscaping later.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good lawn care business names?
Short names that promise a neat result, like Green Stripe Lawn Care or Making the Cut, funny ones like Mow Excuses, or local ones built from your town. Choose Professional for commercial grounds names like Benchmark Lawn Maintenance.
+How do I check a lawn care business name is free?
Press Check next to any name to see if it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. Then make sure: in the UK, search Companies House and the UK IPO trade mark register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. A quick map search for the name in your area helps too.
+What's a good slogan for a lawn care business?
Short, friendly and about the result: We cut, you relax, Green lawns, clean lines or Get your weekends back. Choose Name with a slogan in the Format menu to see names paired with one.
+What are some funny lawn care business names?
Puns on mow and grass work best: Mow Excuses, Mow Regrets, Grass Class Lawn Care or Lawn Shot. Choose Funny for more.
