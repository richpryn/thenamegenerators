The great comic wizards

  • Rincewind, Terry Pratchett's cowardly wizard, can't spell his own job: his hat says WIZZARD. The Unseen University's staff include Archchancellor Mustrum Ridcully, the Bursar and Ponder Stibbons, all names that sound like they belong in a dusty common room.
  • Tim the Enchanter, in Monty Python and the Holy Grail, is funny because his name is so ordinary.
  • Dumbledore was a joke too: J. K. Rowling has said it's an old word for a bumblebee, chosen because she imagined him humming to himself.

What makes a wizard name funny

  • Silly sounds: lots of z, wobble, fizzle and -wick. Say it out loud and it should sound slightly wrong.
  • A grand title that doesn't fit: the Magnificent, the Wise, the Eternal, on someone called Noodlecrack.
  • An honest one: the Forgetful, the Half-Baked, the Bewildered.
  • A surname from the laundry: Thunderpants, Puddlewhisker, Gloomspoon.

Using a funny wizard

  • Comic relief in a serious game: a wizard called Boffle the Bewildered can say things a grim hero can't.
  • A whole silly campaign: give every wizard in the tower a name from here and let the players work out who's actually competent.
