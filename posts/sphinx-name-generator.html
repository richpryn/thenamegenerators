There are two sphinxes. The Greek sphinx sat outside Thebes and asked travellers a riddle, killing those who got it wrong. The Egyptian sphinx, like the Great Sphinx of Giza, is a lion with a human head that guards temples and tombs. This sphinx name generator names both, and sphinxes named for the gate they guard.

Two kinds of sphinx

The Greek and Egyptian sphinxes share a lion's body and a human head, and very little else.

  • The Greek sphinx had a woman's head, a lion's body and wings. She sat outside Thebes and asked every traveller a riddle: what walks on four legs in the morning, two at noon and three in the evening? Oedipus answered "a human", and she threw herself to her death.
  • The Egyptian sphinx is usually male, with a king's head on a lion's body: a guardian, not a riddler.
  • The Great Sphinx of Giza, about 73 metres long, is usually dated to the reign of the pharaoh Khafre, around 2500 BC.

Naming a sphinx

  • Greek riddlers get Greek names (Krypteia, "hidden").
  • Egyptian-style names are built from real Egyptian name parts, like those in the Mummy Name Generator, like Merankh and Amenka.
  • Guardians are named for what they guard: the Sphinx of the Sealed Library, the Winged Sphinx of the Sun Gate.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What was the riddle of the sphinx?
What walks on four legs in the morning, two at noon and three in the evening? A human: crawling as a baby, walking as an adult, and with a stick in old age.
+What's the difference between the Greek and Egyptian sphinx?
The Greek sphinx was a winged woman-lion who asked riddles and killed; the Egyptian sphinx is usually a male guardian with a king's head.
+How old is the Great Sphinx of Giza?
It's usually dated to the reign of the pharaoh Khafre, around 2500 BC.
