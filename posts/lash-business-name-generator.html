Eight good lash business names

All eight came from the generator:

  • Built to Lash: a pun on built to last.
  • Lash Things First: a pun on first things first.
  • Volume Control: a joke on volume lashes.
  • Flutter By Lashes: a soft pun on butterfly.
  • Fawn Lash Co.: cute, and fawns are famous for their lashes.
  • Plume: one modern word that suggests feathery lashes.
  • Clin d'Œil Lashes: luxury French: clin d'œil means a wink.
  • The Thistle Lash Room: aesthetic, with a Scottish touch.

How lash business names work

  • Lash words tell clients what you offer. Classic lashes are one extension per natural lash, volume lashes are a fan of several fine extensions on each, and hybrid mixes the two. Words like volume, wisp and flutter fit straight into names.
  • Puns on "lash" are popular (Lash Forward, The Lash Word), so check yours isn't already used nearby.
  • Lash and brow is a common ending if you do both.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A name from a generator is a starting point, not permission to use it. Before you order the sign, the boxes or the van livery:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; you can't register a limited company with the same name as an existing one. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK trade mark register (or the USPTO in the US) for the goods and services you'll sell.
  • Domain and handles. Check the .co.uk or .com and the Instagram and TikTok handles you'll need.
  • Your area. Search a map app for businesses with the same or a similar name nearby.

Press Check on any name, or use the Business Name Checker, to run most of these at once. For the full process, read our guide, Business Naming Best Practices. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

Making the name work

  • Keep it short for the handle. Lash techs are found on Instagram, so the handle is the real name.
  • Think about growth. Your own name builds a following; a brand name is easier to grow into a studio with staff.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good lash business names?
Soft, short names that fit an Instagram handle: puns like Built to Lash or Flutter By Lashes, cute names like Fawn Lash Co., or French luxury like Clin d'Œil Lashes, which means a wink.
+How do I check a lash business name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, also search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as another salon nearby with a similar name can confuse clients.
+Should my lash business name mention brows too?
If you offer brow lamination, tinting or shaping as well as lashes, saying so helps clients searching for both: "Lash & Brow Studio" is a common, clear ending. If lashes are all you do, or you might add other treatments later, a name such as "Flutter Lash Co." keeps it simple, and you can list brows on your menu.
+What's the difference between classic and volume lashes?
Classic lashes are one extension on each natural lash; volume lashes are a fan of several finer extensions on each one. Hybrid lashes mix the two.
