How the Mage Name Generator Works

It works from 1,824 names and name parts, which combine into over 14,000,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Gender: Any, Male, Female.
  • Kind of magic: Any kind, Fire, Ice, Storm and lightning, Earth and stone, Dark and shadow, Light and sun, Arcane and runes, Battle mage, Old and wise.
  • Name format:
    • Name and surname, e.g. Bryvan Slatespire
    • With a title, e.g. Shadow Mage Haldric Mothglass
    • With an epithet or order, e.g. Velka the Cinder-Crowned

Where the names come from: Every name is invented. First names are new fantasy names; surnames, titles and orders are built from words for each kind of magic. Famous mages and wizards from books, films and games are left out, and the names don't repeat the Wizard, Sorcerer, Warlock or Ice Mage pages.

Mage, wizard or sorcerer?

In most fantasy, mage is the catch-all word for anyone who uses magic, and many games, World of Warcraft and Dragon Age among them, use it as the name of their spellcasting class. Dungeons & Dragons is stricter and splits magic users by where their power comes from:

  • Wizards learn magic from books and years of study. Try the Wizard Name Generator.
  • Sorcerers are born with it, from a dragon's blood or a wild surge. See the Sorcerer Name Generator.
  • Warlocks make a pact with a powerful patron. See the Warlock Name Generator.

This page names mages by the kind of magic they use instead, so it suits any of them, and any game that just says "mage".

Where the words come from

  • Mage comes from Latin magus, which goes back to Old Persian. The magi were a priestly class in ancient Persia, and the same word gives us magic.
  • -mancer comes from Greek manteia, 'divination'. Pyromancy first meant telling the future from fire, and geomancy from marks made in the earth. Fantasy has borrowed the ending for anyone who commands an element, so a pyromancer now throws fire rather than reading it.
  • Archmage means 'chief mage', from the Greek arkhi- found in archbishop and archangel. In the generator it goes to arcane and elder mages.

Building a name by element

Each kind of magic has its own surnames, titles and orders, so a name tells you about the mage without explanation. Start with the kind, then choose how formal it should be:

  • Name and surname for everyday use: Malvric Ashveil, Joren Galeweave.
  • With a title for rank: Archmage Kesira Spellwyn, or a full style such as Fire Mage of the Red Hearth.
  • With an epithet or order for reputation and home: the Emberborn, of the Hollow Tower.

Ice mages get a short list here. For frost elves, winter witches and ice dragons as well, use the Ice Mage Name Generator.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What is a good name for a mage?
One that hints at their magic, like Malvric Ashveil for fire or Ilvara Rimeglass for ice. Choose a kind of magic, then With a title or With an epithet or order for something grander.
+What are some good female mage names?
Choose Female for names like Desmera of the Dawn Spire, Archmage Kesira Spellwyn or Gwenara Gloomspire the Moonless. Every kind of magic works with either gender.
+What are some cool fire mage names?
Choose Fire for names like Pyromancer Valen Cinderlock or Malvric Ashveil. With a title adds ranks such as Pyromancer, Flame Magus and Ember Mage.
+What do you call a battle mage?
Battlemage, war mage and spellblade are all common. Choose Battle mage and With a title for names like Battlemage Davessa Ironcrest.
+What are good dark mage names?
Choose Dark and shadow for surnames like Gloomspire and Shadowmantle, titles like Shadow Magus and orders like the Moonless Court.
+What is the difference between a mage and a wizard?
Mage is the general word for a magic user; wizard usually means one who learned it by study. In D&D, wizards study, sorcerers are born with magic and warlocks make pacts, and each has its own generator here.
