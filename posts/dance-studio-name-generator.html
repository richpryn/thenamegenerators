Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a dance studio name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, also search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as another business nearby with a similar name can confuse clients.
+What are good Indian dance school names?
Many Indian dance schools use a Sanskrit word linked to dance or music: natya (dance-drama), nritya (dance), laya (tempo), tala (rhythm), abhinaya (expression), mudra (hand gesture) or ghungroo (ankle bells). "Nrityalaya" and "Natyalaya" mean a home of dance. Add the style you teach ("Laya Kathak Academy", "Nupura Bharatanatyam School") so families searching for that style find you, and check the spelling with your guru or a trusted source.
