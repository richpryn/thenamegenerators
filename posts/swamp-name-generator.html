How the Swamp Name Generator Works

It works from 1,982 names and name parts, which combine into over 46,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, Fantasy, Haunted, British fen, Bayou, Witchy.
  • Name format:
    • Name only, e.g. The Fens of Iddrin
    • With a description, e.g. The Drowned Marshes of Vorrow, guarded by a hydra that has not been seen in a century

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Every good fantasy map has a stretch of marsh nobody wants to cross. This swamp name generator gives you fantasy marshes, haunted bogs, real-sounding British fens, carrs and mosses, Louisiana bayous and witchy mires, with a short description if you want one.
