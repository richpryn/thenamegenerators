Lizardfolk see the world in practical terms, and their names do too. In D&D's Volo's Guide to Monsters, lizardfolk take their names from Draconic, and the name is a simple description given by the tribe for something the lizardfolk did or is known for. This lizardfolk name generator gives you hissing Draconic-style names, plain descriptive names in Common, or both together.

How lizardfolk get their names

A lizardfolk's name isn't chosen for how it sounds. It's a description, earned through a deed or a trait the tribe has noticed. A lizardfolk might be renamed if they do something more notable later.

Because the names are Draconic words, outsiders often only hear the sound. Ask a lizardfolk what their name means and you'll get a plain answer: "Swims-Deep", "Stormeye", "Patient-Hunter". The Both format gives you the two side by side.

The Draconic-style names in this generator are invented to sound right, not real Draconic words, so they don't have fixed meanings. Pair one with a descriptive name to decide what it means for your character.

What the names sound like

  • Hissing and rolling sounds: ss, sk, rr and th. Kressk, Ussarth, Zhurrk.
  • Short: one or two syllables, said with a flick of the tongue.
  • Descriptive names are blunt. They describe the body (Green-Scale), a skill (Eel-Catcher) or a habit (Sun-Basker). Lizardfolk don't flatter.

Playing a lizardfolk

Lizardfolk player characters are fun because they think so differently from the rest of the party. The name can show that:

  • Ask for the party's names in the same style. A lizardfolk might call the wizard "Talks-Too-Much" and mean it as a simple fact.
  • Change the name when it's earned. After a great deed, the tribe, or the lizardfolk themselves, may give a new descriptive name.
  • Keep it practical. A lizardfolk named Frog-Eater isn't embarrassed. That's just what they eat.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do lizardfolk names work in D&D?

In Volo's Guide to Monsters, lizardfolk names come from Draconic and are simple descriptions given by the tribe, based on a notable deed or trait.

What does my lizardfolk's Draconic name mean?

The Draconic-style names here are invented to sound right rather than real Draconic words, so you can decide. Use the Both format to pair each one with a descriptive name.

Can a lizardfolk change their name?

Yes. Because the name describes something they've done or are known for, a lizardfolk may earn a new one after a more notable deed.
