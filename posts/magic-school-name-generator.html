How the Magic School Name Generator Works

It works from 1,620 names and name parts, which combine into over 64,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, Grand academy, Witch school, Wizard college, Secret school, Little ones.
  • Name format:
    • Name only, e.g. Crowsfoot Lodge
    • With a motto, e.g. The Academy of the Nine Lanterns, motto "To know is to wield"
    • With a description, e.g. Ferncliffe Academy of Spellwork, whose bell can be heard in every village for twenty miles

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Good magic school names sound old and a little grand, usually a place name plus Academy, College or Hall, like Hollyholme Academy or Quillford College, and the best ones come with a motto. Every young spellcaster needs somewhere to learn. This magic school name generator gives you grand academies, witch schools, old wizard colleges, hidden schools and cosy schools for little witches and wizards, with a motto or a short description if you want one.

Academies, colleges and mottos

Fictional magic schools borrow from real ones. Hogwarts has a Latin motto, Draco dormiens nunquam titillandus, "never tickle a sleeping dragon", and Terry Pratchett's Unseen University is named like an old university with a joke attached. Words like lyceum go back further still: the Lyceum was the school in Athens where Aristotle taught.

  • Grand academies are royal, ancient or sovereign: The Sovereign Academy of Transmutation.
  • Witch schools are named after herbs and headmistresses: Vervain House, Dame Sloe's School for Witches.
  • Wizard colleges sound like Oxford or Cambridge: Lanternford College.
  • Secret schools are under, behind or beyond something: The Academy Below the Library.
  • Little ones are nurseries and charm schools: Ladybird Magic Nursery.

Choose With a motto for a school motto. The Latin mottos are real Latin, many of them famous lines, and come with a translation: Sapere aude, "dare to be wise", is from the Roman poet Horace. The witch and little-ones schools get English mottos instead, like Brew kindly.

Building a magic school

  • Give it houses or colleges named after its founders, so students have a side to be on.
  • Use the description as a hook: a clock that runs backwards on feast days is the start of a mystery.
  • Choose the motto last, once you know what the school values.

For the students and staff, try the Wizard and Witch name generators. For what they learn, there's the Spell Name Generator, and for the witches who meet after hours, the Coven Name Generator.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good magic school names?
Old-sounding place names with Academy, College or Hall, like Hollyholme Academy of Wandcraft or Lanternford College, or a cosy witch school like Vervain House. Add a motto for the full effect.
+Are the Latin mottos real Latin?
Yes. They're real Latin phrases, many of them well known, such as Festina lente (make haste slowly) and Sapere aude (dare to be wise), each with a translation.
+Is there a magic school name for young children?
Yes. Choose Little ones for cosy names like Toadstool Hill Charm School, with gentle mottos like “Every wand starts small”.
