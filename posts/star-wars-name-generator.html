How the Star Wars Name Generator Works

It works from 967 names and name parts, which combine into over 2,100,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Character:
    • Jedi, e.g. Eiran Korrin
    • Sith Lord, e.g. Darth Skarrath
    • Mandalorian, e.g. Ezrik Lovak
    • Smuggler or rebel, e.g. Kira Varro
    • Droid, e.g. J6-B7
    • Mandalorian clan, e.g. House Varad

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Star Wars names are instantly recognisable: hyphenated Jedi like Obi-Wan Kenobi and Qui-Gon Jinn, Sith Lords who take the title Darth, Mandalorians who belong to a clan, and smugglers with short, sharp names like Han Solo. This Star Wars name generator builds names in each of those patterns.

Jedi names

Jedi names often sound slightly unfamiliar, and many first names are two short parts joined by a hyphen: Obi-Wan Kenobi, Qui-Gon Jinn, Ki-Adi-Mundi. Others are short and simple: Mace Windu, Kit Fisto, Plo Koon. The generator mixes both, with a surname that could belong to any world in the galaxy.

Sith names

A Sith apprentice takes a new name with the title Darth, often a single dark word, and leaves their old name behind: Anakin Skywalker became Darth Vader, Count Dooku became Darth Tyranus, and Palpatine was Darth Sidious. Other Sith names are short and brutal, like Darth Maul, or sound ancient, like Darth Plagueis.

The generator's Sith names lean on Latin-sounding words and dark ideas, the way many real Sith names do, and none are names already used in Star Wars.

Mandalorian names

Mandalorians identify strongly with their clan: Bo-Katan Kryze of Clan Kryze, Sabine of Clan Wren. Din Djarin, the Mandalorian of the series, was a foundling adopted into the Mandalorian way of life. The generator gives a Mandalorian a first name and a clan, either as "of Clan" or as a surname.

Mandalorian clan names

In Star Wars, Mandalorians belong to clans, and clans can belong to larger houses, like House Kryze and House Vizsla. Choose Mandalorian clan for new clan and house names with hard, short sounds: Clan Arvos, Clan Udrik, House Gorvak, House Ghes'ka. Give your Mandalorian a name from the Mandalorian option, then a clan for them to fight for.

Smugglers and rebels

The galaxy's ordinary heroes have the plainest names: Han Solo, Lando Calrissian, Cassian Andor, Jyn Erso. A short first name and a surname that sounds tough is the classic smuggler name. Use the Smuggler or rebel option for pilots, bounty hunters and anyone without a lightsaber.

Droid names

Star Wars droids are known by a model designation of letters and numbers, like R2-D2 and C-3PO, and the people who own them often turn the designation into a nickname: Artoo, Threepio. Choose Droid for new designations in that style, some with a nickname attached:

  • A nickname from the letters: Beefive (B5-L5), Entoo (N2-X6), Emnine (M9-V2).
  • A nickname from personality: Chirp (NQ-72), Scrap (F4-C2), Bleep (V6-L1).
  • A plain designation for a droid nobody has bothered to name: WZ-9, MX-47.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do Sith get their names?
An apprentice is given the title Darth and a new name by their master, usually a single dark word: Vader, Maul, Sidious, Tyranus.
+Why do Jedi names have hyphens?
Several famous Jedi have two-part first names, like Obi-Wan and Qui-Gon, which became one of the most recognisable Star Wars naming patterns.
+Are these names from Star Wars?
No. They're new names in the same patterns; the generator avoids names already used in the films and shows.
+How do you make a Star Wars droid name?
Use a short designation of letters and numbers, like the generator's T1-C7 or WZ-9, then give it a nickname from the letters (Beefive for B5-L5) or its personality (Chirp). Choose Droid for names like these.
+What are good Mandalorian clan names?
Short, hard names after Clan or House: Clan Arvos, Clan Udrik, House Gorvak. Choose Mandalorian clan for more.
+Is this an official Star Wars generator?
No. This site isn't connected to Lucasfilm or Disney. The names are new ones in Star Wars styles, free to use for fan fiction, games and roleplay.
