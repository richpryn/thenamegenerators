Frequently Asked Questions

+What is a good name for a dog?
One or two syllables, easy to call and unlike any command you'll use. Names such as Milo, Poppy, Buster and Mabel work well. If you like a longer name, pick a short version for every day.
+Do dogs know their names?
Dogs learn that the sound of their name means attention, food or a walk is coming, so they turn when they hear it. Use it in a happy voice, reward them when they look at you, and avoid using it when telling them off.
