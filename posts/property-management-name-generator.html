How property managers are named

Landlords, residents and leaseholders want someone dependable who answers the phone, so names lean on surnames, places, building words (lintel, quoin, gable) and phrases about upkeep. If you specialise, say so: "Lettings" for UK rentals, "Block Management" for flats, "HOA Management" in the US, "Stays" for holiday lets.

Rules to know: in the UK, letting agents and property managers must belong to a government-approved redress scheme, and in England they must also belong to a client money protection (CMP) scheme if they hold client money. "Chartered Surveyors" and "RICS" are for RICS-regulated firms only. In the US, "Realtor" is a trade mark of the National Association of Realtors, and many states require a real estate broker's licence to manage rentals for others.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Every name here has a Check button. Press it before you order business cards, and run through these yourself:

  • The company register: Companies House in the UK, or your state's business register in the US.
  • Trade marks: search the UK IPO or USPTO for the same or a similar name in your field.
  • Domains and handles: a matching .co.uk or .com, and the same handle on the platforms your clients use.
  • Booking sites: keep clear of names like Airbnb in your business name, even if you list there.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good property management company names?
Steady, local-sounding names: a surname (Cartwright Property Managers), a place (Hampton Property Office), or a phrase about good upkeep (In Good Order Property Management). Say what you manage if you specialise.
+What makes a good property management company name?
Landlords and residents want someone steady and reachable, so surnames, place names and words about keys, upkeep and good order work well ("Well Kept Property Management", "Hartley Estate Management"). Say what you manage if you specialise: "Block Management" for UK flats, "HOA Management" in the US, "Stays" or "Holiday Lets" for short lets. Keep clear of big agents' and booking sites' names.
+Are there any words a property manager can't use?
Avoid "Chartered Surveyors" and "RICS" unless you are RICS-regulated, and "Realtor", a trade mark of the US National Association of Realtors. Keep booking sites' names, such as Airbnb, out of your name. In the UK, letting and property management agents must join a government-approved redress scheme, and in England a client money protection scheme too. In many US states, managing rentals for others needs a real estate broker's licence.
+How do I check a property management name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as two similar names nearby confuse customers.
