A good cake business name looks pretty on a box, fits in an Instagram handle and hints at your style, from fun birthday cakes to elegant wedding tiers. Most cake businesses start at home and grow on Instagram, so the name has to work in both places. This cake business name generator has names in seven styles, from punny (Top Tier Cake Co.) to luxury, plus ones built from your town or your own name.

Eight good cake business names

All eight came from the generator:

  • The Caker's Dozen: a twist on a baker's dozen, which is thirteen.
  • Whisked Away: a pun that also sounds like a treat.
  • Batter Up Cakery: a playful name for birthday and party cakes.
  • Pip Bakes: short and cute, easy to fit in a handle.
  • Petals & Piping: an aesthetic name for floral buttercream work.
  • Stack & Spoon: a modern pair of words that suits layer cakes.
  • Belgravia Cake Atelier: luxury: an elegant London district and atelier, French for a workshop or studio.
  • Hampton Custom Cakes: your town plus what you do, the easiest name to find locally.

How cake business names work

  • Match the name to the cakes. Wedding cake makers lean on luxury words (atelier, ivory, lace); birthday and novelty bakers do better with fun and puns.
  • Cake words work hard: tier, layer, sponge, ganache, fondant, buttercream and piping all tell customers what you do.
  • "Cakery" and "Cake Co." are common endings that make even an abstract name clear: Marshmallow Cakery, Sugarplum Cake Co.
  • Your own name builds trust when customers are buying from you personally, but is harder to sell on if the business grows.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A name from a generator is a starting point, not permission to use it. Before you order the sign, the boxes or the van livery:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; you can't register a limited company with the same name as an existing one. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK trade mark register (or the USPTO in the US) for the goods and services you'll sell.
  • Domain and handles. Check the .co.uk or .com and the Instagram and TikTok handles you'll need.
  • Your area. Search a map app for businesses with the same or a similar name nearby.

Press Check on any name, or use the Business Name Checker, to run most of these at once. For the full process, read our guide, Business Naming Best Practices. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

In the UK, a home cake business must register with the local council at least 28 days before it starts trading, like any other food business.

Making the name work on a cake box

  • Picture the sticker. A long name has to shrink to fit on a box lid.
  • Check the handle. If @yourname is taken, adding "cakes" or your town is usually better than adding numbers.
  • Think about wedding fairs. A name that sounds polished helps you charge wedding prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good cake business names?
Names that suit the cakes you make: fun puns like The Caker's Dozen for birthday cakes, aesthetic ones like Petals & Piping, or luxury names like Belgravia Cake Atelier for weddings.
+What should I call a cake business I run from home?
Many home bakers use their own name, such as Cakes by Sarah or Sarah's Cakery, because customers are buying from you. If you might hire help or sell the business one day, a name that isn't yours (The Little Cake Co.) is easier to pass on. In the UK, a home food business also needs to register with the local council before it starts trading.
+How do I check a cake business name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Before you commit, search the official registers too: in the UK, Companies House and the UK trade mark register; in the US, your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Look on a map app for businesses with the same name nearby.
+What does atelier mean in a cake shop name?
Atelier is French for a workshop or studio. In a business name it suggests handmade, high-end work, which is why it's popular with wedding cake makers.
