UK and US words

Snow businesses use different words on each side of the Atlantic, and customers search with their own words. In the UK, the work is usually called gritting, salting or snow clearance, and businesses often sell "winter maintenance" contracts to car parks, schools and industrial estates. In the US and Canada it's snow plowing or snow removal, with salting and de-icing alongside.

Even the spelling changes: a UK business ploughs and a US business plows. Choose UK or US to get names in the right words, and if you work near a border or plan to serve both kinds of customer, a name without the word, like Whitecap Snow Co., avoids the problem.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Press Check next to any name to see if it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Then check the trade mark register (the UK IPO in the UK, the USPTO in the US) and search your area for snow and gritting firms with a similar name. Winter businesses rely on local word of mouth, so a name close to a nearby rival can send your calls to them.

Tips for a snow business name

  • Think all year. Most snow businesses also do landscaping, gardening or groundworks in summer. If yours will, choose a name that works in July too, and put the snow in your slogan.
  • Say it on the phone. Customers will often call in a hurry on an icy morning, so the name should be easy to say and spell.
  • Make it fit a van. Short names read better on the side of a vehicle and on a plough blade.
  • Promise speed. A slogan like "Plowed before your coffee's ready" says what customers care about most.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a snow removal business name is free?
Press Check next to any name to see if it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. Then make sure: in the UK, search Companies House and the UK IPO trade mark register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search.
+What are good names for a lawn care and snow removal business?
If you mow in summer and plow in winter, pick a name that works all year: a family or place name like Hartley & Sons or Pinecrest Winter Services, or a modern one like Granite Co., then add the service in your slogan. Avoid names that only say snow if most of your work is grass.
+What is a catchy snow removal business name?
A short pun on snow, grit or salt: Grit It Done, Salt & Shovel or Sleet Street. Choose Catchy for more, and add a slogan if you like.
+Can I use one of these names for my business?
Yes, they're free to use, but check availability first. Press Check to see if the company name, domain and social handles are free, and search the trade mark register before you print anything.
+Should a snow removal business name include my town?
It helps people find you, as customers usually search for snow clearing near them, but it can hold you back if you expand. If you might grow, use the town in your website and adverts rather than the name itself.
