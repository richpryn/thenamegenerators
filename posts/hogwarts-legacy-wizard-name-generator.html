Slytherin wizards have names that suggest ambition, cunning, and sometimes darkness—names like Alistair Nightshade, Basil Darkwood, and Cedric Blackthorn have a mysterious, powerful feel. Name Styles by House

Gryffindor names emphasize bravery and adventure (Arthur Stormheart, Cedric Ironwill, Gideon Redthorne). They often incorporate fire, storms, lions, and elements that suggest courage. Last names suggest strength and heroism (Stormheart, Ironwill, Redthorne).

Ravenclaw names feature scholarly and mystical elements (Silas Evermere, Phineas Nightvale, Augustus Silverwood). They may reference stars, wisdom, or elemental magic. Last names suggest places of learning or intellectual pursuits (Evermere, Nightvale, Silverwood).

Hufflepuff names emphasize warmth and nature (Aidan Earthwhisper, Bennett Wildbloom, Cedric Goldleaf). They often use plant names, gentle sounds, and references to the natural world. Last names suggest cozy, welcoming places (Earthwhisper, Wildbloom, Goldleaf).

Slytherin names have a darker, more mysterious quality (Alistair Nightshade, Basil Darkwood, Cedric Blackthorn). They often incorporate serpentine, shadow, or dark elements. Last names suggest darkness, power, or cunning (Nightshade, Darkwood, Blackthorn).
