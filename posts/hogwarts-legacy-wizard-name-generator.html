Hogwarts Legacy is set in the 1890s, a hundred years before Harry Potter, and its names belong to that era: Sebastian Sallow, Ominis Gaunt, Eleazar Fig. This Hogwarts Legacy wizard name generator builds names the same way, with a Victorian or wizarding first name and a surname chosen to suit your house.

Wizards of Hogwarts Legacy

The game's cast is a good guide to the style. Among the students and staff you meet:

  • Sebastian Sallow (Slytherin), whose search for a cure for his twin sister Anne leads him somewhere dark.
  • Ominis Gaunt (Slytherin), from the family descended from Salazar Slytherin himself.
  • Amit Thakkar (Ravenclaw), the classmate who'd rather be stargazing.
  • Garreth Weasley (Gryffindor), forever brewing something he shouldn't, and Leander Prewett (Gryffindor).
  • Everett Clopton (Hufflepuff).
  • Professor Eleazar Fig (Magical Theory), your mentor; Professor Aesop Sharp (Potions); Professor Abraham Ronen (Charms); and Headmaster Phineas Nigellus Black.

Notice the mix: plain English surnames (Sallow, Sharp, Clopton), famous wizarding families (Gaunt, Weasley, Prewett, Black) and names from around the world (Thakkar, Ronen).

Victorian first names

The first names here are ones a wizard born around 1880 might really have had. Some were among the most popular boys' names in late Victorian Britain: Arthur, Albert, Frederick, Ernest, Walter. Others have the grand, old-fashioned ring the wizarding world loves, like Phineas, Eleazar and Aesop in the game: Septimus, Thaddeus, Peregrine, Mordecai.

Like the game's own cast, a handful come from further afield: Arjun, Kenji, Emeka, Mateo, Idris.

Surnames by house

The books don't give the houses naming rules, so this generator leans into each house's character instead. Every surname list is made of real-sounding British names:

  • Gryffindor: bold, outdoorsy names (Hardwick, Wolfe, Fairweather).
  • Hufflepuff: warm, homely, slightly whimsical names (Applegate, Merriweather, Kettle).
  • Ravenclaw: bookish, airy names (Inkpen, Featherstone, Lark).
  • Slytherin: old-money, aristocratic names (Cavendish, Mordaunt, Ravenscroft), in the tradition of Malfoy, Rosier and Selwyn.

Choose Any house to mix them all.

Naming your character in the game

  • The game asks for a first and last name, and won't accept every word, so keep it simple.
  • Say it the way a professor would. "Mr Clopton" and "Mr Thakkar" are how you'll be addressed in class, so make sure the surname works with a title.
  • Think about family. A surname like Gaunt or Weasley tells everyone your background. A new name, like the ones here, lets your character's story be their own.
  • Match the decade. Names like Kayden or Brooklyn would stand out in the 1890s; Florence, Edith, Arthur and Albert fit right in.

Frequently Asked Questions

+When is Hogwarts Legacy set?
In the 1890s, about a hundred years before Harry Potter arrives at Hogwarts. Names that were common in late Victorian Britain fit the setting best.
+Are these names from the game?
No: they're new names in the game's style, so your wizard won't share a name with someone you meet. The characters named in the article are from the game.
+Does my house affect my name?
Not in the game: any name works in any house. This generator just leans each house's surnames towards its character, so a Slytherin sounds a little more aristocratic and a Hufflepuff a little more homely.
+Can I use a famous wizarding surname?
For your own character, of course; the game already has a Weasley, a Gaunt and a Black. A family name gives your wizard a ready-made history, so make sure it's one you want.
+What's a good Slytherin name for a wizard?
An old-money, aristocratic name: choose Slytherin for surnames like Cavendish or Mordaunt with a Victorian first name.
+What are good Gryffindor names?
Bold, outdoorsy names: choose Gryffindor for surnames like Hardwick or Wolfe.
