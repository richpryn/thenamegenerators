How the Ranger Name Generator Works

It works from 415 names and name parts, which combine into over 480,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Gender:
    • Any, e.g. Fiona Who Speaks to Beasts
    • Male, e.g. Niall the Beast-Bonded
    • Female, e.g. Robin, Keeper of the Ways
  • Conclave: Any conclave, Hunter, Beast Master, Gloom Stalker, Horizon Walker, Monster Slayer, Fey Wanderer, Swarmkeeper.
  • Name format: Name and epithet, Name only.
  • Starts with: Any letter, A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O, P, R, S, T, W.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Rangers live at the edges of the map, so their names tend to be short, practical and borrowed from the wild. This ranger name generator gives D&D 5e rangers a first name that sounds at home in the woods and an epithet for their Ranger Conclave, from the Hunter to the Gloom Stalker.

The ranger in D&D

A D&D ranger is a hunter and tracker of the wilds, mixing weapons with nature magic.

  • Conclaves: rangers follow the Hunter or the Beast Master in the Player's Handbook, or the Gloom Stalker, Horizon Walker, Monster Slayer, Fey Wanderer or Swarmkeeper in later books.
  • Real rangers: in medieval England a ranger was an officer who patrolled a royal forest, keeping watch on its deer and its poachers.
  • Strider: Aragorn in The Lord of the Rings did more than anyone to fix the ranger as a fantasy hero.

Naming a ranger

Ranger names are short and practical: Brook, Stone, Fallon, Lorne.

  • The epithet follows the conclave: a Gloom Stalker "the Moth-Haunted", a Horizon Walker "of the Far Horizons", a Monster Slayer "the Stake-Bearer".
  • An animal companion needs a name too: try the Werewolf Name Generator's wolf names.

For the rest of the party, try the Druid, Rogue and Wood Elf name generators.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's a good ranger name?
A short, practical name from the wild, like Brook or Fallon, with an epithet for the conclave, like "of the Far Horizons".
+What are the ranger subclasses?
Hunter and Beast Master in the Player's Handbook, plus Gloom Stalker, Horizon Walker, Monster Slayer, Fey Wanderer and Swarmkeeper in later books.
+What was a ranger in history?
In medieval England, an officer who patrolled a royal forest and watched for poachers.
