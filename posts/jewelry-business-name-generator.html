Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I check a jewelry business name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it is free as a company name, a web address and a social media handle. In the UK, search the Companies House register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Do this before you pay for a logo, labels or signage.
+Should I use French words in a jewelry business name?
French works well for jewellery because the trade has used it for centuries: bijou means a jewel, maison a house, or gold and argent silver. Keep it to words your customers can say and spell, and check the meaning, because a name that is wrong in French puts off exactly the customers it is meant to impress.
