How the Quiz Team Name Generator Works

It works from 1,047 names and name parts, which combine into over 31,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Theme: Any theme, Quiz in it, General, Music, Film, Sport, History, Work, Family & kids.
  • Team size: Any size, Two of us, Three, Four, Five or more.

Where the names come from: Every name is invented. Original puns on quizzing, music, film, sport, history and office life. None is based on a real person's name.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

The team name is the first answer the quizmaster reads out, so make it a good one. This quiz team name generator gives funny pub quiz and trivia team names, with quiz in the name or themed on music, film, sport, history, work or family, for a pair, a four or a whole table.

Eight good quiz team names

All eight came from the generator, one from each theme:

  • Quizinformation: quiz squeezed into a word everyone knows.
  • Fact Attack: short and confident: easy for the quizmaster to read out.
  • Treble Makers: a music pun on troublemakers, for a team that knows its music round.
  • Plot Thickeners: for the film buffs.
  • Transfer Window Shoppers: a football joke for a team that's strong on sport.
  • Feudal Attraction: a history pun with a wink.
  • Meeting Could Have Been an Email: an office team name everyone at work will recognise.
  • Sshh, Conferring: for a team of four that argues over every answer.

What makes a good quiz team name

The quizmaster reads every team name out at least twice: when the scores go up and again at the end. So the best names are easy to say, get a laugh in the room, and still sound good if you come last. Self-mocking names like Last Place Champions or Wrong Again take the pressure off, while confident ones like Fact Attack set you up for a fall if you don't win.

Puns work best when they change one word in a phrase everyone knows, as in Quiz Pro Quo or Gone with the Win. If the quizmaster has to stop and work it out, it's probably one change too many.

Matching the name to the night

  • Theme nights. If the quiz has a theme, such as music, film or sport, choose that theme for a name that fits the evening.
  • Work quizzes. Office jokes about meetings, lanyards and the buffet go down well. Keep it friendly, as your manager will hear it.
  • Family and school quizzes. Keep the jokes clean and simple so the children get them too. Choose Family & kids.
  • Team size. Names that count the team, like Tri Hard or Four Gone Conclusion, are easy to remember. Choose your team size for those.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are some funny quiz team names with quiz in the title?
Swap "quiz" into a phrase everyone knows: Quiz Pro Quo, Quizzing the Point, Quiztakes Were Made or Quizzly Bears. Choose Quiz in the name for more like these.
+What is a good quiz team name for 2 people?
Play on there being two of you: Better Halves, Two Clever by Half or One Pen Between Us. Choose Two of us under Team size, and add a theme if your quiz has one.
+What is a good team name for a work quiz?
Something every office will recognise: Meeting Could Have Been an Email, Lanyard Legends or Just Here for the Buffet. Choose Work for more, and keep it friendly, as the whole company will hear it.
+What is a good quiz team name for a big group?
Lean into the numbers: Too Many Cooks, Half a Dozen Super Whizz Kids or Six Quiz Kids. Choose Five or more under Team size.
+Should I keep the same quiz team name every week?
Many regular teams do, so the quizmaster and other teams get to know you. Others change it every week for a new joke. Both are fine, though a regular name makes it easier to track your scores over the season.
