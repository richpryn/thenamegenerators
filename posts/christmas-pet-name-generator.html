How the Christmas Pet Name Generator Works

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

A puppy or kitten that arrives at Christmas often ends up with a Christmas name, and the best ones still work in July. This Christmas pet name generator has classic festive names, Christmas dinner names and funny ones for dogs and cats.

Christmas names that last all year

A Christmas name should still sound right when you're calling the dog in from the garden in July.

  • Classic names like Holly, Noel, Ivy and Robin work all year round.
  • Food names like Sprout, Mince Pie and Pudding are fun, and very British.
  • Funny names like Bah Humbug or Pawliday are best for a pet with the personality to carry them.

A pet is for life

"A dog is for life, not just for Christmas" was coined in 1978 by Clarissa Baldwin of the National Canine Defence League, now Dogs Trust, and it still holds for cats and dogs alike.

  • Christmas is a busy time for a new pet to settle in. Many rescue centres pause adoptions over the holidays for that reason.
  • Keep the namesakes out of reach: chocolate, grapes and raisins (so mince pies and Christmas pudding) and onions are all dangerous for dogs.
