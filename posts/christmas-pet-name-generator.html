How the Christmas Pet Name Generator Works

It works from 145 names and name parts, which combine into over 540 possible results.

The choices:

  • Pet:
    • Any, e.g. Jingle
    • Dog or puppy, e.g. Good Boy Noel
    • Cat or kitten, e.g. Ivy
  • Style: Any, Classic, Christmas food, Funny.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

A puppy or kitten that arrives at Christmas often ends up with a Christmas name, and the best ones still work in July. This Christmas pet name generator has classic festive names, Christmas dinner names and funny ones for dogs and cats.
