Brand or print shop?

A t-shirt business is usually one of three things, and the name should match:

  • A slogan or design brand sells the designs. The name is part of the joke or the look, so puns and cool, short names work: Wisecrack Tees, Mesh Tees.
  • A print-on-demand shop sells online, with printing done to order. The name needs to work as a shop name and a handle more than on a sign.
  • A print shop prints other people's designs by screen printing or direct-to-garment (DTG). Customers want reliable and local: Fine Line Garment Printing. Choose Printing shop.

In the UK, clothing sold to the public has to be labelled with its fibre content, so your neck label has a job beyond branding.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A t-shirt name goes on neck labels, packaging, a shop and a handle, so it's worth a few minutes' checking before anything is printed:

  • Press Check on any name above. The Business Name Checker looks up the domain, links to the company register (Companies House in the UK) and opens the trade mark search and social handles.
  • Search where you'll trade. Look the name up on maps, marketplaces and social media. Two businesses with the same name confuse customers and reviews.
  • Clothing is trade mark class 25, one of the busiest classes, so search the register before you order labels.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, from shortlisting to registering, read our guide to Business Naming Best Practices.

Tips for naming a clothing line

  • Test it as a handle. Most t-shirt brands are found on social media first.
  • Leave room to grow. Tees is clear, but Print Co. stretches to hoodies and tote bags.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What is a good name for a t-shirt business?
One that says tees, print or ink, or hints at your designs, and still fits a neck label and a handle. Puns such as Tee Break suit a slogan or print-on-demand brand; a printing shop does better with a plain, local name such as Hampton Screen Printing. Steer clear of names close to platforms and big brands, such as Threadless or Redbubble.
+What should I call a screen printing business?
Something plain, reliable and local, like The Press Shed or Fine Line Garment Printing, or your town plus Print Co. Choose Printing shop in the Style menu.
+How do I check a t-shirt business name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it is free as a company name, a web address and a social media handle. In the UK, search the Companies House register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Do this before you pay for a logo, labels or signage.
+Do I need a trade mark for a t-shirt brand?
You don't need one to start, but clothing is a busy trade mark class, so search the register before you order labels. Registering your own mark later protects the name as the brand grows. This is general guidance, not legal advice.
