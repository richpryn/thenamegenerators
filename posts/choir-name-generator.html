Good choir names usually take the name of their town or church, like St Winifred's Chamber Choir, while a cappella groups and barbershop quartets love a good pun, like Duly Noted. This choir name generator covers community, chamber, church, school and barbershop choirs, gives a cappella groups a name to sing under, and suggests concert titles too.

  • Ullsmere Male Voice Choir: the classic name for a men's choir, a great tradition in Wales.
  • Rosemoor Choral Society: a choral society is a large amateur choir, often singing big works with orchestra.
  • Ashcombe Singers: simple and friendly for a community choir.
  • Duly Noted: an a cappella pun on a musical word.
  • Harmonic Motion: a pun that's also a real term, for a student a cappella group.
  • Come and Sing Duruflé: a come-and-sing day, where singers learn and perform a work in one go.
  • Carols by Firelight: a Christmas concert title that sells itself.

Kinds of choir and how they're named

  • Church and cathedral choirs take the church's name: St Dunstan's Singers, Wexley Abbey Choir.
  • Community choirs and choral societies take the town's name. A choral society is usually large and sings oratorio and other big works.
  • Chamber choirs are smaller and often sing unaccompanied.
  • Male voice choirs are a proud tradition in Wales and the north of England.
  • A cappella groups (Italian for "in the manner of the chapel") sing without instruments, and student groups especially love puns.
  • Barbershop is four-part close harmony, with the lead singing the tune between tenor, baritone and bass.

Choose Choir name for a choir, A cappella group for a pun name, and Concert title for programmes like Songs of Spring or A Celebration of Madrigals.

Naming your choir

  • Match the name to the music. A pun suits a pop a cappella group; a chamber choir singing Byrd wants something calmer.
  • Put the place first if you want new singers to find you.
  • Search the name locally and online. Choir puns are popular, and the best ones are often taken.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What is a good name for a choir?
For a community or church choir, the town or church plus Singers, Choir or Chorale, like Ashcombe Singers or St Winifred's Chamber Choir. For an a cappella group, a musical pun like Duly Noted.
+What are some funny a cappella group names?
Puns on musical words work best: Duly Noted, Harmonic Motion, Clef Notes. Choose A cappella group in the generator, and search a name before you use it, since popular puns are often taken.
+What's the difference between a choir and a choral society?
A choral society is a kind of choir: usually large and amateur, singing big works such as oratorios, often with an orchestra. Choir is the general word.
