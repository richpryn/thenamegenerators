How the Cultivator Title Generator Works

It works from 100 titles and name parts, which combine into over 1,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Kind of cultivator:
    • Any, e.g. Star Lord of the Silver River
    • Immortal, e.g. Celestial Sovereign of the Moonlit Lake
    • Sword cultivator, e.g. Blade Master of the Falling Stars
    • Demonic, e.g. The Smiling Blood Emperor
    • Alchemist, e.g. The Merciless Herb Immortal

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

In xianxia, a cultivator who reaches the top earns a title, and it's usually the name everyone remembers. This cultivator title generator makes titles for immortals, sword cultivators, demonic cultivators and alchemists, from Immortal Venerable of the Jade Peak to the Drunken Sword Saint.
