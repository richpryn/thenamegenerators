A clan is a family you can call on: the word comes from the Gaelic clann, "children". This clan name generator has names for three kinds: Highland-style clans for fantasy worlds, with a motto or a war cry; tribes named for their totem animal; and gaming clans, with a short tag ready for your team.

How Highland clans did it

  • Mac means "son of". A clan was named for its founding ancestor: MacDonald, the sons of Donald.
  • Mottos sit on a strap and buckle round the crest on a clansman's badge. Many are short and fierce; some are in Gaelic or Latin.
  • War cries were shouted to rally the clan, often the name of a gathering place or a hill. The Gaelic for a host's cry, sluagh-ghairm, is where English gets the word slogan.

The fantasy clans here borrow the pattern with names from the land (-burn is Scots for a stream, -fell a hill) rather than real family names.

Tribes and totems

Many fantasy peoples, from orcs to steppe riders, are organised by tribe, and tribes are often named for an animal and a quality: the Howling Elk, the Painted Hawk. The animal says what they admire; the adjective, what others say about them. Add a motto and you have a culture in a line.

Gaming clans and tags

  • Keep it short. Most games show a clan tag of a few characters next to each player's name. The tags here are four letters, made from the clan name.
  • Make it easy to say on voice chat. "Silent Owls" works shouted mid-match; anything with numbers swapped for letters doesn't.
  • Check it's free. Search your game's clan list before you set it up: popular names go fast.
  • A bit of humour helps. Salty Penguins will be remembered longer than Elite Snipers.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What does "clan" mean?
It comes from the Scottish Gaelic clann, "children" or "offspring": a clan is the descendants of one ancestor.
+How long should a gaming clan tag be?
Usually three to five characters, depending on the game. The generator gives four-letter tags, which fit most.
+Can I use these names?
Yes, they're free to use and you don't need to credit the site. For a gaming clan, check the name isn't taken in your game first.
