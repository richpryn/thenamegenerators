How the Cave Name Generator Works

It works from 1,869 names and name parts, which combine into over 40,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Style: Any style, Fantasy, Monster lair, Crystal, Sea cave, Dark.
  • Name format:
    • Name only, e.g. Kelynack Caves
    • With a description, e.g. The Ruby Hollow, where the walls shine green in lantern light

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet. Once you've seen them all, "Start again" shuffles the whole list again.

Good cave names tell you what's inside before you go in: a dragon, a crystal, the sea, or nothing you'd want to meet. Somewhere under every fantasy map there is a cave worth exploring. This cave name generator gives you fantasy caverns, D&D monster lairs, glittering crystal grottoes, sea caves and dark potholes, with a short description if you want a hook to go with the name.

How caves are named

Real British caves have plain, old names. Hole is the commonest, as in Wookey Hole in Somerset, and in the Yorkshire Dales a deep vertical shaft is a pot or pothole. The generator uses those words, and some local ones from the coast:

  • Zawn is Cornish for a deep, narrow sea inlet in the cliffs.
  • Gloup, from Orkney and Shetland, is a blowhole where the sea booms up through the roof of a cave.
  • Geo is the Northern Isles word for a narrow coastal inlet.

The most famous sea cave in Britain is Fingal's Cave on Staffa, in the Inner Hebrides, with its walls of six-sided basalt columns. A visit there inspired Mendelssohn's Hebrides overture, also called Fingal's Cave.

Choose a style: fantasy caverns, a monster lair, a crystal grotto, a sea cave or a dark pit. With a description adds a hook, like Bogle Pot, where a cold wind blows up from below.

Using caves in a game

  • Let locals name it. The villagers' name (Lost Lad Pit) and the scholar's name (the Celestine Halls) can be different, and the difference is a clue.
  • Name the chambers too. A big cave system needs names inside it: the first chamber, the narrow squeeze, the underground lake.
  • A lair needs a monster. The lair style names the cave after its owner, so decide who that is before the players arrive.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good cave names?
Names that hint at what's inside: the Grotto of Hanging Stars for a crystal cave, Sootwing's Nest for a dragon's lair, Last Candle Pit for somewhere dark. Choose a style to match.
+What's the difference between a cave, a cavern and a grotto?
A cave is any natural hollow; a cavern is usually a large one; a grotto is a small, often picturesque cave, sometimes by water. Use whichever suits the scale.
+What is a pothole?
A deep, mostly vertical cave shaft, especially in the limestone of the Yorkshire Dales, where caves are often called pots.
