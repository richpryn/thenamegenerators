How the Minecraft Town Name Generator Works

It works from 158 names and name parts, which combine into over 2,300 possible results.

The choices:

  • Place:
    • Village, e.g. Beacon Watch
    • Town, e.g. Storm Crossing
    • City, e.g. Sheep Shed Central
    • Kingdom, e.g. The Warden Empire
  • Style: Any, Classic, Block names, Epic, Funny.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

This Minecraft town name generator names villages, towns, cities and kingdoms in four styles: classic place names, names made from blocks, epic strongholds and funny ones. Once a base becomes a village, and a village becomes a town, it needs a name on the signpost.

Minecraft village, town and kingdom names

Minecraft town names work best when they match the size of the place and what it's built from.

  • Classic: English-sounding place names like Stonewick or Birchport, built the way real town names are: a feature plus an ending like -wick, -port or -ford.
  • Blocky: names made from what it's built of or near: Terracotta Crossing, Mossy Cobble Hamlet.
  • Epic: strongholds and capitals: Warden Haven, the Obsidian Empire.
  • Funny: for the town that's really three dirt huts and a farm.

Pick the size first (village, town, city or kingdom) and the generator adds the right sort of ending.

Tips for naming a Minecraft base or kingdom

Name your build after what's around it, and keep every town in a kingdom in the same style.

  • Name it after what's there: a town by a cherry grove, a port on the ocean, a hold in the mountains.
  • Put the name on a sign at the entrance, or on a banner over the gate.
  • Naming a kingdom on a server? Name each town in it in the same style, so they feel like one nation.

Something that fits where it is: choose Village and the classic style for names like Stonewick, or blocky for names like Mossy Cobble Hamlet.
+What should I call my Minecraft kingdom?
Choose Kingdom and the epic style for names like the Obsidian Empire.
+What can I name my Minecraft base?
Try the town generator's classic or epic style for a base that's grown into a town, or the funny style for a base that hasn't.
