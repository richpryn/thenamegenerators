How to name a villager in Minecraft

To name a villager in Minecraft, rename a name tag on an anvil and use it on the villager.

  1. Get a name tag: you can find them in chests, catch them while fishing, or buy them from a librarian villager.
  2. Put the name tag in an anvil, type the name and take it out. This costs one level of experience.
  3. Hold the name tag and use it on the villager. The name shows above its head.

A named villager won't despawn, and the name stays if it changes job. Naming villagers is a big help in a trading hall, where a row of identical librarians is easy to mix up.

Villager names by job and biome

Minecraft villagers have 13 professions, plus the jobless nitwit, and come in 7 biome types.

  • Thirteen jobs: armorer, butcher, cartographer, cleric, farmer, fisherman, fletcher, leatherworker, librarian, mason, shepherd, toolsmith and weaponsmith, each linked to a workstation block. Nitwits, in green coats, never take a job.
  • Seven villager types, one for each biome they come from: plains, desert, savanna, taiga, snowy, swamp and jungle. The names follow the biome too, so a snowy villager might be Frosty and a desert one Dusty.
  • The title format adds the job, so your librarian becomes Herbert Bookworth the Librarian.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do you name a villager in Minecraft?
Rename a name tag on an anvil (it costs one level), then use the name tag on the villager.
+Does naming a villager stop it despawning?
Yes. A villager with a name tag won't despawn.
+What are good names for a librarian villager?
Bookish ones: choose Librarian for names like Herbert Bookworth, or the title format for Herbert Bookworth the Librarian.
+Can you name a villager anything?
Almost: an anvil allows up to 50 characters. A few special names do odd things to mobs, though: Dinnerbone or Grumm turns any mob upside down.
+What are funny villager names?
Funny villager names lean on the villagers' famous "hmm": try names like Hmmmond Edgeworth or Harrumph Beetroot, or give your nitwit a name like Snooze.
