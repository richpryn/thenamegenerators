Eight good beauty salon names

All eight came from the generator:

  • About Face Beauty Bar: a pun that tells you it's about faces.
  • The Glow Up: fun and current, for a younger crowd.
  • Skin Deep Beauty: a familiar phrase turned into a name.
  • Hollis & Whitmore Beauty Rooms: classic: two surnames and the British beauty rooms.
  • Serum Beauty: one modern word from skincare.
  • Clay & Fig Beauty: aesthetic and earthy, for natural treatments.
  • Peau Douce: luxury French: it means "soft skin".
  • Institut de Beauté: the French for a beauty institute, for a salon with a French feel.

How beauty salon names work

  • The ending sets the tone. Rooms and salon feel traditional, studio and bar feel modern, and lounge feels relaxed.
  • Clinic and aesthetics suggest medical-style treatments such as injectables. Use them only if that's what you offer, or clients will expect something you don't do.
  • Calm words sell calm: clay, fig, fern, linen and golden hour all suggest a peaceful treatment room.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A name from a generator is a starting point, not permission to use it. Before you order the sign, the boxes or the van livery:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; you can't register a limited company with the same name as an existing one. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK trade mark register (or the USPTO in the US) for the goods and services you'll sell.
  • Domain and handles. Check the .co.uk or .com and the Instagram and TikTok handles you'll need.
  • Your area. Search a map app for businesses with the same or a similar name nearby.

Press Check on any name, or use the Business Name Checker, to run most of these at once. For the full process, read our guide, Business Naming Best Practices. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good beauty salon names?
Names that feel calm and match your prices: catchy ones like About Face Beauty Bar, classic ones like Hollis & Whitmore Beauty Rooms, or French luxury like Peau Douce, which means soft skin.
+How do I check a beauty salon name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. In the UK, also search Companies House; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. Check your local area too, as another salon nearby with a similar name can confuse clients.
+What's the difference between a beauty salon and a beauty parlour?
Nothing much: they're the same kind of business. "Beauty salon" is the usual phrase in the UK and the US today, while "beauty parlour" ("parlor" in American spelling) sounds old-fashioned in Britain and America but is still common in India and Pakistan. Words such as studio, rooms, bar and clinic are popular in new salon names.
+Should I call my salon a clinic?
Only if you offer medical-style treatments such as injectables or skin peels done by a qualified practitioner. For facials, brows and waxing, studio, rooms or bar sets the right expectations.
