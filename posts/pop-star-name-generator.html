Good pop star names sound like a real person with a little extra sparkle, and a few need only one name. This pop star name generator makes solo stage names, pop group names and album titles in the shapes real chart acts use.

Real names, stage names and one-name stars

Pop stars choose their names in three main ways, and the generator makes all three:

  • A real-sounding name. Some stars use their own name (Dua Lipa, Taylor Swift); others change it. Katy Perry was born Katheryn Hudson and took her mother's maiden name to avoid confusion with the actress Kate Hudson. The generator gives names like Summer Vale and Seth Winter.
  • One name. Madonna and Adele use their first names; Rihanna is her middle name. Single names here include Zinnia and Quill.
  • A full invention. Elton John was born Reginald Dwight, and Lady Gaga is Stefani Germanotta. The Stage Name Generator is good for names like that.

Choose Pop group for bands like Juno Nash and the Lovers, and Album for titles like Half Past Midnight.

Using the names

  • Say it after "Ladies and gentlemen…" If it sounds right announced, it's a pop name.
  • Check it's free on Spotify, Instagram and TikTok, and check it isn't trademarked if you plan to sell anything with it on.
  • Match it to your sound. A soft single name suits an indie-pop artist; a bright two-word name suits chart pop.

Starting a group instead? Try the Boy Band, Girl Group and K-pop name generators. For the songs, there's the Song Title Generator.

Frequently Asked Questions

+How do I come up with a pop star name?
Start with a name that sounds like a real person, then add a little sparkle: a bright surname (Eden Summers), a single name (Hollis), or a full stage name. Say it out loud as if someone is introducing you on stage.
+Do pop stars use their real names?
Some do, like Dua Lipa and Taylor Swift. Others change them: Katy Perry was born Katheryn Hudson, and Elton John was born Reginald Dwight.
+Can I use these names?
Yes, they're free to use. Search the name on streaming services and social media first, and check it isn't trademarked if you're building a career on it.
