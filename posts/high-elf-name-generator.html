High elves are the oldest, proudest and most magical of their kind, and their names sound it: long vowels, soft endings, and a family name with a meaning. This high elf name generator pairs an elegant first name with a family name built from Quenya, the High-elven language Tolkien invented, and can tell you what it means: Isillótë, "Moonflower".

What makes an elf "high"

  • In Tolkien, the High Elves are the ones who made the great journey west to the light of Valinor. Their language, Quenya, became a language of lore and ceremony, a little like Latin in our world.
  • In D&D, high elves are the subrace of magic and old cities: proud, learned and long-lived, often a little distant with shorter-lived folk.
  • In Warcraft they became the blood elves, after a disaster. See the Blood Elf Name Generator.

Family names with meanings

D&D's elves have family names that translate into Common: the Player's Handbook gives examples like Amastacia, "Starflower". The family names here work the same way, but the parts are real Quenya words from Tolkien's own vocabulary:

  • elen star, isil moon, anar sun, laure gold, telpe silver, ilma starlight, menel heaven.
  • lótë flower, lindë song, mírë jewel, calma lamp, fána veil, alda tree, súrë wind.

So Elenlindë is Starsong, and Laurealda Goldtree, a nod to Laurelin, the golden tree of Valinor. Choose "With what the family name means" to see the meaning every time.

Playing a high elf

  • Use the full name rarely. A high elf might introduce themselves with first name, family name and lineage, and expect everyone else to remember it.
  • Let the meaning matter. A family called Starsong might keep an old songbook; one called Silverlamp might guard a light that has never gone out.
  • Elves live long. In D&D they don't take an adult name until around a hundred; the Elf Name Generator explains child names and adult names.

Frequently Asked Questions

+Are these real Elvish names?
The family names are built from real Quenya words, and the meaning shown is what those words mean. The first names are new, in the same graceful style. None are names of characters from Tolkien or the D&D books.
+What's the difference between high elves and wood elves?
High elves are the proud, magical city-dwellers; wood elves are the swift, quiet folk of the forests. Here, high elf family names come from Quenya, the High-elven tongue, and wood elf family names from Sindarin. See the Wood Elf Name Generator.
+Can I use these names in D&D?
Yes, they're free to use and you don't need to credit the site.
