Good pet sitting names feel warm and trustworthy, with a touch of fun: Furry Godmother, Willowbrook Pet Minders or Auntie Sarah's Pet Care. Owners hand a pet sitter their keys and their best friend, so the name should feel warm and trustworthy. Sitting, minding or boarding?

The words in the name tell owners what you offer before they ring.

  • Pet sitting usually means you look after pets in their own home, or pop in to feed them.
  • Pet minding is the same idea, and a little more British.
  • Pet boarding means the pets stay with you. In England that usually needs an animal activity licence from your council.
  • Pet care covers all of these, plus walks, so it leaves room to grow.

Cats, rabbits and birds are often looked after at home, so a name that isn't only about dogs (Feathers & Whiskers, The Cheeky Critters Club) brings in more clients.

Check it's free before you print the sign

Every name here has a Check button, which opens the Business Name Checker: it looks up domains, social handles, the company register and trade mark searches in one place. Before you order signs or cards:

  • Company register. In the UK, search Companies House; a limited company's name can't be the same as one already registered. In the US, search your state's business register.
  • Trade marks. Search the UK Intellectual Property Office or the USPTO for marks in your line of work. Registering a company doesn't give you trade mark rights.
  • Domain and handles. Try the .co.uk or .com and the Instagram and Facebook names at the same time, and change the name slightly rather than settle for a messy handle.
  • Local search. Look up the name with your town. Two similar businesses a few miles apart confuse customers and reviews.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, read Business Naming Best Practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good pet sitting business names?
Warm, trustworthy names with a little fun, like Furry Godmother, Whiskers & Wags or Willowbrook Pet Minders. Your own name, as in Auntie Sarah's Pet Care, works well for a local sitter.
+Should I call it pet sitting, pet care or pet boarding?
Pet sitting means you look after pets in their own home, or pop in to feed them. Pet boarding means the pets stay with you, which usually needs a licence (in England, an animal activity licence from your council). Pet care covers both, plus walks, so it leaves room to grow.
+What are funny pet sitting names?
Puns that owners will repeat, like Furry Godmother, Purr-sonal Assistants or Hairy Houseguests. Choose the Funny style for more.
+How do I check a pet sitting name is free?
Press Check on any name to see whether it's free as a company name, a domain and a social media handle. Before you commit, search the official registers too: in the UK, Companies House; in the US, your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search.
