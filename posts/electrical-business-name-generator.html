What makes an electrical name work

Electricians are "sparkies" in British slang, and the trade has plenty of words that double as puns. The units are named after scientists: the watt after James Watt, the ohm after Georg Ohm and the joule after James Prescott Joule.

  • Puns on units and wiring make a name memorable: Watt the Fuse, Sparks & Recreation. Pair them with a slogan that says you're qualified.
  • Say the specialism. EV chargers, solar, data cabling and lighting are all searched for, so a name like Hampton Bright Solar & Electrical tells people what you do.
  • Professional names with Contractors, Installations or Group suit firms bidding for commercial work.
  • Qualifications sell. In England and Wales, notifiable domestic electrical work falls under Part P of the Building Regulations. Electricians registered with a competent person scheme, such as NICEIC or NAPIT, can self-certify it, and customers look for those logos.

Check it's free before you print the sign

A electrical name ends up on a van, a website, a Google Business Profile and a stack of invoices, so it's worth a few minutes' checking before any of that is printed:

  • Press Check on any name above. The Business Name Checker looks up the domain, links to the company register (Companies House in the UK) and opens the trade mark search and social handles.
  • Search your area. Look up the name on a map and in local search results. Two firms with the same name a few miles apart confuse customers and reviews.
  • Don't use a scheme's name or logo, such as NICEIC, in your business name. Show it beside the name once you're registered.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. For the full process, from shortlisting to registering, read our guide to Business Naming Best Practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What are good electrician business names?
Short names that sound safe and switched on: Switched On, Live & Neutral, Barlow Electrical Contractors or Three Phase & Co. Add a specialism like EV chargers or solar if that's your main work.
+How do I check an electrical business name is free?
Press Check next to any name to see if it's free as a company, a domain and a social media handle. Then make sure: in the UK, search Companies House and the UK IPO trade mark register; in the US, search your state's business register and the USPTO trade mark search. A quick map search for the name in your area helps too.
+What are some funny electrician business names?
The best play on electrical words that sound like everyday ones: Ohm Sweet Ohm, Socket Science, Crown Joules or Sparks & Recreation. Pair a joke name with a serious slogan so customers still know you're qualified. Choose Funny in the Style menu for more.
+Should I put NICEIC in my business name?
No. Show the scheme's logo beside your name once you're registered, but don't make it part of the name. The scheme's name belongs to the scheme.
