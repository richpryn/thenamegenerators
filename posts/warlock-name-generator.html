The warlock in D&D

A D&D warlock gets their magic from a pact with a powerful patron.

  • Patrons: the Archfey, the Fiend and the Great Old One in the Player's Handbook, and the Celestial, Hexblade, Fathomless, Genie and Undead in later books.
  • The word: warlock comes from the Old English wǣrloga, an oath-breaker, which later came to mean a sorcerer in league with the Devil.

Naming a warlock

The best warlock names hint at who's on the other end of the pact.

  • The name (Belladonna, Morwenna, Ambrose) has a dark edge.
  • The epithet follows the patron: an Archfey warlock "of the Summer Court", a Genie warlock "Servant of the Lamp", an Undead warlock "the Lich-Sworn".
  • Name the patron too: the Demon, Fairy and Genie name generators all make good ones.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What's a good warlock name?
A name with a dark edge, like Belladonna or Ambrose, and an epithet that hints at the patron, like "of the Summer Court".
+What does warlock mean?
It comes from the Old English wǣrloga, an oath-breaker.
+What are the warlock patrons?
The Archfey, Fiend and Great Old One in the Player's Handbook, plus the Celestial, Hexblade, Fathomless, Genie and Undead in later books.
