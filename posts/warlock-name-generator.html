How the Warlock Name Generator Works

It works from 171 names and name parts, which combine into over 7,800 possible results.

The choices:

  • Gender:
    • Any, e.g. Evander the Grave-Sworn
    • Male, e.g. Vane of the Burning Pact
    • Female, e.g. Florence the Soul-Debtor
  • Patron: Any patron, The Archfey, The Fiend, The Great Old One, The Celestial, The Hexblade, The Fathomless, The Genie, The Undead.
  • Name format: Name and epithet, Name only.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

A warlock made a deal with something powerful, and the best warlock names hint at who's on the other end of the pact. This warlock name generator gives D&D 5e warlocks a first name with a gothic edge and an epithet for their Otherworldly Patron, from the Archfey to the Fiend and the Great Old One.
