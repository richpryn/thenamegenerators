How the Fursona Name Generator Works

It works from 436 names and name parts, which combine into over 19,000 possible results.

The choices:

  • Species: Any species, Wolf, Fox, Cat, Dog, Dragon, Rabbit, Deer, Bird, Raccoon, Shark.
  • Name format: Name only, With a species, With a surname.

Each press gives you 8 names. "Show 8 more" adds 8 you haven't seen yet, until the names for the choices you've made run out.

A fursona is the animal character you make to represent yourself in the furry fandom, and its name is usually the one other furries know you by. This fursona name generator suggests names that suit your species, with a species description or a matching surname if you'd like one.
