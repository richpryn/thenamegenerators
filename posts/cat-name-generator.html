How the Cat Name Generator Works

It works from 3,539 names and name parts, which combine into over 11,000 possible results.

Frequently Asked Questions

+Do cats know their names?
Yes. A 2019 study in Scientific Reports found that pet cats react differently to their own name than to similar-sounding words, even when a stranger says it. Whether they come when called is another matter.
+What should I name a ginger cat?
Food and autumn colours suit ginger cats well: Marmalade, Satsuma, Toffee, Rusty or Copper. Pick Ginger as the style for a full list, with boy and girl options.
