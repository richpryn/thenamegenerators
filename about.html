About The Name Generators

Why this site exists

I make a lot of music and have more ideas than I can keep track of—band names, album titles, project names, character names for stories. I kept needing something to help me come up with names quickly, and I couldn’t find one place that did exactly what I wanted.

So I built this site to scratch my own itch. The Name Generators is the tool I wanted: free, simple, and focused on generating names for creative projects—whether that’s D&D characters, fantasy worlds, businesses, or music. I kept adding more generators as I needed them, and eventually decided to put it all online so others could use it too.

Everything here is free to use. I hope it helps you name your next project, character, or idea.
