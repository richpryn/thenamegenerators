How we check this library

Each fact is checked against at least two sources, such as historical dictionaries, etymological dictionaries and academic works, and those sources are listed on the page. Where scholars disagree, we say so. Names our generators invent are always labelled as invented, so you never mistake a name that sounds Norse for one that is.
