Embed a Name Generator

Put any of our 406 name generators on your own site: a blog, a campaign wiki, a class page or a writing group. It's free, with no ads and no sign-up.

The code

How it looks

Elf Name Generator by The Name Generators

How it works

  1. Pick a generator above, or press Put this generator on your website under any generator.
  2. Copy the code and paste it where your site accepts HTML.
  3. Your readers get the full generator, with every option, right on your page.

The generator runs inside a frame, so it always has our latest names and fixes without you changing anything. Names are made in your reader's browser; nothing they press is sent to us or to you.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Any site can embed any generator, including business sites, and the names it makes are free to use.

Does it show ads or track my visitors?

No. The embedded generator has no ads, sets no cookies and loads no analytics. Names a visitor saves are kept in their own browser.

Why is there a credit line under it?

The line under the generator tells your readers where it comes from. Please leave it in: it is the only thing we ask in return.

Can I change the size?

Yes. It fills the width you give it, up to 720 pixels. The last line of the code lets it grow to fit the names; if your site removes scripts, it stays at 560 pixels tall, and you can change height="560" to suit.

Where do I paste the code?

Anywhere your site accepts HTML: a Custom HTML block in WordPress, an Embed or Code block in Squarespace and Wix, or straight into a page on your own site.
