Music Name Generators

Names for music projects, albums and gigs, from a site that began with music.

This site started because its maker, a musician, kept needing names: for projects, albums, songs and nights out. The music generators are where that began.

The Dungeon Synth Name Generator names atmospheric fantasy projects and albums, and the Event Name Generator has a whole section for gigs, club nights and open mics.
