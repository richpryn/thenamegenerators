Warhammer Name Generators

Names for Warhammer 40k and Warhammer Fantasy characters: every 40k faction from Space Marines to Necrons, plus Orks and Ogryns.

Warhammer names tell you who someone serves. A Space Wolf sounds like a saga hero, an Ultramarine like a Roman legionary, a T'au carries caste, rank and sept in their name, and an Ork is named for being loud and hitting things. These generators follow the setting's styles, with examples from the books and games.
