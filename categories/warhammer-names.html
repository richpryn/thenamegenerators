Warhammer Name Generators

Names for Warhammer 40k and Warhammer Fantasy characters: Space Marines, Imperial Guard, Aeldari, Orks and Ogryns.

Warhammer names tell you who someone serves. A Space Wolf sounds like a saga hero, an Ultramarine like a Roman legionary, an Imperial Guard officer comes with a regiment, and an Ork is named for being loud and hitting things. These generators follow the setting's styles, with examples from the books and games.
