🪓 World of Warcraft Name Generators: Orc, Night Elf, Blood Elf and Troll

Names for the races of Azeroth, for World of Warcraft characters and roleplay.

4 generators available

Each race in World of Warcraft has its own sound. Orcs have harsh, short names like Thrall and Grom; night elves pair a flowing first name with a surname like Whisperwind; blood elves are elegant and golden; trolls snap out names like Vol'jin. These generators follow each race's patterns, with examples from the games.

WoW names must be one word with no apostrophes and unique on your realm, so each page explains how to turn a full name into one that fits, and keep the rest for roleplay.
