Blog & Brand Name Generators

Names for blogs and brands, and how to check they're free to use.

A blog or brand name has to work as a web address, a social handle and a logo, so short and distinctive beats clever. The Blog Name Generator works by topic, from fitness to finance; the Brand Name Generator gives you single brandable words in 18 styles, from tech to heritage.

Whichever you use, check the domain and handles before you fall for a name. The Business Name Generator explains how to check company registers and trade marks too.
