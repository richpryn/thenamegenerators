Pop Culture Name Generators

Names for Hogwarts Legacy, Harry Potter, Skyrim, Warcraft, Warhammer, Lord of the Rings and more.

Every big fantasy world has its own naming rules. Skyrim's orcs take gro- or gra- and a parent's name; the 1890s students of Hogwarts Legacy have Victorian first names; Tolkien's orcs sound nothing like Warcraft's. These generators follow those rules, and each page explains them with characters from the source.

Most of the names are new ones in each world's style, so your character fits in without borrowing someone else's.
