Business names by trade

Trades & Home Services

Names for cleaners, builders, landscapers, plumbers and other trades, each with a check to see if it's free.

A trade name ends up on a van, a quote and a review site, so it has to be easy to remember and sound like someone you'd let into your home. These generators work trade by trade, from cleaning and construction to landscaping and lawn care, with catchy names for the van, classic family names for the quote, and names built from your town or your own name, the way most local firms are named.

Every name has a Check button that opens the Business Name Checker, which looks up the company register, trade marks, domains and social handles in one go.

How to approach trade business names

Most local trades are named in one of three ways: a family name (Carter & Sons), the town (Hampton Garden Care) or a catchy line that sticks (Mow Excuses). Each says something different: a family name promises a reputation, a town promises you're nearby, and a pun promises you're friendly. Pick the one that matches how you want customers to see you, keep it short enough for the side of a van, and say what you do if you specialise, because customers search for the job, not the name.

Quick tips

  • Keep it short enough for a van.
  • Add your town to help local search.
  • Say what you do if you specialise.
  • Match it to a Google Business Profile.
  • Check it's free before you print.

Examples

Dust Till Dawn

CLEANING

A pun people repeat to their friends.

It twists the film title From Dusk Till Dawn into the job itself, so it's memorable and says what you do in three words.

Ashlar

CONSTRUCTION

A one-word trade name that sounds skilled.

Ashlar is finely cut, squared building stone. Using a word from the trade shows you know the craft without boasting.

Rowan & Wildflower Landscapes

LANDSCAPING

Gentle and natural, for planting and garden work.

Two native plants make a pairing that's easy to picture and draw as a logo, and 'Landscapes' tells customers what you do.

Green Stripe Lawn Care

LAWN CARE

The result every customer wants, in the name.

Neat stripes are the sign of a well-mown lawn, so the name promises the finish rather than the work.
