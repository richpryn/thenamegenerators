Witches, covens and spells

Witch Name Generators

Witch names for fantasy, games and Halloween: swamp witches, Harry Potter and Hogwarts Legacy witches, Path of Exile witches and more.

Witches come in many kinds, and so do their names. A swamp witch in a fairy tale sounds nothing like a student at Hogwarts, and a Path of Exile witch is named for her dark arts. These generators each follow one tradition, so you can find the right kind of witch.

How to approach witch names

Witch names in British folklore come from three places: plants, places and familiars. Village cunning folk were known by where they lived, hedge witches by the herbs they gathered, and the witch-finder Matthew Hopkins recorded familiars in the 1640s with names like Pyewacket. Fictional witches follow the same habits. Take a surname from a wild plant, add a title like Granny or Old Mother, and give the witch a familiar with a name of its own. For covens and spells, a moon, a place or a maker's name does the work.

Quick tips

  • Take a surname from a wild plant.
  • Give the witch a familiar with a name.
  • Name a coven after a moon or a place.
  • Put a maker's name on a spell.
  • Keep cosy witches cosy.
