🏺 Historical Name Generators: Real Names From Rome to the Old West

Real names from history for stories and games: Roman, Ancient Greek, Anglo-Saxon, medieval English, Victorian and Old West.

6 generators available

Every period named people its own way. Romans had three names, Anglo-Saxons built names from two meaningful parts, medieval English surnames came from jobs and places, and the Old West ran on nicknames. These generators use real names from each period and explain how naming worked at the time, so your characters sound like they belong there.

Where to start

  • 🏛️ Roman Name Generator: praenomen, nomen and cognomen, with what the cognomen meant.
  • 🛡️ Anglo-Saxon Name Generator: names built from two parts, with their meanings.
  • 🏰 Medieval Name Generator: first names and surnames from jobs, places and nicknames.
  • 🤠 Old West Name Generator: frontier names with a nickname.

All 6 Historical generators

🏛️ Roman Name Generator

Real Roman names built the Roman way: praenomen, nomen and cognomen for men (Marcus Tullius Cicero), and the family name for women, with what each cognomen meant.

🏺 Ancient Greek Name Generator

Real Ancient Greek names with their meanings (Nikias, victory; Philippos, lover of horses), as a single name, with a father's name, or with a city.

🛡️ Anglo-Saxon Name Generator

Old English names built the Anglo-Saxon way, from two meaningful parts: Æthelwulf (noble wolf), Eadgifu (prosperity and gift), with the meaning of each part.

🏰 Medieval Name Generator

Real medieval English names with surnames from jobs (Fletcher, arrow-maker), places (atte Wode), fathers (FitzWalter) and nicknames (Armstrong), with what each surname meant.

🎩 Victorian Name Generator

Real Victorian first names (Ada, Florence, Septimus, Herbert) with English surnames, optionally with a title or a real Victorian job like lamplighter or knocker-upper.

🤠 Old West Name Generator

Old West names for cowboys, outlaws and frontier folk: 19th-century American first names with a surname, and a nickname like Two-Gun or Deadeye.
