Halloween Name Generators

Spooky names for Halloween: vampires, ghosts, zombies, werewolves, witches and demons, for costumes, parties, haunted houses and stories.

A good Halloween name tells you what kind of scary you're in for. A vampire wants an old title and a family name with history behind it. A ghost is usually named for the colour it wears or the place it haunts, like the Grey Ladies of English ghost stories. A zombie can be grim or downright silly, and a werewolf name often means "wolf" in an old language.

The Witch, Black Cat and Haunted House name generators cover costumes, pets and attractions. Use any of these for a costume character, a haunted house at the school fair, a one-shot for your gaming group or a spooky short story. For the party itself, the Event Name Generator has party names, and the Villain and Demon generators cover anything with horns or a cape.
