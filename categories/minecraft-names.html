Minecraft Name Generators

Names for everything in Minecraft: usernames, villagers, pets, worlds, servers, towns, clans and enchanted tools.

These Minecraft name generators cover everything you can name in the game, and every username they make fits Minecraft's rules. Minecraft gives you a lot to name: yourself, your world, your server, the wolf that follows you everywhere and the pickaxe you've put three enchantments on. Start with a username that fits the rules (3 to 16 letters, numbers and underscores), or build one from your own name.

For the things you name with a name tag, there are villager names by profession and biome and pet names for everything from wolves to axolotls. For the places you build, try the world, server and SMP and town, village and kingdom name generators. And for everything you fight with, there are clan and faction names, names for tools and weapons and funny sword names.

How to approach Minecraft names

Minecraft names have practical limits. A Java Edition username is 3 to 16 characters, using letters, numbers and underscores, so short wins. Everything else you name with a name tag or an anvil: a pet, a villager, a sword. The best Minecraft names come from the game itself, from the mobs, the blocks and the ways you've died, because every player recognises them. A joke about falling in lava lands better on a server than a name that could belong to any game.

Quick tips

  • Keep usernames to 16 characters.
  • Borrow from mobs, blocks and biomes.
  • Name pets with a name tag.
  • Rename swords and tools in an anvil.
  • Check a username is free first.
