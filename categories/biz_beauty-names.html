Business names by trade

Beauty Businesses

Names for hair and beauty salons, nail techs, lash studios, spas and fitness businesses, each with a check to see if it's free.

Clients choose a salon on Instagram before they ever walk in, so a beauty business name needs to look good as a handle and feel right for your prices. Each generator here is made for one trade: the Hair Salon, Beauty Salon, Nail Salon and Lash Business name generators. Each has styles from catchy puns and cute names to aesthetic and French luxury, and can use your town or your own name.

Every name has a Check button: it opens the Business Name Checker, which looks up the company register, domains, trade marks and social handles in one go.

How to approach salon names

Decide first how you want clients to feel: welcomed by a friendly local salon, calmed by a quiet treatment room, or treated by a premium studio. The name should match your prices. Puns on shear, mane, gel or lash say friendly; one clean word from the craft says modern; French words like atelier, maison and peau say luxury. Because clients find salons on Instagram and Google Maps, check the handle and the area before you commit.

Quick tips

  • Match the name to your prices.
  • Check the Instagram handle first.
  • Name a speciality if you have one.
  • Your own name suits one chair.
  • Search nearby for similar salons.

Examples

Shear Luck

HAIR SALON

The classic hairdresser's pun, done well.

It plays on "sheer luck" with the shears every stylist uses, and it's short enough for a sign and a handle.

Peau Douce

BEAUTY SALON

A calm, premium name for skin treatments.

It's French for "soft skin", which says exactly what clients come in for, with a luxury feel.

Maison Vernis

NAIL SALON

Nail polish, said elegantly.

Vernis is French for varnish, the French word for nail polish, and maison (house) makes it sound like an established studio.

A lash name with a wink in it, literally.

Clin d'œil is French for a wink, the perfect image for a lash studio.
