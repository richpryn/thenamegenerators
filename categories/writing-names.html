Writing

Story prompts, characters, villains, sidekicks, legends and titles: generators for writers stuck on the next name or idea.

Every story needs names, and most writers have stalled on one. These generators cover the people in your story, the legends around it and the titles on it. Stuck before the names? Start with the Story Prompt Generator.
