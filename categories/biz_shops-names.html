Business names by trade

Shops & Makers

Names for clothing brands, boutiques, candle makers, jewellers and Etsy shops, each with a check to see if it's free.

For a shop or a small brand, the name is the brand: it goes on the label, the packaging and the shop page. These generators work product by product, from a clothing brand or a boutique to a jewellery business or a candle business, in styles from aesthetic and cute to modern and luxury. Add your own name for a maker's mark nobody else can use.

Every name has a Check button that opens the Business Name Checker, which looks up the company register, trade marks, domains and social handles in one go.

How to approach shop and brand names

A product name has to work small: on a woven label, a jar or a 32-pixel profile picture. Short names and clear images do best. Decide first what customers should feel when they see it (playful, cosy, luxurious, nostalgic) and pick a style to match: a pun for a market stall, a scent pairing for a premium candle, French words for fine jewellery. Then check the handle as well as the domain, because most small brands are found on Instagram, Etsy and TikTok before anywhere else.

Quick tips

  • Write it on a real label first.
  • Match the style to your price.
  • Check the handle, not just the domain.
  • Stay clear of big brands' names.
  • Your own name makes a maker's mark.

Examples

Thread Carefully

CLOTHING

A pun that doubles as a brand value.

It plays on 'tread carefully' with thread, so it's both funny and a quiet promise of careful making.

Chez Mathilde

BOUTIQUE

A French touch that feels personal.

Chez means 'at the home of', so the name sounds like being welcomed into someone's shop rather than a chain.

Snowdrop & Silver

JEWELLERY

Delicate, with the metal in the name.

A spring flower and the material you work in: customers know what you make and what it feels like before they look.

Wax & Wane Candle Co.

CANDLES

A pun that fits the product exactly.

'Wax and wane' describes the moon growing and shrinking, and candles do the same as they burn.
