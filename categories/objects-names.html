Object Name Generators

Swords, axes, shields, wands, war hammers and poisons for games and stories.

Named weapons are an old tradition: Excalibur, Durendal, Kusanagi. A name turns a sword into a story, which is why a magic item that's only a "+1 longsword" is so easy to forget.

These generators cover everything from swords and axes to wands and war hammers, with separate generators for elven, Japanese, Game of Thrones and Minecraft swords. When you name an item, decide who named it and why: the best item names come with a history.
