Names for Tamriel

Elder Scrolls Name Generators

Names for the races of Tamriel, for Skyrim, Elder Scrolls Online and your own stories.

Every race in the Elder Scrolls names itself differently. Orcs add gro- or gra- and a parent's name; Khajiit wear prefixes like the J' in J'zargo; Nords have hyphenated family names like Battle-Born; Argonians may go by a description, like Scouts-Many-Marshes. These generators follow each race's customs, with examples from Skyrim, so your character sounds like they belong in Tamriel.

Each page explains how that race's names work. They suit Skyrim, Elder Scrolls Online, where names must be unique on your server, and tabletop games set in Tamriel.

How to approach Elder Scrolls names

Each people in The Elder Scrolls names itself differently, so start with the race. Nords take Old Norse-style names and an epithet earned in battle. Khajiit names often carry an apostrophe prefix that says something about the bearer's standing. Argonians may be known by what they do, translated into a phrase like Wades-Through-Fog. Dark elves and high elves have long, formal names with their own sounds. Match the pattern, and an original name will fit in anywhere in Tamriel without borrowing anyone else's.

Quick tips

  • Start with the character's race.
  • Give Nords an earned epithet.
  • Use one Khajiit prefix, not two.
  • Let Argonians be named by deeds.
  • Avoid names from the games.

Examples

Asgeir Ash-Fist

NORD

A Nord who sounds like he'd win an argument by punching the table.

Asgeir is a real Old Norse name, from 'god' and 'spear'. The hyphenated epithet is the Nord habit of being named for a deed or a trait.

Dro'bajhar

KHAJIIT

A name with weight and age to it.

The apostrophe prefix is part of the Khajiit pattern, and the soft 'jh' and rolling vowels keep it from sounding like any other race.

Wades-Through-Fog

ARGONIAN

An Argonian name you understand the first time you hear it.

A short phrase describing what the character does, joined with hyphens. It suits a scout, a fisher or anyone at home in the marshes.

Vesthas, Doom of Kings

WEAPON

A Daedric blade with a reputation it hasn't finished earning.

A hard two-syllable name with a grand title after the comma, the way legendary weapons are named. It isn't a real artefact from the games, so it won't clash with anything.
