Animals & Pets

Names for pets, toys and made-up animals, from brand-new dinosaur species to a cat for Halloween.

Names for animals real and imagined. The Dinosaur Name Generator makes up new species from real Greek and Latin, with what each name means, or turns your own name into a dinosaur. The Pet Dinosaur Name Generator names the dinosaur in your life: a teddy, a toy, a pet or a character in a story.

For parties, pets and farm visits, the Farm Animal Name Generator names lambs, calves, piglets, hens, ponies, goats and ducks, and the Easter Bunny Name Generator names bunnies, chicks and lambs for the egg hunt.
