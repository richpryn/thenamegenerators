Business names by trade

Food & Drink Businesses

Names for bakeries, cake makers, food trucks, caterers, bars and cafés, each with a check to see if it's free.

A food business name has to look good on a sign, a box and an Instagram grid, and say what you sell before anyone has tasted it. Each generator here is made for one trade: the Bakery, Cake Business, Cookie Business and Dessert Business name generators, plus the Café Name Generator. Each has styles from punny and cute to classic and French, and can build a name from your town or your own name.

Every name has a Check button: it opens the Business Name Checker, which looks up the company register, domains, trade marks and social handles in one go. In the UK, remember to register a food business with your council at least 28 days before you start trading.

How to approach food business names

Start with what you sell and who buys it. A birthday-cake maker, a sourdough bakery and a dessert parlour all want different names, even though they all bake. Food names work best when they make people hungry and say what's on offer: a pun built on a baking word (proof, knead, crumb), a classic name from your street or family, or a French word if your pastries live up to it. Then check it reads on a box, fits an Instagram handle and isn't already used nearby.

Quick tips

  • Put what you sell in the name.
  • Puns work best for fun, casual food.
  • Use French only if you can say it.
  • Add your town to be found locally.
  • Check the handle before the sign.

Examples

Well Bread

BAKERY

A pun everyone gets on first reading.

It plays on "well bred" and tells you it's a bakery in two words, short enough for a sign, a bag and a handle.

Belgravia Cake Atelier

CAKE BUSINESS

A wedding-cake name that justifies wedding-cake prices.

Belgravia is one of London's smartest districts, and atelier is French for a workshop or studio, so the name says handmade and high-end.

Cookies Are My Cardio

COOKIE BUSINESS

A joke that sells itself on Instagram.

It's funny, it's shareable, and it tells you exactly what the business sells.

Maison Chantilly

DESSERT BAR

Soft, French and a little bit fancy.

Maison means house, and Chantilly cream is sweetened whipped cream named after the town of Chantilly in France.
