Business names

Food & Drink Businesses

Names for bakeries, cake makers, food trucks, caterers, bars and cafés, each with a check to see if it's free.

A food business name has to look good on a sign, a box and an Instagram grid, and say what you sell before anyone has tasted it. These generators work trade by trade, so a bakery gets bakery names and a food truck gets food truck names, in styles from cute and punny to classic and premium. Add your own name or town for names nobody else will have.

Every name has a Check button: it opens the Business Name Checker, which looks up the company register, domains, trade marks and social handles in one go.

Choose your kind of business

Pick the generator for your trade, then press Check on any name you like.
