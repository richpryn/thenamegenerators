Wuxia & Xianxia Name Generators

Names for wuxia and xianxia stories: cultivation sects, martial techniques, character names with meanings and cultivator titles.

These wuxia and xianxia name generators are for martial arts stories, cultivation web novels and the games they inspired. Start with a hero from the Wuxia Name Generator, which pairs real Chinese surnames with given names made from real words and their meanings. Then give them a sect, a signature technique and, once they reach the top, a cultivator title.

For the creatures of Chinese myth, try the Chinese Dragon Name Generator, and for more legends from around the world, the mythology name generators.
