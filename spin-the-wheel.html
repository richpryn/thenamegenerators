How to use the wheel

Put one name on each line, press Spin, and the wheel picks one at random.

  1. Add names. Type them, paste a list from a spreadsheet, or add 8 from one of our name generators.
  2. Spin. Press Spin, tap the wheel, or press Space or Enter when the wheel is selected.
  3. Keep going. "Remove & spin again" takes the winner off so they can't come up again: handy for turn order, or a raffle with several prizes.
  4. Share it. Copy the link and anyone who opens it gets the same list.

Is it fair?

Every name gets a slice the same size, and duplicates are removed so nobody gets two. The winner is picked by your browser's built-in secure random number generator the moment you press Spin; the spin is just there to show you where it landed, and the wheel always stops on the name that was picked.

Questions

+Is the wheel really random?

Yes. The winner is picked by your browser's built-in secure random number generator the moment you press Spin, and every name gets a slice the same size. The spin just shows you where it landed.

+Do you keep my list?

No. The list is saved in your own browser, so it's still there next time, and nowhere else. A share link carries the names inside the link itself, after the # sign, which browsers never send to the server.

+How many names can I add?

Up to 500. Names are written on the wheel up to 120; beyond that the slices are too thin to read, so the wheel shows colours only and the winner appears in the card.

+What happens if I add the same name twice?

Duplicates are removed automatically, so everyone gets one slice. If you want someone to have a better chance, add a variation like "Sam (2)".

+Can I take a name off after it wins?

Yes. Press "Remove & spin again" in the winner card, or "Remove winner" under the wheel. Handy for turn order or a raffle with several prizes.
