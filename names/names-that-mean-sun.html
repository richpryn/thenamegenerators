Names That Mean 'Sun'

Sun names come from almost every language: Sol and Soleil from Latin roots, Sunniva and Solveig from Old English and Old Norse, Heulwen from Welsh, Ravi and Surya from Sanskrit, Shams from Arabic, Inti from Quechua. One of the oldest is Samson, from the Hebrew word for sun. Every meaning here has been checked against a cited source, mostly Behind the Name and Wiktionary, and ranks for England and Wales come from the ONS.

About this list

Some names are sold as sun names on weak evidence. Cyrus, Elio and Eliana are the common ones. We've kept them in, marked uncertain, so you can see what the sources actually say.

Sun names from the north

Old English and Old Norse both used sól or sunne for the sun, and a few names built on them are still around. Sunniva ('sun gift') is the Norwegian form of an Old English name. Solveig, 'sun strength', is well known in Scandinavia, and Solfrid is a 19th-century Norwegian coinage. They're unusual in England but easy to say and spell.

Pairing and nicknames

Short sun names (Sol, Ravi, Shams, Inti) suit longer surnames, and three-syllable ones like Sunniva, Savitri and Heliodoro suit short ones. Sunniva and Solveig shorten naturally to Sunny and Sol, which gives a child a choice between a formal name and an everyday one.

Day names

Sunday is a 'day name', given to a child born on that day. The habit is strongest in parts of West Africa, where Sunday and Monday are used for boys and girls. If you like the idea, it carries the sun meaning through Old English sunnandæg, 'sun's day'.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What girl names mean 'sun'?
Soleil (French), Sunniva ('sun gift'), Solveig ('sun strength'), Heulwen (Welsh, 'sunshine'), Saulė (Lithuanian), Arev (Armenian) and Güneş (Turkish). Sol, Spanish for sun, is mainly a girl's name in Spanish.
+What boy names mean 'sun'?
Samson comes from the Hebrew word for sun. Ravi, Surya and Suraj mean 'sun' in Sanskrit and Hindi, Helios in Greek, and Tonatiuh in Nahuatl. Shams (Arabic) and Inti (Quechua) are also used.
+What name means 'sunshine'?
Heulwen in Welsh and Shandiin in Navajo both mean 'sunshine'. Sunshine itself is also used as an English word name.
