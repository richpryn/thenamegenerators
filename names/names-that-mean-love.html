Names That Mean 'Love'

Love names fall into two groups: names that mean 'love' itself, like Carys (Welsh), Aroha (Māori) or Sevgi (Turkish), and names that mean 'beloved' or 'dear', which is a much older idea. Amy, Mabel, David, Kevin and Priya are all in the second group, and several of them are among the most-used names in England and Wales. Every meaning here has been checked against a cited source, mostly Behind the Name and Wiktionary, and the ranks come from the ONS.

About this list

Love is also where baby name myths cluster. Romeo, Valentine and Freya are linked with love by story and tradition, not by meaning. We've marked them uncertain and explained.

Beloved names

Names meaning 'beloved' or 'dear' are some of the oldest in use. David comes from Hebrew dod, Amy from Old French amée, Kevin from Irish cóem, Priya from Sanskrit and Habib from Arabic. If you want a love meaning in a name people already know, start here.

The Sikh -preet names

Many Sikh names end in -preet, from Sanskrit prīti, 'love'. They're used for both boys and girls: Harpreet is 'love of Hari (God)', Gurpreet 'love of the guru', Manpreet 'love of the mind'. The first part changes the sense, so it's worth knowing what each one means before you choose.

Nicknames and short forms

Amabel gives Mabel or Bel, Philomena gives Mina, Jedidiah gives Jed, and Amadeus gives Amadeo or Deo. David has Dave, Davy and Dai in Welsh. If you're choosing a long love name, try the short form too, because that's what most people will use.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What name means loved by God?
Jedidiah means 'beloved of Yahweh' in Hebrew, and Amadeus 'love of God' in Latin. Olufemi, from Yoruba, means 'God loves me'.
+What name means lover of horses?
Philip, from Greek Philippos, made of philos ('friend, lover') and hippos ('horse'). Philippa is the feminine form.
+What girl names mean 'love'?
Carys (Welsh), Ahava (Hebrew), Kerensa (Cornish), Lyubov (Russian), Sevgi (Turkish) and Aiko (Japanese) mean 'love'. Amy, Mabel, Davina, Caoimhe and Priya mean 'beloved' or 'dear'.
