Names That Mean 'Night'

Fewer names mean 'night' than you might expect, so this list also takes in names meaning 'evening' and 'darkness'. The best known is Layla, Arabic for night, and its many spellings: Leila, Laila and Leyla. Alongside them are Nyx and Nox, the Greek and Latin goddesses of night, Sanskrit Nisha and Yamini, and Irish names from ciar, 'black, dark', such as Kieran and Ciara. Every meaning is checked against a cited source, mostly Behind the Name and Wiktionary, and England and Wales ranks come from the ONS.

Night has its share of myths. Lyla, Lilah and Lila are sometimes forms of Leila and sometimes not, Lilith's link to night is an old folk etymology, and Delilah has nothing to do with it. We've marked these uncertain.

Layla and her spellings

Layla is Arabic for night, and it reaches England and Wales in several spellings: Layla (the most common), Leila (the usual Persian form, made familiar in English by Byron), Laila and Leyla (Turkish). They're all the same name. Lyla, Lilah and Lila are harder: each can be a form of Leila, but each also has another source, so we've marked them uncertain.

Dark names from Ireland and Scotland

Irish ciar ('black') and dub ('dark') are behind a whole set of names. From ciar come Ciar, Ciara, Kiera and Keira for girls and Ciarán and Kieran for boys; from dub come Dougal ('dark stranger'), Duff and Dubhán.

Evening names

If 'night' feels too dark, evening names are gentler. Vesper and Hesperos are the Latin and Greek names for the evening star, Ilta is Finnish for evening, and Samar and Samir are Arabic names about talking together in the evening. Odhiambo, from Luo in Kenya, is given to a child born in the evening.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What name means 'night'?
Layla (and Leila, Laila, Leyla) means 'night' in Arabic. Nyx is Greek and Nox Latin for night, Nisha and Yamini mean 'night' in Sanskrit, and Tuta means 'night' in Quechua.
+What names mean 'night' or 'darkness'?
For darkness: Melanie ('black, dark'), Kieran and Ciarán ('little dark one'), Ciara and Keira, Dougal ('dark stranger'), Krishna ('black, dark') and Erebus ('nether darkness'). For night: Layla, Nyx, Nox and Nisha.
+What name means 'night' in Arabic?
Layla, which is also written Leila, Laila and Leyla. Asra means 'travel at night', and Samar 'evening conversation'.
