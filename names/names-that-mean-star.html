This list collects real given names whose meaning is 'star', 'morning star' or a named star, each checked against a cited source, mostly Behind the Name and Wiktionary. About this list

A few famous 'star' names aren't what they seem. Esther may or may not mean 'star'; Astrid doesn't; and Mary's 'star of the sea' is a title, not a meaning. We've marked these uncertain and said why.

Stella, Estelle and their relatives

Latin stella is behind most of the best-known star names in English: Stella itself, Estelle and Estella from French, Estrella from Spanish, and Stela in Romanian and other languages. If you want the meaning to be obvious, these are the safest choices. Seren, the Welsh word for star, does the same job with a shorter, softer sound.

Morning star names

Several languages have a name for Venus as the morning star: Danica in the Slavic languages, Zornitsa in Bulgarian, Lucero in Spanish and Tariq in Arabic. They suit a child born early in the morning, or anyone who wants the star meaning without the obvious Stella.

Two names, one spelling

Tara and Dara are both 'star' in one language and something else in another. The Sanskrit Tara means 'star'; the Irish Tara is a royal hill. The Khmer Dara means 'star'; the Irish Dara comes from Dáire. Both versions are used in England and Wales, so the rank beside them counts everyone with that spelling.

Frequently Asked Questions

+What name means 'star of the sea'?
Maristela comes from Stella Maris, Latin for 'star of the sea', a title of the Virgin Mary. Mary itself is often said to mean 'star of the sea', but that is the title, not the meaning of the name, which is uncertain.
+What boy names mean 'star'?
Najm ('star') and Shihab ('shooting star') are Arabic, and Tariq is the Arabic name of the morning star. Dhruv means the Pole Star in Sanskrit. Ylli (Albanian) and Dzvezdan (Macedonian) also mean 'star'.
+What name means 'star' in Japanese?
Hoshi, written 星, means 'star', and Hoshiko means 'star child'. As with all Japanese names, the meaning depends on the kanji, and the same sounds can be written other ways.
